# Shared writer rules

## Authority and time

Treat canon and interpretation as separate layers. Use this authority order:

1. Direct authorial statements and corrections.
2. Accepted or subsequently relied-upon transcript developments.
3. Specialized canon references inside their domains.
4. Setting Timeline, Setting Bible, Character Dossier, and resolved Questions and Confirmations.
5. Existing wiki material that does not conflict with higher authority.
6. Conservative extrapolation required for coherence.

Use the latest date established by the transcript corpus as the current in-setting horizon. Do not silently turn later plans, forecasts, or proposals into history.

Use `[a]` for facts explicitly established by the setting author and `[e]` for incorporated extrapolation. These marks identify provenance, not intellectual ownership.

## Permitted work

Create missing pages and substantially improve existing ones. Add supported facts, conservative connective tissue, technical explanation, context, consequences, links, structured data, biographies, and institutional detail. Reorganize weak material when the result remains faithful to canon.

Specialists may reframe established events and disagree over:

- cause and relative importance;
- avoidability and contingency;
- strategic or economic success;
- institutional purpose;
- consequences and periodization;
- contemporary understanding and later historiography.

Never use writer opinion to change whether an established event occurred. Put unresolved factual contradictions into Questions and Confirmations.

## Collision discipline

Preserve another writer's attributed interpretation when it remains compatible with the facts. Qualify or answer it under the new writer's own block. Create a functional `Interpretation`, `Debate`, `Reception`, `Assessment`, or `Historiography` section when the disagreement becomes substantial.

The article lead remains consensus-driven unless the disagreement itself is the subject. Controlled seams are desirable; factual incoherence is not.

## General editor and specialists

The General Encyclopedic Editor grows coverage, coherence, structured information, and links. It has no personal writer code for neutral synthesis.

Specialists supply identifiable interpretation through permanent writer codes. A specialist must not become automatically correct. Preserve the profile's blind spots and allow other writers' questions to remain visible.

## Character discipline

- For historical people, retain reliable pre-divergence biography and setting-controlled later history.
- For detailed fictional people, preserve established temperament, relationships, politics, formative experience, and career.
- For lightly mentioned fictional people, create a coherent but proportionate life and update the Character Dossier when characterization matters beyond the public article.

Do not make every invented person exceptional or dramatic.

## Images

Use real-world images only when their visible circumstances remain plausible in-setting. Flag genuinely alternate portraits, flags, maps, vehicles, architecture, or scenes for custom art instead of presenting a contradictory real image as authentic.
