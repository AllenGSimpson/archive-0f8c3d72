The world after victory · 1947–1985

The war ended.
The struggle did not.

The United States stayed out. Britain survived defeat. Germany conquered the east, Japan secured a maritime empire, and the atom never became a weapon. Five unequal systems now compete to define modernity.

Women, work, and the Latin household

Italy, Spain, and Occitania organize female economic activity around marriage, household production, care, and selected professions. Their postwar systems move from blunt employment exclusions toward differentiated work over the life cycle. Serious education and legal capacity coexist with a social expectation that mothers of young children principally manage the household.
