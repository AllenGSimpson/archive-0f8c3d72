The General Canon Editor is an audit role rather than a coverage or viewpoint specialist. `[g]` reviews existing canon-era material; the unannotated General Encyclopedic Editor creates and expands coverage. `[v]` fills connective gaps within the established horizon and always marks its extrapolations visibly.

The current in-setting canon date is **5 January 2012**. Events after that date are post-canon unless a later direct authorial statement advances the horizon. Writers must not convert later plans, projections, or real-world biographical dates into completed setting history.

## Candidate roles

Add candidates slowly, with a permanent unused code and a complete profile, after the active voices have demonstrated useful disagreement. Strong candidates include:

- Russian émigré historian;
- African or Arab social historian working outside the Italian imperial archive;
- comparative legal historian;
- intelligence historian;
- business and technology historian;
- demographer and historical statistician;
- cultural historian;
- diplomatic realist or international-relations scholar;
- labor historian.

## Expected interpretive pairings

Productive disagreements should include Hargreaves and Bellandi on constitutional continuity and colonial consent; Ishikawa and regional or nationalist historians on integration after preservationism; Valcárcel and German or Italian political writers on national capture of the Church; Bellandi and Eberhardt on the limits of colonial comparison; Eberhardt and future Russian or Ukrainian historians on settlement, sovereignty, and resistance; and Mercer and Reinhardt on price, capacity, and directed development.
