The article listed the settlement's monetary instruments and later investment fields but did not connect them through an investment sequence. I have treated blocked balances as an exchange of creditor liquidity for British reconstruction time, defensible only where that time produced measurable network and productive capacity. The same framework preserves the objection from creditor choice: unsuitable tied equipment, unusable plant, or indefinite blocking remains a forced transfer even when recorded as development. Later work could apply the proposed tests—completion, utilization, maintenance, linkage, import substitution, export earnings, and recipient choice—to individual country schedules once those schedules are established.
