The article used an earlier 47.8-tonne, 50 km/h baseline and called Panzer X Germany's first main battle tank. The current register assigns that conceptual distinction to Panzer IX and fixes Panzer X as the 49.5-tonne, 56 km/h integrated technological generation with an 18-round selectable automatic loader and three-station active infrared. Variant chronology and open technical fields were otherwise preserved.
