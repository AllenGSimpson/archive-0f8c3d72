The authorial transcript establishes the nunciature's practical recognition of Occitania, Rome's refusal to make political and canonical borders identical, free episcopal appointment and communication, protected schools and religious orders, Lourdes as a universalizing sanctuary, and the constitutional distinction between ecclesiastical freedom and clerical government. I have carried those principles into the country's institutional article while retaining the record's cautions about patronage and exclusion. The exact concordat or collection of fundamental instruments, diocesan boundaries, bishops, property distribution, subsidies, school law, civil effects of marriage, and scale of northern migration remain open.
