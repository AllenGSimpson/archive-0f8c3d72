Mercer's distinction between physical achievement and allocative success is sound. My reply gives greater weight to network returns from common standards, transport, maintenance, and trained technical staffs, then separates the ledgers and institutions that would have to recognize failure. The listed remedies are an institutional test rather than a fixed statute or confirmed sequence of cases. Exact audit offices, reporting forms, write-down rules, associated-state standing, closures, and restructurings remain open; the direct canon boundary is the 1970s survival of protected firms through refinancing.
