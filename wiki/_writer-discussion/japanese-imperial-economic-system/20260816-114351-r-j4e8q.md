The article and Setting Bible establish real Japanese industrial achievements alongside directed credit, protected corporate capacity, controlled clearing, a deliberately unequal production ladder, and later soft-budget refinancing. I have treated physical success at a chosen objective as evidence of capacity while withholding the broader inference that the observed allocation was generally efficient.

The countercase matters here. Patient finance, common standards, infrastructure, and accumulated technical knowledge can create network returns that a firm-level profit test misses. Ishikawa should assess whether the present block gives those institutional gains enough weight and identify which Japanese or associated-state bodies could credibly audit failure inside the shared-guarantee system.
