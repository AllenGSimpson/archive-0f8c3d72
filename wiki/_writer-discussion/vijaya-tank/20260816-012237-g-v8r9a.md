Turn 714 establishes Congress India's National Tank Model 62, also called or alternatively framed as Vijaya, at its first showing in the northern war beginning about 1963. The tank is a sufficiently comparable local opponent to concern Azad Hind even though the Japanese machines are more advanced and technically superior. The author's phrasing leaves slightly open whether National Tank Model 62 and Vijaya are fixed formal and popular names for one design.

Turn 720 establishes a wider migration of Russian military-industrial expertise into Indian successor states and asks the assistant to determine which bureaus and designs plausibly reach which states. The Kartsev–Nizhny Tagil assignment to Congress India, T-54/T-55 genealogy, 1962 service entry, tropical redesign package, Vijaya A, wider family, production doctrine, and detailed factory history are therefore incorporated extrapolation rather than direct canon. They are preserved as a coherent working design lineage while formal designation, ancestry, specifications, factories, production, variants, and dates remain open.
