The prior authority-chain section explains who commands an overseas affiliate. This addition explains what the federation can do when one affiliate fails: reorganize above the subsidiary, preserve the transaction chain, and transfer management or orders while charging a loss somewhere in the house or public guarantee. That capacity distinguishes the corporate house from both an isolated company and a ministry.

Exact boards, ownership, acquisitions, and postwar firm lists remain open. The section also records the danger that group continuity becomes concealed protection and that host assets can be reorganized through decisions made principally in Tokyo.
