The article establishes court participation in Cabinet formation, severe-crisis arbitration, the 1947 transition, Hirohito's procedural style, Akihito's diplomatic role, and influence through information, access, precedent, appointment, and ceremony. I have expanded the information function by identifying what different institutions contribute and how each can distort the record. This is analysis rather than a named court-paper procedure. Household organization, Privy Council status, audience schedule, senior-statesman roster, appointment law, paper circulation, archival rules, and Cabinet-selection chronology remain open.
