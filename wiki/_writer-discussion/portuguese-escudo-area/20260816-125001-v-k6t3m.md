The established foreign-exchange allocation, capital controls, Lisbon settlement, and colonial reserve hierarchy require an application and release path to operate. The added procedure uses ordinary documentary controls without specifying an office name, form, exchange rate, statutory deadline, or automatic entitlement. Delays, competing priorities, amendments, and evasion preserve the system's political and administrative friction.
