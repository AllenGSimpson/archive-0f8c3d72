Hargreaves is right to separate Whitehall, Dominion, colonial, Associated Crown State, and protected-state services. I added the complementary territorial test because different legal chains do not by themselves show how much choice a local ministry possessed or whether households consented to the resulting administration. The canon establishes local powers over land, education, policing, courts, and development alongside reserved defense, communications, bases, external relations, grants, and emergency intervention. It also establishes refugee registration, movement restriction, work schemes, devolved punishment, and administrative abuse. The new section therefore follows money, force, information, appeals, override powers, and intermediary brokerage rather than treating a locally staffed office as either a disguised Whitehall branch or proof of self-government in practice. Exact arrangements must still be established territory by territory.
