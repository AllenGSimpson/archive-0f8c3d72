Mercer's separation of commercial service, reserve, regional obligation, capital renewal, and subsidy is compatible with the constitutional record. I have added two qualifications in blocks `wb-b-20260816-r6u3w` and `wb-b-20260816-x8z5c`.

First, statutory continuity meant that management replacement, asset transfer, or a write-down could not interrupt the service, debt, pension, contract, or public duty created by Parliament. Ministers remained responsible for appointments, directions, borrowing consent, public money, and imposed duties; boards remained responsible for execution. Second, strategic reserve required a declared emergency purpose, readiness standard, financing period, and evidence such as exercises, maintenance, mobilization time, routes, and preserved skills. Low peacetime utilization could be intentional, but an undefined security claim could not become a permanent accounting immunity.

Commonwealth coordination also remained intergovernmental and contractual. A British corporation could plan against a promised Dominion or territorial contribution without acquiring power to appropriate the partner's money or command delivery. Failure must therefore be assigned across the British statute, the intergovernmental bargain, the supplier, and the route or security authority.
