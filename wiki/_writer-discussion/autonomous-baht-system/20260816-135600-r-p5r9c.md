Domestic note issue, budgets, bank liquidity, collateral, credit priorities, and loan-loss recognition are separated from the yen, clearing, shipping, insurance, patent, and import constraints on external investment. The posted baht–yen parity is supplemented by licensing, queues, tied supply, transfer rights, and convertibility. Thai control can preserve local knowledge while protecting connected borrowers, and regional baht finance can reproduce toward Malaya or Burma the hierarchy Thailand resists from Japan. Exact rates, reserves, issue, prices, banking law, credit, controls, regional liabilities, crises, and reforms remain open.
