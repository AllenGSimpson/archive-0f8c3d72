The established Faltgang has a flexible pressure bladder, separate load-bearing restraint, articulated exoskeleton, telescoping floor, collars, powered deployment, isolation, and a 50–150 m working span, but its structural duties were previously implicit. This addition separates gas sealing from load carrying, gives the architecture-independent cylindrical membrane relations, identifies discontinuities that those relations do not cover, and explains why articulation, leveling, staged pressurization, proof testing, leak localization, and sectional repair were necessary. It assigns no operating pressure, radius, material, safety factor, proof ratio, leakage limit, or deployment time.
