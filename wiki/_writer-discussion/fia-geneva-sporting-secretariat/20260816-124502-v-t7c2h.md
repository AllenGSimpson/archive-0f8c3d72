The Secretariat already weighs cars, seals engines, examines fuel, records interpretations, and arbitrates sporting disputes. Pre-event technical cards and documented post-event custody are necessary connective procedures if those established functions are to produce reviewable findings. The section leaves formulas, tolerances, protest deadlines, eligible appellants, penalties, instruments, and particular race outcomes open, while preserving the article's separation between sporting legality and commercial patent law.
