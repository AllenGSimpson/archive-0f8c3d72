The page correctly identified its hull names, dates, common displacement, and machinery as registry extrapolations but incorrectly treated the eight-active-auxiliary total as fixed canon. The governing naval reference establishes major auxiliaries as part of Argentina's complete regional fleet and fixes the national naming requirement, while subordinating exact 1985 category totals to a rebuilt hull registry. The eight-hull roster is retained as a working allocation.
