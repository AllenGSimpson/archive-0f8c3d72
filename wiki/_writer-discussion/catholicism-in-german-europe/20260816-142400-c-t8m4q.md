The German Catholic article now distinguishes the authority of conciliar, papal, liturgical, episcopal, catechetical, devotional, translated, and partisan texts from their civil permission to circulate. It also states the documentary comparison needed before censorship, falsification, Roman silence, or clandestine coordination can be inferred. Named translations, censor alterations, promulgation acts, distribution records, and episcopal remedies remain open.
