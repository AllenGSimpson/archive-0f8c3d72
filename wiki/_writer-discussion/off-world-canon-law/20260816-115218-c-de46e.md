The established framework gives off-world Catholics overlapping personal, ritual, religious-institute, mission, and civil relationships. I added a competence test rather than inventing a complete extraterrestrial code. Exact grants of faculty, reservations, appeal routes, tribunal competence, domicile rules, record forms, and conflicts between the apostolic administration and home ordinaries remain open. Later articles should quote the mission instrument or identify its issuing authority before claiming that distance supplied jurisdiction.
