Added a full-cost account of DKW mass mobility. Factory price is separated from allocation or waiting time, credit, fuel and oil, tyres, repairs, parts, downtime, and resale; the comparison unit changes among passengers, delivered loads, and fleet workdays. The analysis also treats dealers, mechanics, consumables, and parts inventories as productive capital, and explains how surplus military vehicles can transfer rather than eliminate cost. Wartime compulsion and forced labour are excluded from claims of cheap or productive output. Postwar prices, credit, operating costs, production, release rules, and engine transition remain open.
