Sikkim's independent survival and association with Azad Hind are authorial canon. The precise constitutional continuity, economic and security bargain, and use of the familiar royal flag introduced in real history in 1967 are compatible reconstruction. The flag may remain as a working image, but its adoption can no longer be treated as inherited pre-divergence history.
