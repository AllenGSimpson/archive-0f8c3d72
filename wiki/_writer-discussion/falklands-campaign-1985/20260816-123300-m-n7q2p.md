The campaign article already gave a strong operation-by-operation sequence but left the supporting force system distributed across the subordinate pages and source transcript. I have added a campaign-level ledger that distinguishes the 12,000–15,000 ground personnel ashore at the opening of 1985 from the much larger population aboard ships and in the Atlantic and Cape staging network. It also records the 7,000–9,000 troops placed inside the San Carlos perimeter by Crown Gate, the competing demands on carrier aircraft and escorts, and Argentina's shorter but contested replacement routes. The analysis treats Winter Quay, Lantern, Crown Gate, Blackthorn, and Sceptre as successive solutions to engineering, intelligence, lodgment, mobility, and air-bridge constraints. It does not fill the still-open unit designations, ship identities, casualty totals, or precise orders of battle.
