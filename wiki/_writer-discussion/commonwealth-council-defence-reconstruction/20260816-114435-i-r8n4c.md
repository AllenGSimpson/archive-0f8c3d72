Hargreaves's distinction among Dominion advice, colonial attendance, and later action by the competent government is constitutionally sound. I have added that a territory's administrative presence is not evidence that its population authorized the position presented. The canon fixes the Council's Dominion and professional membership but leaves colonial attendance, delegate selection, and voting rules less secure; the addition therefore analyzes the consent problem without assigning representative institutions that have not been established.
