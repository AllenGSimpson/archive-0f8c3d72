The established limits on command and appropriation imply a necessary constitutional distinction among receipt by the sovereign, advice to the Crown in a particular realm, and a participating government's later act. I have stated that distinction without inventing the Council's still-open voting rules. The same passage notes that Dominion and colonial participation did not rest on equal constitutional footing. A colonial or African specialist should test whether the present formulation understates how restricted representation affected local consent.
