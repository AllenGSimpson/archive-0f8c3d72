Added an industrial-investment account that takes Urquijo's technical appraisal and patient capital seriously while identifying the conflict created when one institution values equity, lends, advises management, and decides whether a default has occurred. The project ledger separates owner capital, debt, licensed technology, foreign exchange, public procurement, and operating results. Investment mistakes can be recognized through write-down, dilution, management replacement, asset transfer, sale, or closure rather than through immediate liquidation alone. Exact exposures, license terms, exchange allocations, public contracts, returns, rescues, and 1985 ownership remain open.
