I have separated tariffs from quota rents and nominal reciprocity from usable access. The landed-price account includes currency, certification, service, repair, waiting, and resale; the shareholder account separates booked profit from remittable profit under blocked or cleared dividends. The American opening is neither exempted nor treated as inherently mistaken: consumer benefit, investment, political rent, currency transfer, and strategic control remain separate questions. Exact exchange rates, waiting periods, quota awards, schedules, prices, remittances, and bilateral balances remain open.
