The controlling reference establishes conference consensus, national implementation, four principal associates, and Japanese reserve supremacy but leaves conference names, voting rules, and treaty schedules open. I have therefore described the implementing chain through institutions already established across the four states rather than inventing a supranational secretariat or uniform statute. The post-preservationist test is institutional capacity, paired with the warning that Tokyo still defines strategic failure and controls many instruments of pressure.
