The Setting Bible and Timeline establish restored Philippine sovereignty, the 1958 Cebu accession, the 1959 treaty, constitutional and religious guarantees, defense access, peso–yen clearing, reconstruction, and a normal elected government in 1960. The transcript and existing article fix Philippine title and command at the named bases, legislative participation in permanent facilities, and retained jurisdiction over courts, police, education, taxation, and land. I used those facts to describe implementation through two national chains rather than as Japanese administration of a dependency.

Exact signatories, signing date, duration, annexes, status-of-forces procedure, access schedules, construction approvals, force ceilings, tariff schedules, exchange band, and appropriations remain open. The section treats Philippine jurisdiction as real while recording that covert intervention, war, reconstruction dependence, and Japanese naval predominance constrained the bargain before the treaty was signed.
