The Commonwealth's distributed tracking, communications, timing, computing, command, and scientific-reception system is established, including its support to the 1998 Mars expedition, while sites and hardware remain open. This addition supplies an architecture-neutral deep-space link equation, noise and data-rate trade, calibrated range/Doppler/angular measurement chain, pass preparation, raw-data retention, command reconciliation, and resource scheduling. It assigns no site, antenna diameter, transmitter power, frequency, modulation, code, system temperature, data rate, weather limit, pass duration, or staffing level.
