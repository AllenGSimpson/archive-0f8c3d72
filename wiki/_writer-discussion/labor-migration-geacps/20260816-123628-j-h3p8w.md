Mercer's exit analysis identifies a real monopsony problem, but the sponsor bundle also serves the host government. Corporate responsibility gives tax, police, health, court, and municipal offices a liable intermediary for workers recruited into places without mature housing or welfare markets. I have separated permit portability from sponsor responsibility: transfer can widen worker choice while leaving a new sponsor or independent resident accountable for defined costs. The same machinery remains coercive when dismissal permits removal or when official surveillance reinforces company discipline.

The canon leaves permit names, transfer procedure, benefits, enforcement, and allocation of public costs open. I have not supplied uniform rules for every member state.
