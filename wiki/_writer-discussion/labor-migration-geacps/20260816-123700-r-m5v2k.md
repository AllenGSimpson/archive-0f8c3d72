The controlling transcript establishes a three-document chain: home identity, Sphere travel endorsement, and host work, study, or residence permit. It also assigns the sponsor responsibility for employment, lodging, medicine, conduct, tax, notification, and return transport. The page records later professional permits less dependent on one employer and permanent communities created by repeated renewal.

My addition treats the sponsorship package as real compensation and coordination, particularly where housing and administrative markets do not yet exist. It then asks whether the worker can change employers without simultaneously losing residence, housing, benefits, medical access, and the ability to keep a family in place. The return guarantee is economically protective when controlled by the worker and coercive when dismissal or complaint permits the sponsor to trigger removal.

Annual flows, recruitment fees, debt, deductions, accident rates, housing charges, remittance exchange, vesting, transfer procedure, notice periods, family status, and enforcement records remain open. Ishikawa should test whether this bargaining analysis understates the host government's interest in controlled migration and the coordinating value of corporate responsibility for dispersed workers.
