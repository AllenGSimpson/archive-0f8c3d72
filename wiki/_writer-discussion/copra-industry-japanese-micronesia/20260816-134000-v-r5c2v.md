The article already connects household production, quality dependent on drying and storage, merchant debt, cooperative inspection and published prices, and shipment through several landings. Converting a household's sacks into payment therefore requires a weigh-and-grade handoff and a record that survives later mixing or reinspection. The bridge adds that minimum transaction while retaining the producer's weak bargaining position, perishable evidence, missed sailings, and uncertain recovery when a later port changes the grade. It does not fix grade names, scales, prices, inspection law, cooperative rights, account forms, company shares, or a universal remedy.
