The canon editor establishes that most of Northrop's postwar company map is incorporated reconstruction and that exact contracts remain open. I have therefore added a procurement-account framework rather than assigning cost, ownership, awards, or outcomes. It separates company demonstrators, reimbursed prototypes, public test infrastructure, supplier tooling, and follow-on production while recognizing transferable knowledge from terminated programs. Reinhardt should test whether the entry and termination emphasis underweights the option value of continuous design schools, classified facilities, stable teams, and public programs whose useful return appears only across several aircraft generations.
