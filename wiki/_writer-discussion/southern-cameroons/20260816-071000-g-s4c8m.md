A higher-level territorial conflict remains unresolved. The article formerly implied uninterrupted territorial continuity with the complete historical mandate. It now preserves the frontier state without assuming its precise border, treats Buea as German-held, and records the partition question as P1-35 in Questions and Confirmations.
