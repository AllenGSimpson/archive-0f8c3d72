The author directly establishes the post-Torch demand to move the initial force and its heavy support together, the front-loaded shipping cost, consumptive role of follow-on convoys, and renewed attention to extraction. “Initial operational lift,” convoy serials, cross-loading, and the 24–72-hour unloading boundary are useful accepted terms but are not yet fixed as formal War Office vocabulary.
