The Banaba article establishes surveyed mining plots, leases, compensation schedules, customary and intergenerational interests, dispersed households, and demands for transparent royalties. The linked Micronesian land-tenure article establishes overlapping rights, adoption and residence, colonial registration, and several forums for adjudication. The new block supplies the claim file connecting those premises. It does not choose a royalty rate, company, tribunal, or final settlement, and it allows extraction, notice, proof, and payment to proceed at different speeds.
