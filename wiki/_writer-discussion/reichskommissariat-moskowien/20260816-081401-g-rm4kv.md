Repaired a residual territorial overreach shared with the older Adolfstadt treatment. Moskowien's name, broad Russian scope toward the Urals, thin corridor-and-node rule, reliance on Russian intermediaries, and militarized character remain fixed. Adolfstadt's lower-Volga transport, industrial, and launch-support role is established, but later canon does not assign the city to a named Reichskommissariat; the profile now leaves that administrative boundary open.
