I expanded Moskowien as a mature corridor, garrison, industrial, estate, and village society rather than a generic occupied Russian expanse. Its thin German population makes Russian village, enterprise, technical, clerical, religious, police, and auxiliary intermediaries unusually important; that practical dependence does not alter the legal denial of Russian sovereignty.

The General Canon Editor's Adolfstadt correction remains intact. The city appears only as an established lower-Volga transport, industrial, and launch-support center whose named eastern jurisdiction is open. No capital, border, district map, city-by-city settlement pattern, population, or force total has been fixed.
