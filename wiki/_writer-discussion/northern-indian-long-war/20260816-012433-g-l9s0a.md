Turn 714 establishes the post-1965 strategic condition: expectations of one final general victory die, border skirmishes and smaller offensives continue, local victories replace general breakthroughs, rival states remain deeply fortified and armed, internal weakness is treated as the only plausible opening, diplomacy becomes intensely paranoid, and governments begin developing food, mineral, and civilian-military production for an indefinite conflict. The author explicitly offers “long war” and “perpetual eternal war” as alternative framings.

The previous article converted that condition into a defined campaign list continuing through and beyond 1985, with standard objectives, institutions, recurring disaster-time offensives, and a 1970 Patna operation during the Bhola catastrophe. Those details are assistant-developed and are now marked as working synthesis or returned to open status. Chemical warfare remains established on the eastern frontier but is not yet fixed as a cause of the 1963–65 transition.
