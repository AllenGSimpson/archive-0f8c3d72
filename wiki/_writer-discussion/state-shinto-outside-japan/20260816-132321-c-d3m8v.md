The section refuses to classify every shrine-related act in advance. It assigns civil description to public authorities and the religious judgment of a particular act to the competent religious authority, using words, gestures, object, intention, and instruction as evidence. It also tests formal voluntariness against employment, school, military, housing, welfare, and community pressure and leaves all concrete attendance rules and disputes open.
