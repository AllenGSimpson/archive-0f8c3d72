The established library distributes descriptions, masters, tests, corrections, machine versions, and translation notes physically. Those functions require the supplied pieces to remain associated, a received version to be distinguished from later corrections, and outgoing copies to be traceable enough for a correction notice. The bridge describes those minimum operations without naming an accession form, catalogue, fee, deposit term, or legal effect. It also limits a successful test to the stated example so that library custody does not become technical certification or distribution authority.
