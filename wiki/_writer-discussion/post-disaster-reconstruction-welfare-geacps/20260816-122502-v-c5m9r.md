The article establishes damage assessment, recorded deaths, burial, replacement documents, family tracing, widows and orphans, insurance, compensation, and restoration of civil administration. These functions require an emergency record to follow remains from recovery through provisional burial and later identification, while preserving the legal distinction between missing and dead. The passage leaves national law, forms, identification methods, religious rites, time limits, presumptions of death, and any common Sphere standard open.
