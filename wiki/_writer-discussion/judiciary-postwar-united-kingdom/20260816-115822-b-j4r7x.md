The common sovereign did not create one Commonwealth jurisdiction. The setting establishes local courts in Associated Crown States, a common legal minimum under Ordered Particularism, and demands for judicial review of detention, while leaving exact appellate and reserve arrangements open. I have therefore stated the constitutional test—identify the instrument, responsible government, competent court, and retained jurisdiction—without supplying an unestablished hierarchy. This also records the limit in my profile: judicial restraint in Britain cannot be used as proof that colonial, emergency, or protected-state administrations acting in the Crown's name were restrained in the same way. A colonial or regional specialist should test the practical availability of representation and remedies.
