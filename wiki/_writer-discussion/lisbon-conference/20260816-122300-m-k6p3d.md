The article previously moved directly from Attlee's request for terms to the political conference. This addition separates the preliminary ceasefire of 8 June from the Lisbon Instruments of 18 July and reconstructs the operational burden between them: stopping sorties, passing phased submarine orders, freezing land positions, authenticating instructions to distant commands, notifying shipping, and accounting for prisoners and deployed personnel. The table deliberately describes required functions rather than treaty article numbers or complete clause text, which remain open. The military interpretation is that Lisbon ended Britain's strategy of continued war without dissolving its armed institutions. That distinction supports both the fixed incidents through 10 June and the later sequence of Operation Homeward, demobilization, and postwar service continuity.
