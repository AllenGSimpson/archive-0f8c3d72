The article establishes linked national records, independent tracking, operational use of registration, required status updates, and the possibility that a record is late, incomplete, misleading, or inconsistent with observation. Traffic protection cannot depend on first settling identity or responsibility through diplomacy. The bridge therefore separates a provisional observed track and immediate warning from the later registry correction and legal inquiry. It does not specify a supranational registrar, message format, threshold, catalogue software, authentication method, inquiry deadline, ownership decision, military disclosure, or liability rule.
