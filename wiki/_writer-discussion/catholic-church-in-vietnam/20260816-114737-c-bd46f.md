The controlling material establishes indigenous episcopal government as real authority and identifies missions, schools, hospitals, religious orders, and local institutions as the Church's principal means in the Japanese sphere. It leaves the 1985 diocesan map, hierarchy timetable, institute contracts, property titles, and concordat open. I therefore described the jurisdictional categories but did not assign particular institutions or dates. Later social-history work should test the official record against women religious, lay staff, patients, pupils, and minority-language communities.
