The article already establishes careers moving from Japanese-owned firms into ministries or national companies, training certificates that shape the later market, standardized procurement, localization, and continuing Japanese technical influence. The bridge is the minimum verification needed for an employer to decide whether prior training permits access to its installed system: documentary comparison, evidence of actual duties, and, where necessary, a practical retest or further training. It does not assert a common curriculum, a universal certificate, a vendor rule, or uniform recognition across systems and countries.
