The article used “Commonwealth opening” and “Commonwealth authority” in ways that could suggest a single market sovereign. The established Halifax participants were the United States, Britain, and Canada, with Canada expressly present in its own right. I have separated the authority governing London and British institutions from Canadian and American authority, and treated wider Commonwealth participation as requiring action by the competent government or institution. Exact schedules, implementing enactments, thresholds, and signatories remain open, so the section does not assign a uniform legal form to the framework.
