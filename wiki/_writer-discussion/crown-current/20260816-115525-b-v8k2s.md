The page correctly presents the Crown Current as cross-party, but a reader could still mistake influence for authority. I have distinguished the movement's program from the ministerial advice, parliamentary enactment and supply, and accepted convention required to carry proposals into effect. The Commonwealth Council is the established example. The still-open details of reserve powers and upper-chamber reform remain proposals rather than silently codified rules.
