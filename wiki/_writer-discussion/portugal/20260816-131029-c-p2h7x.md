The addition distinguishes a religious institute's internal government, the local ordinary's pastoral jurisdiction, institutional administration, lay association statutes, and Portuguese civil patronage. It does not infer a named concordat settlement or particular order. The section also marks the colonial problem: missions could provide worship, education, and health care while depending on land, movement, and finance controlled by an unequal state. African Catholic testimony and records made by women religious, catechists, teachers, and medical workers are therefore necessary alongside official archives.
