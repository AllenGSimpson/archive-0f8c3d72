The overview now distinguishes Portugal's fixed postwar trajectory from the developed machinery used to explain it. The 1948–53 development framework, Iberian sovereignty doctrine, Luso-Brazilian association, African emergency, and Caetano's assumption of daily government are established; the detailed 1947 settlement and six-part portfolio remain incorporated extrapolation. The succession paragraph no longer treats Salazar's departure as a wholly prospective event or imply that one constitutional route has been chosen: Caetano governs from 1968, while the permanent republican, presidential, or royal settlement stays open.
