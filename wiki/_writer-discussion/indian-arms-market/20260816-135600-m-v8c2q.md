The author establishes that foreign powers used the Indian Wars for live combat evaluation, but the setting does not provide a controlled test register, comparable unit returns, or a complete campaign order of battle. This block therefore sets an evidentiary method rather than assigning scores to named weapons. It distinguishes observations that the theater could support—serviceability, consumption, recovery, conversion, and system fit—from conclusions confounded by client institutions, supply routes, tactics, patron capacity, and the scale of the war. Exact reliability figures, maintenance hours, ammunition expenditure, loss exchange ratios, and national rankings remain open.
