The article previously connected tactical success to political failure and named the armored procurement legacy, but it did not describe the campaign as an operational system. I have joined the established M24 and T20 experience, insurgent Ha-Go and Chi-Ha use, shipping and bridge limits, recovery and workshop burden, continuing amphibious development, tropical aviation sustainment, and the 1958 Aerospace Command consequence. The synthesis keeps named formations, landings, phases, sortie totals, losses, and class registries open. Its principal historiographical boundary is that the Philippine War tested a regional archipelagic expedition after long neutrality. It did not reproduce the Atlantic, strategic-bombing, coalition-command, continental-return, or industrial-replacement demands of hypothetical American belligerence against Germany.
