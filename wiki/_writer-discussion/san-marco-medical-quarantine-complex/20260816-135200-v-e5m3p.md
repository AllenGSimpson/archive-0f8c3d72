The article establishes screening, controlled observation of crews, staff, equipment, and biological payloads, recovery reception, coordination with public-health and operational teams, and later long-duration medicine. A quarantine facility must distinguish the person or item creating uncertainty from unaffected movements and must record why a restriction ends. The bridge adds access history, confirmatory checking, separate release, and the ordinary cost of delay without asserting that medicine can prove zero risk. It does not create a universal quarantine period, disease, outbreak, test panel, building, bed count, named protocol, medical director, or final legal allocation of authority.
