The established board sectors imply a project-administration chain, but canon does not fix a charter, forms, appeals, labor code, compensation law, or named postwar projects. I have therefore marked the conversion from an approved project into surveys, tenders, parcel files, hiring lists, and inspections as incorporated extrapolation and left the exact procedure open.

The pre-divergence baseline matters because irrigation, credit, and factory recruitment were not institutional blank spaces when Italian protection began. Egyptian public works had replaced most canal-clearance corvée with paid departmental work while retaining coercive flood-work precedents and dependence on provincial and village intermediaries. The 1931 agricultural credit institution and cooperatives widened formal lending without eliminating landlord control or the exclusion of poorly secured small borrowers. At al-Mahalla, factory recruitment already moved through village headmen, kin, temporary migrants, and male and female labor contractors. These inheritances support analysis of how board finance could be captured, negotiated, or redirected below the ministry without inventing a single postwar social outcome.

The new table identifies questions that later source work should answer rather than claiming settled statistics: water timing and drainage burdens; tenants and compensation rolls; cooperative access and guarantors; labor recruitment, wages, housing, and electrical service. Project reports should be checked against petitions, court and municipal files, cooperative minutes, wage disputes, and testimony. I have not treated visible construction or output growth as evidence of consent.
