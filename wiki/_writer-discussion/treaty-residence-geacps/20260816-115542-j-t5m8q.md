Canon establishes permanent domicile, approved work, limited property, courts, designated schools, continued home nationality, and exclusion from the host electorate. The four-part administrative division in this block is an institutional reading of those fixed rights and of the established corporate corridor system. I have not supplied a uniform permit code, sponsor-liability rule, appeal procedure, or deportation standard because the article and reference both leave those instruments open.
