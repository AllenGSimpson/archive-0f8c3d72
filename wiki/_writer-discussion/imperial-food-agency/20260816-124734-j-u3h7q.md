This section defines the Agency as a coordinating authority over reports, contracts, reserves, transport requests, and relief rather than a super-ministry that farms, mills, stores, and carries every sack. It also treats the competent regional government as the institution able to combine subsistence, public health, local transport, crop recovery, and a delivery commitment.

Constitutional status remains material: principal associates can bargain through treaties and fiscal institutions, while integral regions and dependencies possess narrower remedies. The precise Agency charter, leadership, reserve formulas, scandal chronology, and emergency authority remain open.
