The article establishes a route system of varied metropolitan, island, and African ports together with pilots, tugs, warehouses, refrigeration, radio, navigation, customs-related commerce, repair, and onward corridors. A scheduled ship can use those services only when the port coordinates its arrival, berth, cargo, labor, formalities, and inland capacity. The bridge records the minimum port-call chain and allows weather, congestion, equipment, labor, and customs delays. It does not choose port authorities, companies, berth priorities, tariffs, tonnage, schedules, or a uniform imperial operating code.
