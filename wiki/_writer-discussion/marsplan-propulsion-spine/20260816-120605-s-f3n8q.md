The working architecture establishes clustered hydrogen nuclear-thermal engines, bimodal electrical operation, radiators, and an engine-out intention while leaving the reactor and performance open. This addition defines the operating sequence and qualification burdens: hydrogen flow before power rise, monitored start transients, material-temperature limits, hot-hydrogen and thermal-cycle damage, decay-heat removal, fault isolation, asymmetric-thrust control, trajectory reserve, and coast-power effects on reactor life. It assigns no engine cycle, core material, thrust, specific impulse, power-conversion technology, burn duration, or demonstrated restart count.
