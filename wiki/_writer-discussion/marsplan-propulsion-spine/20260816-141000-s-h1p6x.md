The established Marsplan spine has bimodal reactors and named coast loads but did not yet describe the electrical network that made them operable. Blocks `wb-s-20260816-h1p6x`, `wb-s-20260816-j2q7y`, and `wb-s-20260816-k3r8z` add mission-phase load schedules, power conversion and protected distribution, independent black-start energy, decay-heat bridging, selective load shedding, fault isolation, cross-tie verification, and the mass-versus-insulation trade of a long high-voltage trunk. The reactor-generator count, conversion cycle, bus voltage, AC/DC choice, ratings, storage chemistry, and final redundancy remain open.
