Military review confirms that the article's broad distinction is sound but needed an operational allocation between active incident response and the larger standing establishment. The added table separates local response from permanent corridor protection, territorial readiness, frontier control, and expeditionary service. It does not assign numerical strengths, readiness rates, or mobilization thresholds, which remain unestablished. This preserves the canon's limited post-1970s insurgency while explaining why a mature security and military establishment could remain disproportionately large.
