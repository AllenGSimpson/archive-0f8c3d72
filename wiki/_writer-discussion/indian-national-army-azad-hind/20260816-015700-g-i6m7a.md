Turns 712 and 714 establish Azad Hind's Japanese-supported army, limited armored advantage, early internal pacification, campaigns against Congress India, 1956 ammunition-driven reversal, later domestic munitions effort, and participation in the northern long war.

Turn 713 develops the wartime INA into the postwar army as assistant reconstruction. Legal continuity, founding-estate status, recruitment streams, veteran privileges and command dominance, regional composition, women's service lineage, service title, units, strength, ranks, commanders, civil–military law, and detailed doctrinal institutions therefore remain extrapolated or open.
