The StG 60 remains the 5.6×40 mm standard rifle with the registered gas-piston, rotating-bolt, stamped-receiver, 420 mm barrel, and 3.7 kg loaded specification. Cuban and Argentine forms and the A6 adapter are established; other variants and totals remain open.
