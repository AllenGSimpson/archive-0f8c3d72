Direct canon establishes one Japanese nationality across the integral territories and internal residence and employment without immigration status. The existing article already identifies municipal family registers as the proof and administrative carrier of that status. I have joined those established points without inventing a register-transfer statute, database, naming reform, privacy act, or complete family-law settlement. The treatment of mixed households and Japanese-language public life is post-preservationist interpretation rather than a claim that cultural distinctions disappeared.
