The canon establishes nearly continuous rather than uninterrupted polar illumination, storage, reactor backup, directed transmission, and failure of the Moon-wide grid. This addition turns those conditions into an operating architecture: end-of-life delivered solar power, terrain-sensitive array availability, outage-based storage sizing, protected and deferrable buses, independent black start, and end-to-end transmission losses. It fixes no site, array area, cell technology, storage chemistry, load, outage duration, reactor design, voltage, transmitter frequency, optical wavelength, aperture, beam power, receiver design, relay route, or customer distance.
