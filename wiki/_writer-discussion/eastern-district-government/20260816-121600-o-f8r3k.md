This article separates district government from the narrower estate-tenure page. The dedicated eastern canon establishes the mature proprietary governor, his concern with production and local institutions, the codified councils and courts, recognized intermediaries, regulated retainers, enterprise remedies, selective repression, and four regional forms. It does not supply a uniform organigram, statutory titles, boundaries, named governors, budgets, inspection cycles, or appellate code, so I have kept those matters open. My continuity test treats maintained households, infrastructure, records, and restrained coercion as obligations of German authority. That is deliberately an Ostdeutsch defense: it records practical remedies while conceding that Germans alone defined the law and that competent administration made conquest more durable.
