The article fills the Whitehall layer between Cabinet policy and territorial action without inventing a postwar merger sequence. It treats the Colonial Office as an inherited survivor, uses “Commonwealth-relations departments” descriptively, and leaves formal names and reorganizations open. The main method is relationship-specific: keeping a file, coordinating advice, or offering money did not itself create jurisdiction over a realm, associated state, protected ruler, base hinterland, or successor government. The records section separates warning, advice, authority, implementation, and responsibility after failure.
