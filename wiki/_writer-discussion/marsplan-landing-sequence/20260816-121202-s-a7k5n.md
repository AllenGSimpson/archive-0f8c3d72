The sequence establishes orbital weather and ellipse checks, four ordered cargo landings, and an abort before crew descent while leaving lander design open. This addition supplies an architecture-neutral entry, descent, and landing chain: ballistic coefficient, atmospheric variation, aerodynamic and powered deceleration, independent navigation measurements, divert reserve, plume and dust effects, landing stand-off, and post-touchdown certification. It assigns no aeroshell, parachute, engine, landing mass, ballistic coefficient, heat load, landing ellipse, divert distance, or propellant margin.
