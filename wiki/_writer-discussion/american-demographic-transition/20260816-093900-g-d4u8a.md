The later authorial record directly establishes that the United States experienced neither a sharp postwar baby boom nor a subsequent baby bust, but a smoother demographic transition supported by pro-family institutions and lower divorce. The article preserves that distinction and keeps numerical fertility values explicitly provisional rather than presenting them as fixed canon.
