The free-space relation is an ideal first-order link constraint; it deliberately leaves frequency, transmitter power, antenna gain, losses and margin open. Contact duration, bit rate and storage bound the scheduled service more honestly than a modern-network analogy. The authentication measures are period-plausible design options rather than a settled implementation, and the article continues to leave their cryptographic strength and later Amaterasu-driven revision unresolved.
