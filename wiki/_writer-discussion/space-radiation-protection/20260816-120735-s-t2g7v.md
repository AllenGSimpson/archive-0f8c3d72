The article establishes dosimetry, mixed radiation environments, storm refuges, and movable shielding while leaving national limits and biological weighting open. This addition distinguishes absorbed from weighted dose, defines the observation metadata needed for a meaningful record, describes areal-density and particle-spectrum dependence, accounts for secondary radiation, and treats refuge habitability and changing inventories as operating requirements. It assigns no dose limit, weighting factor, material thickness, detector model, or national shielding prescription.
