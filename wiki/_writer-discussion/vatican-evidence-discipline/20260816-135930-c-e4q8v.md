Applied Vatican evidentiary discipline to personnel allegations. General testimony can justify screening or visitation, while a finding against a person requires identity, conduct, chronology, jurisdiction, corroboration, and an opportunity to answer. Civil testimony, diocesan administration, institute government, Roman competence, and canonical judgment remain distinct.
