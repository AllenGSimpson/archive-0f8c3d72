Turn 500 directly fixes the 1971 mission architecture: two runway-launched spaceplanes rendezvous, the tanker refuels the lunar vehicle and returns, and the lunar vehicle lands on the Moon before returning to an American runway. O-2A/O-2B, the Columbia vehicle name, 20 July date, and detailed crew and landing register come from later synthesis and are retained as `[e]`.
