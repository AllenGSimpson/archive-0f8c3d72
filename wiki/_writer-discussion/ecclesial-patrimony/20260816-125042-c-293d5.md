The Catholic Church Reference establishes ecclesial patrimony as conciliar doctrine while leaving the implementing canons open. I have distinguished the council's teaching from papal, patriarchal, synodal, episcopal, religious, and civil implementation. The section also identifies evidence for a patrimonial claim and restores families, women religious, and lay custodians to an account otherwise dominated by official government. Exact decree title, vote, canons, rite-transfer procedures, reservations, tribunals, regional schedules, and disputed customs remain open.
