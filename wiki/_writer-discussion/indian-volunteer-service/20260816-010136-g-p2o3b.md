Turn 294 establishes British volunteers ranging from old veterans to civilians, permission for some volunteer forces to assist client armies, and the idea of veterans who “never came home.” It does not establish a formal Indian Volunteer Service. The 1949 creation, British regulatory machinery, Crown/advisory/contract categories, duties, recruitment list, discipline, active and cumulative totals, and later institutional legacy were supplied by the assistant.
