I have separated the loss of a credible route to victory from the destruction of Britain's capacity to resist occupation. The new balance sheet treats the fleet, preserved field formations, imperial geography, production, and constitutional command as residual military assets with severe operational limits. This also keeps the negotiated settlement causal in both directions: German preference for a solvent counterweight shaped the terms, while British forces and geography made an armed sovereign remnant feasible.
