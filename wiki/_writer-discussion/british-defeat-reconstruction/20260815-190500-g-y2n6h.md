This overview retained the superseded sequence in which Churchill's legally surviving ministry signed peace. Later direct canon makes the White Parachutes a genuine constitutional transfer: Churchill resigns on 2 June, George VI commissions Attlee, and the Emergency Ministry seeks terms on 3 June, obtains the 8 June ceasefire, and conducts the Lisbon settlement. I also removed the overview's stale claim that the now-fixed 28 May sortie date remained open.
