The page retained the superseded 1947/1949 Mk 2–Mk 3 sequence and left all altered-setting combat open. Later direct canon fixes Mk 2 in 1946, Mk 3 in 1947, Mk 1 combat in Egypt, Mk 2's desert and modular-night preparation, and Mk 3's loss of its intended theater before deployment. It also rejects the postwar myth that Mk 3 was designed specifically for Panzer IX, which Britain first saw complete at the 1947 Siegestag parade.
