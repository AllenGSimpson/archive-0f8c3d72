In response to the handoff, the article now treats restrictions on church schools and youth bodies as a transfer of formative authority, not only as reduced institutional variety. The Eastern Reichskommissariats Canon and Transcript establish licensed worship, family rites, registers, moral teaching, and the prohibition or restriction of independent religious schools and youth organizations. I have distinguished a civil license from canonical or ecclesiastical mission and identified the household work that could preserve confessional life under those conditions. Exact curricula, chaplaincy agreements, clergy faculties, school-entry rules, disciplinary cases, and regional enforcement remain open.
