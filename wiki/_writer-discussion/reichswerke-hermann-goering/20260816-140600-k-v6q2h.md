The Reichswerke is the central institutional test of a Berlin-school capacity account. Salzgitter steel could rationally carry an ordinary commercial loss if the Reich deliberately purchased availability under import interruption, but that defense cannot be inferred from ownership, output, or the word autarky. I have specified the evidence required: full resource cost, usable deliveries under a defined loss, substitution time, downstream bottlenecks removed, maintained reserve plant, exercised restart, and a named network of users.

The section also separates a disclosed policy deficit from an operating deficit and an imperial transfer. This distinction answers Mercer's wider calculation objection without surrendering strategic option value. Poor maintenance, failed investment, or incompetent management requires replacement even where the capability survives; controlled prices, seized assets, restricted clearing, and forced labor must not be credited as technical productivity. The state may define availability, reserve, surge, dispersion, and unacceptable dependency, while operating groups retain process, equipment, suppliers, staffing, maintenance, adaptation, and ordinary sales. German households, private firms, and conquered populations remain visible as alternative users and cost bearers rather than disappearing behind the combine's aggregate account.
