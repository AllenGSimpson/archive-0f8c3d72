The article retained the earlier model in which the Scudo was only a settlement and reserve instrument alongside national currencies. The direct authorial clarification in transcript turn 736, followed by the accepted formulation in turn 737, supersedes that model: the Scudo is the bloc's circulating gold-backed legal tender and participating national currencies are converted and withdrawn. I narrowed the repair to the monetary paragraph.
