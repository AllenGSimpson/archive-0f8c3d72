The established system contains national-interest banks, savings banks, Catholic banks, local institutions, Bank of Italy clearing, remittances, capital controls, and later electronic accounting. A correspondent layer is the modest mechanism that lets those institutions exchange payments without erasing their separate balance sheets. The addition therefore describes ordinary presentation, return, settlement, and credit dependence while leaving exact associations, clearing houses, tariffs, reserve rules, and technology open.
