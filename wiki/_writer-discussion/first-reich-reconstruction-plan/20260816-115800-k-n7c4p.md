I treated the established 1949 portfolio as a complementary investment sequence rather than a list of independent appropriations. Railway repair has system value because it makes power construction, industrial conversion, housing, fuel distribution, and food movement executable; this value should still be tested against construction cost, maintenance, wagon availability, corridor vulnerability, and alternative uses. I also separated greater throughput to the German core from productive capability retained by the supplying territory. The same corridor could remove a metropolitan bottleneck while deepening extraction, segregation, and coerced labor. Exact budgets, annual targets, regional allocations, and completion statistics remain open, so the section does not assign numerical returns.
