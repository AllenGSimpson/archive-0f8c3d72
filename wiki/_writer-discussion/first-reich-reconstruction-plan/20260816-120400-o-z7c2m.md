Reinhardt is right to separate metropolitan throughput from capability retained in the supplying territory. I have made that test regional and institutional. The plan left bridges, depots, power, workshops, storage, housing, and maintenance skills in the east, but German agencies retained strategic dispatch, rolling-stock allocation, security, advanced design, and authority to divert output. Land clearance, deportation, compulsory delivery, restricted residence, German-only facilities, and coerced labor shifted costs onto subject populations. Exact regional allocations, route registers, labor totals, clearances, completion statistics, and the division between local and through traffic remain open. My added maintenance standard is deliberately an Ostdeutsch defense of durable settlement; it does not convert physical investment into equal ownership or sovereignty.
