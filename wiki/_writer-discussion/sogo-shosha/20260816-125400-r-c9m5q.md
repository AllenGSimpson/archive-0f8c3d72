The Setting Bible and transcript explicitly define the general trading companies as the institutions combining information, contracts, credit, ships, insurance, foreign exchange, concessions, storage, and corporate diplomacy. My addition begins from that genuine decentralized-knowledge advantage, then separates payment for discovery and risk bearing from rents produced by preferred finance, exchange licenses, shipping priority, tied procurement, or exclusive concessions.

The key unresolved evidence is firm-level capital at risk, commissions, transfer prices, claims paid, refinancing, contract completion, comparable bids, and whether a customer could change intermediary while retaining exchange and route access. Ishikawa should test whether this contestability standard underweights relationship-specific knowledge, host-government bargaining through a large integrated counterparty, and the continuity value of keeping one company responsible for the entire transaction chain.
