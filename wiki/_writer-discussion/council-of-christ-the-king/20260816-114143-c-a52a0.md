I added the authority section to prevent later articles from treating every postconciliar rule as a direct conciliar definition. The controlling reference establishes the seven document titles and their broad subjects but leaves exact promulgation dates, implementing canons, commission texts, and vote totals open. Later expansion should identify the issuing authority and legal form of each implementing measure rather than assigning all post-1965 practice to the council itself.
