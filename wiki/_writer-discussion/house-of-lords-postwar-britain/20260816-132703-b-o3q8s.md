The expansion keeps the inherited Parliament Act 1911 baseline while refusing to import the real-world 1949, 1958, 1963, or later reform sequence without setting evidence. It separates nominal peerage composition from actual attendance, ministerial participation from Commons confidence, legislative membership from appellate jurisdiction, and Commonwealth experience from representation of another realm or colonial population. The setting fixes reform pressure and survival of the chamber but leaves the resulting membership bargain open.
