The four-pseudorange example describes the observability of an unrestricted three-dimensional receiver with one clock offset; it does not establish a four-satellite constellation at every destination. Surface constraints, observations accumulated over time, two-way ranging, Doppler, optical angles and inertial propagation can change the required geometry. The important institutional consequence is that public navigation service must publish reference frame, ephemeris, clock, health and validity information as well as a carrier signal.
