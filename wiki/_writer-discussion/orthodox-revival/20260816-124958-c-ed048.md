The sources establish an uneven Orthodox recovery under occupation and emphasize clergy, monasteries, household devotion, and regional plurality. I have distinguished visible worship, civil recognition, property access, canonical government, apostolic succession, and the less documented labor of lay communities. Exact dioceses, bishops, successions, monasteries, registers, property settlements, occupation policies, regional chronologies, and disputed appointments remain open.
