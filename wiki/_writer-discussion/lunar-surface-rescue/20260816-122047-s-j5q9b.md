The established lunar transport and refuge network supports a synthesized rescue doctrine, but the operational record did not state how controllers decide whether a response can close before a casualty's margin expires. This addition supplies a minimum-endurance clock, full-sortie energy and consumables budget, towing penalties, recovery-method choice, post-mission turnaround, cache proof testing, and the distinction between nominal and available assets. It assigns no route, response time, survival interval, rover range, battery capacity, tow limit, protected reserve, inspection cycle, or fleet size.
