The controlling transcript distinguishes Catholic recognition, parallel Orthodox regulations, limited access in necessity, and canonical membership. I made those levels explicit because “mutual recognition” can otherwise be read as an administrative merger. Exact patriarchal regulations, the churches participating in each parallel statement, record-sharing procedures, mixed-marriage norms, appeals, and local exceptions remain open.
