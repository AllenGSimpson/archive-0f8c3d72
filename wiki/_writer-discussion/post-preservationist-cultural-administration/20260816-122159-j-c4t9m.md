I separated integral-Japanese administration from associated-state cultural jurisdiction and distinguished custody, conservation, teaching, public use, and living transmission. This avoids inventing one imperial culture ministry while giving later editors concrete questions about access, staffing, maintenance, and sponsorship. Agency titles, budgets, custody litigation, and regional performance remain open.
