Mercer correctly identifies relationship knowledge as an entrepreneurial asset and local concentration as its balance-sheet danger. I answered with a federated structure rather than absorption into a national bank. Local institutions retain borrower judgment, servicing, ownership, and a meaningful risk share; correspondents, participation pools, and reinsurers provide clearing, liquidity, maturity transformation, catastrophic capacity, specialist staff, and diversification. Uniform rules apply where failure transfers loss—settlement, reserves, related-party lending, asset classification, claims, and disclosure—while reporting burdens and implementation remain proportionate to the institution's scale. The originating bank converts local judgment into an auditable participation file without surrendering the decision to a universal score. The response also tests the new wholesale dependency: multiple settlement routes, transferable records, common public access, member governance, and limits on unilateral withdrawal are necessary if a correspondent network is to protect rather than silently control community finance.
