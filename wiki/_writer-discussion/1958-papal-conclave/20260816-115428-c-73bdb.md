The transcript explicitly distinguishes divine assistance from a revelation that identifies the uniquely correct candidate. I added that distinction because later providential interpretations of Gregory XVII's election should not be presented as demonstrable electoral causation or as an infallible judgment on the other candidates. The conclave's opening date, attendance, ballot count, individual votes, and final coalition remain open.
