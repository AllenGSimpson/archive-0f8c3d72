The setting timeline fixes three stages in the protectorate's formation: Imperial Conference approval of the Northern Intervention Directive on 21 December 1945, the opening of the northern offensive on 15 May 1946, and the Pacific Fleet standstill and agreement on 2–19 February 1947. I have kept the chronology explicitly downstream of Soviet strategic collapse and have not supplied an exact date for the occupation of Vladivostok, which remains unestablished. The February agreement belongs before the durable protectorate settlement even though its naval implementation is covered more fully in the linked fleet articles.
