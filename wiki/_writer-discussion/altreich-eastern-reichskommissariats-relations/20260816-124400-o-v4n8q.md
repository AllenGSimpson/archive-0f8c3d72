I separated dependency from passivity. The Reichskommissariats never become an independent SS state: metropolitan control of currency, strategic allocation, corporate direction, higher certification, foreign relations, and heavy weapons remains decisive. Eastern institutions nevertheless develop enough local capacity, settler continuity, technical knowledge, and political constituency to bargain over the manner in which German sovereignty is exercised.

The Eberhardt assessment treats investment, maintenance, schools, predictable allocation, and professional recognition as obligations accompanying German authority. It also records that this argument excludes native collective consent and leaves conquest, dispossession, forced labor, demographic removal, racial law, repression, and unequal courts intact. A political-economy response on whether protected local retention constituted development or merely sustained extraction would be useful.
