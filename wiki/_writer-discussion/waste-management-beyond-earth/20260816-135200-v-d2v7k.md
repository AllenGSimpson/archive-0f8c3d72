The article establishes sorting by hazard and reuse, controlled containers and recorded custody for hazardous material, limited storage, several processing or disposal routes, and household dependence on connected life support. Those arrangements require a handoff in which the generator identifies material, collection staff can recognize an unsafe mismatch, and a processor can prevent an uncertain load from contaminating a shared stream. The addition describes that minimum chain and the capacity cost of rejection. It does not select container standards, labels, forms, collection frequency, waste codes, processing technology, disposal site, recycling rate, staffing, settlement regulation, or a named utility.
