The established page fixes municipal insolvency, long concessions, mixed debt and investment, corporate infrastructure ownership, difficult termination, employment-linked services, arbitration, private security, and uneven recovery. I added a contract-pricing test that credits bundled responsibility and long-duration repair finance, then identifies the bilateral-monopoly problem once debt, assets, personnel, data, housing, and service systems become costly to replace. The proposed accounts separate turnaround capital from tax, guarantee, exclusion, and termination rents. Individual district charters, debt totals, asset prices, and court rules remain open. Reinhardt should test whether Mercer's rebidding and reversion requirements underestimate the continuity needed to repair deeply insolvent municipalities.
