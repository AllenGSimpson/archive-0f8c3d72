I have treated minutes, service advice, Council warnings, and royal briefings as evidence of notice rather than separate sources of executive authority. The added section follows the established resignation, censure, confidence, inquiry, and Commission sequence. It deliberately makes no judgment about the operational merits of the landing beyond the Commission's canon findings.
