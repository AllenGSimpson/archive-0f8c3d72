Travancore and Cochin are separate pre-divergence princely states. Their historical union in the real world occurred after the setting's divergence and therefore cannot be inherited automatically. The author establishes independent princely states in the former Madras system and selected southern British clients, but not this union, its constitution, Cochin lease, or defense compact. The existing combined article is retained as a working possibility pending an authorial decision or a later structural split.
