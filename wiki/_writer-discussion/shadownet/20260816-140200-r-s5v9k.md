Added a market account of ShadowNet privacy and pseudonymous reputation. Routing, hosting, directories, moderation, keys, payments, and user labour are treated as produced services with money and nonmoney costs. Signed-handle reputation becomes user capital only to the extent that keys, archives, recommendations, and addresses remain portable. Decentralized providers prevent single-firm closure while leaving access, discovery, hosting, payments, employment, and insurance as concentrated chokepoints. Corporate profit is separated among user demand, data sale, sponsorship, surveillance, and the political retaliation that creates demand for privacy. Exact prices, firms, topology, logs, sponsorship, fraud, insurance, portability, blacklists, subsidies, and law remain open.
