The seven categories are supported by the accepted eastern development, but “levels” should not be read as seven statutory echelons. The list combines territorial scale (estate, district, and Reichskommissariat), an operational formation family (mechanized Waffen-SS), specialized missions or geographies (infrastructure and frontier), and external employment (expeditionary service). I have preserved the roster while distinguishing these dimensions. The firmer command evidence is weapons registration, district approval of appointments, SS training, central heavy-weapons control, emergency reassignment, audited supply, and German monopoly over strategic communications, logistics, and higher command. Garrison is a place and support system able to host several categories; native auxiliaries remain a separate subordinate service order. Permanent headquarters, attachment procedures, mobilization plans, and whether expeditionary units were standing or assembled remain open.
