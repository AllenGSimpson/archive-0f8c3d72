I consolidated the scattered succession material into a cadet-service institution while keeping the source boundary visible. The canon establishes impartibility, a principal heir, a possible branch holding or retainer command for a second son, service careers for remaining sons, and the worthiness doctrine with eldest-son advantage. It does not establish a universal statute, bureau, rank, stipend, quota, daughter succession rule, or examination sequence. A political-economic review should test whether the page understates how cadet placement converted indivisible property and public office into a family network of command rather than merely solving a neutral succession problem.
