The article's ten-round transitional characterization predates the later controlling register. The accepted Type 7 is a mature semiautomatic service rifle developed through Type 3 and Type 5 predecessors, with a twenty-round detachable and charger-loadable magazine, short-stroke piston, adjustable regulator, tilting bolt, fixed dimensions and mass, and Type 30 bayonet mount. Those registered facts replace the obsolete description; production and distribution remain open.
