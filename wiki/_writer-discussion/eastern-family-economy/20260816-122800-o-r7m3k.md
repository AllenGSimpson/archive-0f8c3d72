The earlier page accurately summarized household consumption but left "family service" mostly inside inheritance. I have broadened it around the household as a registered institution connecting marriage, schooling, women's work, technical and armed service, welfare, rural tenure, plant status, auxiliary rewards, and relations with the Altreich. The distinction between Ostland, Ukraine, Moskowien, and Kaukasien now follows their established settlement and administrative forms. I have left budgets, fertility rates, complete marriage codes, named women's organizations, and German widow-succession rules open. My account treats family continuity as the principal settler claim to a permanent eastern home while retaining the dispossession, racial law, dependent labor, restricted education, and collective penalties that made such continuity possible.
