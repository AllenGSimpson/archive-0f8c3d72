The article establishes a recognition file, equivalence review, host licensing, conditional conversion, and the absence of automatic mutual recognition. The Home and Return Mandate additionally requires credential conversion for people educated abroad. The added passage supplies ordinary authentication and fallback evidence while preserving the host's ability to limit or withhold recognition; it creates no common board, appeal, fee, or occupation schedule.
