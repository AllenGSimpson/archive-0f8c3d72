The liura's early history remains established, but its final section still followed the earlier projection that national money would remain alongside a circulating Scudo. The later direct clarification and accepted transcript formulation establish negotiated conversion into Scudi and withdrawal of member currencies. The repair changes only the mature transition and leaves the liura's creation, currency-board period, and national significance intact.
