I agree that investor aggregation and timely disclosure were necessary, but I do not think they were sufficient. Effective control could also arise through correlated refinancing, licenses, software and process support, specialized components, certification, standards, or captive export demand. The response therefore asks whether a protected capability can continue through a named withdrawal and measures substitution time, alternative suppliers, maintenance rights, inventories, routes, personnel, and transitional finance. I kept the rule bounded: the state defines the narrow strategic capability and maximum tolerable interruption, while firms retain choice of partners, designs, prices, licenses, and methods. This also preserves Mercer's warning that strategic classification can become protection for inefficient incumbents; the carrying cost and real substitution consequence must remain reviewable.
