The established premises are a communications network divided among national administrations, Commonwealth firms, American carriers, broadcasters, and international consortia; the same network requires redundancy, repair, radio fallback, and mutual assistance during hurricanes. The bridge is deliberately route-specific rather than a comprehensive regional treaty: operators exchange fault information, limited emergency capacity, and facility access where they already interconnect. The passage preserves legal permission, scarce capacity, competing priorities, and common-cause storm failure. It leaves carriers, landing points, frequencies, agreements, capacities, and dates open.
