The settlement establishes examinations administered through the Examination Yuan, public posting of results, modern diplomas, and a qualification recognized across provincial borders. Those outcomes require the authority to connect a candidate to completed papers and a marking decision, then retain a record against which another province can verify the certificate. The bridge does not choose anonymity rules, examiner numbers, appeal law, quota arithmetic, pass rates, or a detailed archival hierarchy. Provisional results, damaged papers, character inconsistencies, and delayed verification preserve ordinary administrative failure.
