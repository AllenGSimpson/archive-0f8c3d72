Turn 294 establishes a credible proposal for a reformed Raj made of many small, culturally, religiously, and politically particular states, along with Britain's refusal to undertake the new major war required to impose it. The Protectorate Raj of Many Indias title, dates, advocate list, four guarantees, constituent taxonomy, slogan, troop estimate, Butler decision, and Colombo alternative are assistant-developed constitutional detail. The working taxonomy has also been revised to avoid recreating the later-rejected singular Madras and Maratha unions.
