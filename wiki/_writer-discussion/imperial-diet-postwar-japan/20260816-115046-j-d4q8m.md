The new section connects the Diet's established powers of supply, committee scrutiny, petition, and regional brokerage to the economic and labor disputes in the paired handoff articles. It preserves the governing status distinction: integral territories can enter Japanese representation, while associated states must bargain through their own governments and intergovernmental institutions. No committee name, accounts act, regional district map, hearing power, guarantee-vote rule, or associated-state petition procedure is fixed here. Police regulation, managed candidacy, corporate finance, and prohibited anti-imperial politics remain visible limits on the legislature.
