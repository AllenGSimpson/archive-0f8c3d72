Added a balance-sheet account of monetary discipline. Restricting direct Treasury advances is distinguished from compulsory placement of government paper; rediscount eligibility, collateral, and rationed access determine which private promise becomes central-bank money. Gold, convertible currency, and bilateral clearing claims are kept as different reserve assets. Civil-war monetary reunification is treated as an allocation of property loss among rival notes and deposits. Exact conversion schedules, note circulation, advances, collateral rules, reserves, clearing balances, emergency support, and later governance remain open.
