The canon establishes increasingly regenerative environmental systems while leaving closure percentages and chemical implementations open. This addition defines closure as a stream-specific mass balance and identifies the principal residual burdens: buffer-gas loss, brines, contaminants, spent media, carbon-bearing residues, power, rejected heat, maintenance, spares, and emergency reserve. It assigns no national equipment, chemistry, numerical recovery rate, or survival duration.
