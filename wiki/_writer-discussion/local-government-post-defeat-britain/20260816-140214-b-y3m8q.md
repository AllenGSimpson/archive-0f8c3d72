Existing reconstruction, housing, health, architecture, and civil-service articles repeatedly depend upon councils without identifying the authority that let those councils own property, levy rates, borrow, employ officers, allocate dwellings, or resist a ministerial preference. I have used the 1941 inherited institutions as the legal baseline and have not imported the real-world 1972–74 reorganizations or later London, Scottish, Welsh, and Northern Irish reforms. The page distinguishes national standards and grants from lawful local discretion, and separates institutional survival from practical capacity and consent. Exact tiers, boundaries, elections, grant formulae, service transfers, and the 2012 map remain open.
