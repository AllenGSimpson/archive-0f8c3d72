The sources establish the need for broad mission faculties while leaving their standard form unresolved. I have distinguished sacred order, office, delegation, emergency authority, civil crew status, sacramental validity, and licitness so that distance does not become a blanket source of clerical power. Exact forms, canon numbers, grantors, duration rules, subdelegation, reserved cases, authentication, penalties, and the legal effects of particular defects remain open.
