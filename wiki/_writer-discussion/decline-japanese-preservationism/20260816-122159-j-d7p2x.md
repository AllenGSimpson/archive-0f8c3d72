The canon establishes gradual decline without a single repeal, cabinet, or electoral annihilation. I therefore located the change in inter-institutional weight: preservation offices continue to advise and administer, but their claims no longer decide borders, security, transport, or constitutional form. Exact budget changes, ministries, cabinet disputes, votes, and legal instruments remain open.
