This expansion treats Ukraine as a mature colonial society rather than a generic grain province. Its regional form joins estates and hereditary villages to settlement towns, machinery stations, grain boards, mines, factories, Black Sea connections, transport corridors, service families, and auxiliary traditions. I have not fixed a capital, formal districts, borders, ports, production totals, or demographic shares.

The Eberhardt interpretation makes repeated production the test of stewardship: an authority that exhausts seed, soil, machinery, labor, or households for one quota has failed the obligations it claims accompany German sovereignty. The article preserves the limit of that argument. Regular tenure, schools, clinics, technical succession, and local retention stabilized expropriated property and coerced labor; they did not restore Ukrainian collective sovereignty or make German rule consensual.
