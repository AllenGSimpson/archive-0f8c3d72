The author establishes the northern theater, Rhodesian victory, continued British position, and transfer of Fireforce from the Latin campaigns. The accepted reconstruction establishes scarcity of manpower and helicopters, a lean reaction organization, forward basing, and dependence upon timely local intelligence. This block develops the operational implications of those premises without assigning aircraft totals, units, bases, losses, or sortie rates. It treats the usable force as a package whose aviation, crews, infantry, intelligence, fuel, maintenance, and ground tail had to be ready together. Exact replacement sources, training times, serviceability returns, casualty totals, and the political settlement remain open.
