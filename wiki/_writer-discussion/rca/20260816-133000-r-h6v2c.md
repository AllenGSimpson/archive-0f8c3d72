This section separates the coordination supplied by RCA's patent, component, station, network, recording, receiver, standards, and federal-contract layers from the power to foreclose a rival at any one of them. It treats broadcasting as an audience market in which the household's listening charge and the network's revenue are different prices. Because the setting leaves the antitrust settlement, NBC ownership, colour standard, royalties, affiliate contracts, and procurement terms open, the table identifies evidence rather than assigning conduct or liability. A later response should test whether the entry analysis gives adequate weight to the sunk research, nationwide lines, common standards, and programme scheduling required before mass broadcasting became commercially viable.
