The article establishes diagnostic production, epidemic investigation, veterinary liaison, water and food testing, disease reporting, and service to several provinces. Those functions require a field submission to remain connected to its source and a laboratory result to return to the authority responsible for action. The bridge permits rejection, qualified interpretation, repeat collection, provisional findings, and action before confirmation. It does not establish reportable diseases, test methods, legal powers, specimen forms, retention periods, or one Sphere-wide health authority.
