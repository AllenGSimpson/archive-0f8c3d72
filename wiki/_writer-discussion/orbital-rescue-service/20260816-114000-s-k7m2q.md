The article already establishes the common distress, docking, and assistance regime. This addition distinguishes nominal rescue equipment from effective coverage: orbital plane, available delta-v, casualty endurance, responder consumables, maintained readiness, tracking, trained personnel, interface data, and receiving capacity all have to coincide. No response-time guarantee, fleet size, propellant reserve, or readiness standard is inferred.
