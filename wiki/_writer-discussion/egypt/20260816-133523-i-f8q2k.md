The country profile explained the protectorate from the palace, ministries, cities, canal, and development board, while leaving the rural majority as a single social category. I added the village and household scale: large estates, smaller holders, tenants, sharecroppers, landless households, village headmen, irrigation administration, creditors, merchants, contractors, and seasonal workers.

The historical sources establish that cotton and perennial irrigation reorganized land and labor before the divergence. They also show that agricultural labor did not remain within a fixed household or village. Contractors moved men and women from land-poor districts to estates, construction, cotton gins, and textile centers, while official sources recorded contracted and waged work more readily than unpaid family labor and bargaining within households.

The protectorate analysis preserves Egyptian institutional primacy in ordinary government. Rural people generally meet Egyptian ministry staff, village officials, tax collectors, police, schools, landowners, merchants, creditors, and religious bodies. Italian authority enters through development finance, reserve policy, machinery, procurement, concessions, transport, ports, and export credit. This distinction is important because Egypt is a protected national monarchy rather than an Italian colony.

No postwar equivalent of the historical 1952 agrarian reform is assumed. The monarchy survives and the setting has not fixed a later land reform, tenancy code, irrigation-rights settlement, cooperative system, or village-government law. Later work must establish those institutions before assigning holdings, rents, labor rights, or political constituencies by class.

The archive warning also applies to development claims. Acreage, water delivery, taxes, crop grades, wages, credit, and shipments can demonstrate administrative activity and output. They cannot alone show who lost land or water, who supplied unpaid labor, which household retained the return, or whether cultivators accepted the export priorities set above them.
