Southampton is established as an ocean-passenger, mail, baggage, hotel, rail, road, customs, and aviation gateway on long scheduled routes. Those functions require terminal staff to reconcile the traveler, travel documents, accommodation, passenger list, and separately handled baggage, then manage connections that cross carriers and modes. The passage describes that ordinary work and its failure cases without naming a terminal, carrier, form, route, baggage allowance, sailing time, or passenger-control statute.
