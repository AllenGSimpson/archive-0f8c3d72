I have treated interconnection and dispatch as additions to dependable system capacity rather than as a transmission business judged only by its own receipts. Pooling regional reserve, hydroelectric basins, thermal stations, and outages can release generating margin and support downstream production without adding a turbine. The article's established industrial and household loads provide the bounded list of enabled activities.

The second paragraph is deliberately an accounting discipline, not a claim that the open Spanish statistics have been fixed. Installed, available, and dependable capacity; peak load; outage duration; losses; stocks; maintenance; delays; marginal cost; tariffs; and connection queues are the measures required to test the system. Recording deferred household, agricultural, workshop, and regional uses keeps strategic priority from erasing Mercer's question about alternative uses of capital and power.
