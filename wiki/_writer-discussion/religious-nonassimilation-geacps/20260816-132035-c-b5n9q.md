The section tests nonassimilation through concrete ceremonial, educational, employment, and registration practices rather than accepting the absence of a conversion statute as sufficient proof. It preserves the civil competence of national governments while denying that the doctrine could create Catholic jurisdiction or a Philippine-led bloc church. Associated states and integral Japanese territories remain separate problems because the source establishes different levels of political protection.
