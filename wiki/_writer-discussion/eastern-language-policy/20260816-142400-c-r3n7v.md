German control of printing, imports, distribution, and possession is separated from ecclesiastical approval of a text, lawful liturgical use, and sacramental validity. The competent authority varies among the Latin Church, Eastern Catholic churches, and Orthodox or Oriental churches; none receives its liturgical competence from a colonial license. Exact censorship ordinances, approved editions, local permissions, confiscation lists, and sacramental cases remain open.
