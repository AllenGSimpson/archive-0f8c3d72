The Eastern Reichskommissariats Canon and Transcript establish German civil authority, restrictions on publishing, generational script loss, clerical preservation of Cyrillic, and the Baltic distinction. I have separated civil language requirements from ecclesiastical authorization and civil family certificates from sacramental records. Parallel books and household preservation are stated as pressures and possible practices rather than a uniform documented system. Exact diocesan policies, authorized editions, translation rules, register forms, surviving archives, and the effectiveness of clandestine preservation remain open.
