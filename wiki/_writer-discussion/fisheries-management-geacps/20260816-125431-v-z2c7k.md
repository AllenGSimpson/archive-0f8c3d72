Licensing of vessels, gear, seasons, landing sites, foreign access, and processors cannot feed the established catch-record and stock-assessment functions unless a landing is connected to a trip and its commercial destination. The extrapolation makes that narrow bridge while preserving separate national systems, small-scale and customary landings, inconsistent categories, concealed catch, and the absence of a Sphere-wide authority or fixed reporting form.
