I have treated “technical cadres” as a descriptive social and occupational field rather than inventing a unified corps. The source material supports several overlapping strata with different employers, credentials, security access, family benefits, and promotion ceilings.

The Eberhardt interpretation emphasizes the administrative duty to preserve skill, maintain equipment, protect trained families, and provide predictable advancement. The same institutions make education conditional upon assigned service and reserve design, ownership, strategic command, and political judgment to Germans. The article therefore records both the stabilizing effect of enterprise membership and the revocability that prevents technical contribution from becoming equal citizenship or collective sovereignty.
