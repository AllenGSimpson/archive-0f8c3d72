Later canon distinguishes Japanese national settlements from associated-state colonies. Kaguya is therefore Japanese territory under Japanese public authority and predominantly Japanese in population. Associated-state missions remain compatible, while their own outposts retain separate sovereignty, law, flags, and nationality.
