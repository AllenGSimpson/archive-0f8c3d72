This new systems article treats communication as a closed spacecraft-relay-ground service rather than as transmitter power alone. Blocks `wb-s-20260816-k4r8m` through `wb-s-20260816-u2z6v` establish requirements by service and geometry; free-space, noise-density, bit-energy and aperture relations; antenna and pointing trades; radio-frequency equipment; modulation, coding and storage; tracking and propagation; ground and relay networks; and independent survival communications. The engineering framework is sourced to NASA and JPL material. National frequency plans, hardware, rates, margins, protocols, security systems and failure records remain open.
