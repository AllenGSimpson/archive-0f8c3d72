The earlier roster incorrectly counted Shinano as a carrier and preserved the superseded Amagi allocation. The controlling composition is eight first-line fleet carriers in four divisions: two Hiryū II, four Taihō II, and two Hakuryū, plus four Zuihō II light carriers. Individual names and air-group totals remain open where not separately fixed.
