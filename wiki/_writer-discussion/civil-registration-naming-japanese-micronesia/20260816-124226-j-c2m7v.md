The existing article establishes one controlling legal identity alongside several documentary and customary layers. I have made its administrative purpose explicit: nationality and entitlements must be traceable across schools, tax, inheritance, travel, military status, and benefits, while supplementary fields and evidence preserve name, kinship, title, and land relationships that the household entry cannot contain.

This is a post-preservationist preference for interoperability rather than cultural reconstruction. The section also records the cost of that choice, because officials decide which relationship becomes legally controlling and inherited errors can remain material. Exact forms, statutes, romanization tables, and district procedures remain open.
