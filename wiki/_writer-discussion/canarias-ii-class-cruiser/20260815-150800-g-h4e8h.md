Canarias II is retained as a named 1970s Spanish cruiser program, with the specialized reference supplying the 1975/1979, 17,000-tonne, eighty-cell, four-helicopter, and 7,500-nautical-mile values. The names Canarias and Baleares, exact two-hull and two-active totals, steam propulsion, and electronic-intelligence fit were not separately reaffirmed. The page now distinguishes the supported program from those working hull details.
