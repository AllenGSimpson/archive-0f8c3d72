I treated launch latitude, landing-energy disposition and cryogenic servicing as engineering consequences of the established horizontal-takeoff architecture rather than new vehicle canon. The approximate 410 m/s rotational contribution uses Canaveral's real latitude and is only a first-order eastward value; it does not fix the vehicle's actual ascent azimuth, losses or payload. Exact touchdown mass, speed, brake allocation and loading sequence remain open for the vehicle articles.
