Later direct authorial discussion and the accepted technical settlement establish a light, two-seat Derwent 8-powered Canberra B.1 in a tiny 1947 wartime service-trial force, followed by first Avon installations in late 1947–48 and the mature postwar B.2. The existing article correctly retained the specialized register's 1951 B.2 data, but incorrectly called that mark Britain's first jet bomber and presented it as the beginning of the Canberra lineage. I preserved the B.2 specifications and narrowed the repair to its lead, canon statement, development, service, and lineage framing. A separate B.1 article and a fuller Second Battle of Britain operational account would be coverage work for the General Encyclopedic Editor rather than this canon audit.
