The original operational test separated mobilization into time windows but did not force the six theater claims onto one allocation ledger. This addition supplies that check. It treats escorts, merchant and assault shipping, tankers and oilers, landing craft, aircrew, maintenance labor, port throughput, and replacements as concurrent demands rather than independent national capacities. The point is not that American industry could never expand all of them. It is that early intervention branches must identify which commitment receives scarce trained capacity first and which strategic effect consequently arrives later. A counterfactual that gives Britain fuller imports, the Soviet Union larger aid flows, North Africa an American landing, Britain a strategic bomber force, the Pacific a carrier advance, and Europe a return operation on their familiar schedules has counted the same opening mobilization more than once.
