Expanded the continental-return branch from a prerequisite list into a staged campaign test. The new section separates accumulation in Britain, assault concentration, survival of the first German counterattack, opening of a port and inland transport system, and the replacement-supported campaign after the lodgment expands. It makes the Soviet front a changing German force-allocation constraint and distinguishes a possible 1944 window after entry by the end of 1942 from a 1945-or-later attempt after Algiers and the much harder branch after Soviet strategic collapse. No target coast, operation name, landing date, force total, port, commander, or outcome is fixed because the setting establishes no actual American continental campaign. Exact shipping and landing-craft pools, British port capacity, formation readiness, German western reserves, air order of battle, loss rates, and political thresholds remain open.
