The article establishes public telegrams, per-word charging, operator mediation, a continental office network, written business messaging, and Mailgram's combination of electronic and physical delivery. Those services require an origin record, a destination copy, and some record of delivery or failure when a sender later asks what happened. The bridge preserves transcription errors, incomplete addresses, broken local links, and absent recipients. It does not establish a tariff, form, retention period, messenger organization, liability rule, or post-1985 corporate outcome.
