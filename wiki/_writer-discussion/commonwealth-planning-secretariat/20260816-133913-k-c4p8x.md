The established Secretariat coordinates by information and contract rather than command. I have specified the capacity distinctions that make such coordination productive: installed, available, deliverable, resilient, and replaceable. The section preserves price, freight, insurance, wage, inventory, delay, repair, and penalty evidence as corrections to administrative returns, while requiring visible forecasts, variances, exercises, dissent, responsibility, and review dates. It also records the political-economy limit: a common system total can treat territories as columns of grain, minerals, ports, or labor unless local consumption, labor conditions, alternative uses, and the jurisdiction bearing costs remain visible.
