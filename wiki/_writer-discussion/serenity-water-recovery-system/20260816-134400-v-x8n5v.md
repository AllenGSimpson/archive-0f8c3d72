The article establishes monitored storage, isolatable loops, several water uses, protected reserves, imperfect closure, and daily inventory shared among engineers, medicine, hydroponics, and household services. Isolation has practical meaning only if a suspect stream is held, checked independently, routed around where possible, and released or reassigned through a recorded decision. The bridge adds that operating consequence and the competing cost of false alarm versus premature release. It does not choose sensors, contaminants, process stages, quality classes, pipe layout, reserve duration, ration level, failure history, or a named safety office.
