The article previously treated `OV` and `OSV` as separate categories created by the 1963 act. Direct authorial development in transcript turns 1242–1246 instead establishes one `O` sequence, with `X` and `Y` used as ordinary status prefixes, and places Pathfinder at O-1, Zenith at O-7, and Pilgrim at O-100. The Aerospace Armaments Reference has incorporated that correction and explicitly records the older `OV`/`OSV` scheme as superseded. The Setting Timeline and Setting Bible still use the older designations; they should not be treated as controlling on this point until their nomenclature is reconciled.
