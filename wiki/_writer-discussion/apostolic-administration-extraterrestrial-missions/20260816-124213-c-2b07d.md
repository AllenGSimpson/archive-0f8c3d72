The sources establish a permanent personal jurisdiction for off-world Catholics and leave its formal erection open. I have explained the juridical information that an erection instrument would need to supply without inventing that instrument. The contribution also preserves rite, home church, religious institute, military status, and recourse as distinct legal relationships. Exact enrollment rules, curial attachment, ordinary or delegated powers, Eastern coordination, appeal procedure, archive forms, and reserved cases remain open.
