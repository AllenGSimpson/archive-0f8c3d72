The later direct account makes the charter distinction controlling: companies can function as local governments in American extraterrestrial settlements, but they are not sovereign states. Washington retains federal sovereignty, constitutional jurisdiction, defense, foreign relations, criminal law, courts, customs, traffic, rescue, and requisition. International recognition of the territorial extent remains open.
