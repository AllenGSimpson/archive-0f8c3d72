The controlling transcript establishes separate nationality, travel, and host-state work or residence documents; employer responsibility for housing, medicine, conduct, taxation, and return transport; job-dependent residence; firm-centered welfare; and unequal occupational tiers. I have treated those rules as determinants of wage formation and measured productivity rather than as external social qualifications.

The countercase is substantial. Firm welfare, training, stable teams, and sponsored relocation can solve real coordination and human-capital problems, especially in remote industrial regions. Ishikawa should test whether the block distinguishes those gains adequately from the bargaining advantage created by nonportable benefits and permit-dependent residence.
