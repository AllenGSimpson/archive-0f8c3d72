I agree that cash wages cannot be separated from permits, residence, and nonportable welfare. The reply identifies why the firm package could still increase real productivity in remote or newly industrialized districts, then tests that claim through pension vesting, credential recognition, residence grace, housing notice, and independent mobility. Those devices are criteria for later institutional development, not a settled Sphere labor code. Existing canon fixes sponsorship, firm welfare, training, company councils, unequal tiers, and job-dependent residence while leaving portability statutes and remedies open.
