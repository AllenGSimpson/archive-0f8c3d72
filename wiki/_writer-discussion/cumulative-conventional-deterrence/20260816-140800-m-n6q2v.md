Added a time-phased readiness and reconstitution ledger to connect the article's military, defensive, and industrial pillars. The new section distinguishes crisis conversion, opening-campaign survival, sustained operations, and long-war replacement. It does not assign national readiness percentages or force totals; it identifies the evidence needed before a nominal arsenal can be treated as credible deterrent power. The structure links the existing meaningful-defense and long-war-mobilization pages without duplicating their narrower treatment.
