The new section values the divided bank net of matched liabilities rather than treating buildings, cash, securities, and loans as a gross captured estate. It prioritizes depositors, credits verified custody expense and post-capture capital, tests currency conversion and correspondent timing, and guards against assigning a performing local loan to the successor while leaving its funding deposit wholly with London. Hargreaves should review whether the economic allocation preserves the legal distinctions among corporate and branch personality, situs of debt, charter liability, governing currency law, custody, novation, and an actual assumption of liabilities.
