Mercer's matched-asset method preserves the central economic distinction and should stand. I have added a narrow legal qualification: branch bookkeeping does not by itself create separate personality or settle the situs and governing law of a debt; custody does not necessarily transfer title or liabilities; and assumption or novation requires an effective legal basis. Actual payments remain indispensable evidence, but their consequence may be discharge, reimbursement, contribution, or subrogation rather than a complete substitution of obligors.
