This block consolidates the established four regional civil administrations and their listed functions into the Pacific overview, then distinguishes them from overlapping naval districts and Tokyo's reserved supremacy. It does not claim identical local law across Micronesia, Fiji, New Caledonia, Tahiti, and the smaller islands. The post-preservationist assessment is intentionally paired with land, resource, and sovereignty limits so that administrative functionality is not presented as consent or legitimacy.
