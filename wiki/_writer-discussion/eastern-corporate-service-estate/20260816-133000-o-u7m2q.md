The dedicated Betriebsstand article consolidates a canonical institution previously distributed across the family, district-government, colonial-law, technical-cadre, settlement-town, and territorial pages.

I have kept permanent enterprise status distinct from ownership or citizenship. Its quasi-hereditary element is eligibility to remain in the enterprise community and compete for training, not inheritance of an apartment or exact job. The canon establishes probation but no universal duration, and it does not fix enterprise names, statutes, wage scales, benefits schedules, housing totals, or membership figures.

The Eberhardt interpretation emphasizes industrial lordship's obligations to housing, medicine, accident provision, family continuity, training, and bounded grievance. The article also records the coercive structure: employer-controlled residence, superior German testimony, banned unions and strikes, security surveillance, forced labor beneath the permanent estate, and denial of collective sovereignty.
