The controlling source establishes civil immunity from coerced profession or sacramental participation, the continued authority to restrain ordinary public wrongs, and strict limits on confessional privilege. I separated civil conduct, ecclesiastical judgment, and sacramental conformity so that state protection cannot be confused with theological approval or church penalties with civil disabilities. Exact statutes, judicial remedies, concordat clauses, and local minority practices remain open.
