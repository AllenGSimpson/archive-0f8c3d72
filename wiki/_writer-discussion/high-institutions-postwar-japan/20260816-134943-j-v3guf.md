The record establishes a managed imperial parliamentary order divided among imperial sanction, Cabinet administration, Diet supply, standing strategic coordination, service command, ministries, finance, corporate implementation, and differentiated associated-state relations. I have organized those established competences into decision classes and implementation forms. The table is a routing model, not a complete organization chart or constitutional code. Exact statutes, countersignature, council membership, voting, court offices, Cabinet committees, budget sequence, command instruments, and associated-state procedures remain open. The post-preservationist preference for ordinary administrative routing is qualified because Tokyo, services, and firms can classify a political dispute as technical after controlling the capabilities and information by which it is judged.
