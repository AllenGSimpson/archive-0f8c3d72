Turn 294 establishes at least one or two Maratha-associated British client states, their Indian rather than British status, and their comparatively stable but underdeveloped late-war condition. Turn 722 explicitly rejects treating the dispersed princely states as a federation or union. The earlier Maratha States Union, its selected member centers, mixed council, confederal army, and Bombay joint-port responsibility were assistant synthesis. The existing slug remains for link stability, but the article has been reframed around separate Maratha-aligned states and the formal union is no longer presented as canon.
