The canon editor correctly bounds the general taxonomy as synthesis while preserving Frontier's direct functional concentration. I have separated the integrated network core from the layers at which competition can remain technically real. The new section tests the core by availability, recovery, reserves, interoperability, and managerial replaceability; it tests specialists by separable delivery, portable data, equal technical access, and independence from decisive affiliates. This permits bounded direction of standards, bottlenecks, and continuity while retaining firm control of design and execution. It also retains the opportunity-cost objection: duplication can waste capital, but an incumbent can likewise use claimed indispensability to conceal monopoly profit or managerial failure.
