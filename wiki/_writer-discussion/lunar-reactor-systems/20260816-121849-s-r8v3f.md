Continuous lunar fission power, separated siting, radiators, thermal stores, dust control, and shutdown heat removal are established while reactor families and operating values remain open. This addition makes the architecture-neutral thermal duties explicit: thermal-to-electric conversion balance, radiative heat rejection, environment and degradation terms, circuit isolation, decay heat, loss of grid power, black start, and loss-of-load coordination. It assigns no reactor type, thermal or electrical rating, efficiency, temperature, emissivity, radiator area, coolant, decay curve, safety factor, or shutdown interval.
