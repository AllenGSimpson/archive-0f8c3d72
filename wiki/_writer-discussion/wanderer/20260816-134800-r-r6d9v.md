Added an analysis of diversification and product exit. It keeps the machinery concern, office-equipment activity, and the automobile division in separate capital accounts, then asks when shared components, distribution, or skills reduce real cost and when transfer prices conceal duplication. Product narrowing can preserve viable tooling, records, parts, engineers, dealers, or components without preserving every model or owner. Strategic design, emergency-production, and regional-network value remains admissible when purchased openly against a defined capability. Postwar ownership, divisional accounts, public orders, plant allocation, models, and asset dispositions remain open.
