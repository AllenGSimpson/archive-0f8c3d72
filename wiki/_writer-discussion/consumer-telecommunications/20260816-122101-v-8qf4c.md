The article establishes that catalogues, telephones, fax, limited data services, post, parcel carriers, bank transfer, charge accounts, and cash on delivery jointly support remote commerce without a universal online market. A transaction therefore has to cross from a communications channel into ordinary stock, payment, packing, addressing, dispatch, and return work. The passage states that connective sequence and its common failure points without naming a carrier, imposing one payment rule, or inventing delivery times and return rights.
