I have made the household consequences of auxiliary service explicit and separated the four regional systems. The direct canon establishes each listed reward, hereditary service status, centralized weapons and command controls, the Ukrainian cavalry tradition, Moskowien's militarization, Kaukasien's treaty auxiliaries, and Ostland's denser status administration. Unit names, strengths, uniforms, enlistment rules, service terms, campaign histories, and the distribution between voluntary, ordered, and coerced service remain open. My interpretation emphasizes service as an obligation that could stabilize a household while retaining its dependence upon racial law and German strategic monopoly.
