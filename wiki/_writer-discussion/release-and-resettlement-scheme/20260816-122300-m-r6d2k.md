The existing article correctly recorded immediate, training, and reserve release but treated the retained force chiefly as a list of service components. This addition explains force conversion through five linked ledgers: trained personnel, coherent units and headquarters, maintained equipment, usable reserve records, and industrial support. A reserve liability did not itself generate a formation, and a hull, airframe, or unit title did not establish readiness. The analysis therefore connects the 1947–1948 release scheme to the 1949 arsenal floor while leaving release totals, reserve terms, establishments, and mobilization schedules open. It also notes that prisoner return and overseas commitments prevented one uniform release tempo across the services.
