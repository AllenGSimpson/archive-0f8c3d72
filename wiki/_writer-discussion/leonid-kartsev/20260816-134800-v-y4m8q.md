The article establishes that Kartsev's displaced lineage reached Congress India as people, drawings, test practice, and tooling knowledge rather than an intact bureau; it later became the locally adapted Vijaya school and trained Indian engineers who controlled requirements, trials, and production. A receiving state could not rationally commit workshops and a mass army to that lineage without first comparing inherited knowledge with its own plant, supply, repair, and field conditions. The bridge therefore uses repair, substitution, review, and trial work as the modest shared route by which the newcomers were assessed and Indian counterparts learned their methods. It does not choose a migration route, arrival date, formal office, contract, rank, citizenship, personnel list, workshop, vehicle project, or administrative settlement.
