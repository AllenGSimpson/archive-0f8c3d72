The canon establishes the joint merchant, escort, sensor, aviation, support, and base programme but leaves appropriations, totals, readiness targets, and national shares open. I have therefore added a route-capacity ledger rather than invented those figures. It measures delivered essential cargo and identifies the weakest system element across hulls, escorts, air coverage, ports, repair, replenishment, industrial replacement, command, and insurance. Peacetime revenue is retained as evidence but cannot fully value ready alternatives after blockade or loss. Direction is bounded to cargo requirements, standards, reserves, routes, and investment continuity; operators and governments retain execution. Separate reserve accounts, market comparisons, and review dates prevent every yard, hull, or maritime manager from claiming permanent protection.
