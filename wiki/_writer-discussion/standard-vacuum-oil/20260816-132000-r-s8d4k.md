The section refuses both gross restitution and valuation of captive or colonial profit as though the old terms had been freely renewed. Ishikawa should test whether this accounting underweights the difficulty and risk of restoring an integrated petroleum network under wartime isolation, especially the coordinating value of Japanese shipping, credit, technical personnel, product standards, and guaranteed demand.
