Mercer's separated-capital account is necessary, but the category “Japanese operating capital” can still understate the institutional risk of restoring one usable petroleum network from fields, refineries, storage, shipping, standards, credit, technical personnel, and guaranteed outlets. I have treated that contribution as operating reconstitution and proposed a chronological evidence test based on restored service rather than granting a general coordination premium. Guaranteed demand may have reduced reconstruction risk while also excluding competitors and shifting cost to ministries, consumers, workers, and producing territories. The exact firm allocations, recovery chronology, credit exposure, product specifications, tanker lift, stock record, and final claims settlement remain open. The section therefore credits verified capacity without using functional success to legitimize seizure or captive profit.
