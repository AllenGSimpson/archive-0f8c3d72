The article establishes seasonal navigation, changing draft, government cargo reservations, vessel and pilot constraints, caches, winter rationing, incomplete Soviet allocation, and repeated transfer through ports and landings. The last practical sailing therefore requires a comparison between remaining demand and safe transport capacity, followed by an inventory of cargo left for winter. The bridge describes that common decision and its immediate consequence for inland plans. It does not invent a closing date, priority table, port authority, tariff, fleet, warehouse standard, ice-road route, emergency voyage, cargo quantity, or uniform law across successor states.
