The section separates civil title and legal personality from canonical erection, entrustment, and government. It also distinguishes the powers of institutional administrators, boards, religious superiors, ordinaries, and public inspectors. Because exact statutes remain open, the contribution provides a paired civil-and-canonical archive rather than inventing a uniform Sphere corporation form, and it includes women religious, lay workers, patients, pupils, donors, and families in the institutional record.
