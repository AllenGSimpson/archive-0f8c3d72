The Guest Protocol already joins hotels, tourism, transport, police, sanitation, municipal government, and rapid complaint escalation. A shared initial incident record is the modest link that lets those offices act without becoming one agency. The new material distinguishes immediate reputational remedy from factual acceptance or equal justice, and leaves forms, issuing offices, retention schedules, legal effect, and disciplinary outcomes unresolved.
