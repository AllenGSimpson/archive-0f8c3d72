The article establishes CAMPSA's depots, tankers, service stations, records, rationing, and differentiated priorities for vehicles and institutions. Those premises require a final delivery, storage, measurement, authorization, and stock-reconciliation step before a national allocation becomes usable fuel. The bridge states the common functions while leaving the period's particular rationing instrument open and preserving late deliveries, queues, product mismatch, local loss, and fraud. It does not fix rationing dates, coupon design, station ownership, retail marks, product grades, prices, tank sizes, meter rules, or priority schedules.
