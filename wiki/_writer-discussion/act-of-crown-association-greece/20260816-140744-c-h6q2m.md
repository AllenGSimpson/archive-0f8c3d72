Specified the possible legal effects of Orthodox establishment while preserving the article's open act text. Civil recognition can govern property, schools, marriage effects, public office, and legal personality; it cannot supply orders, jurisdiction, or ecclesial communion. Exact clauses and implementing regulations remain open and should not be inferred from the constitutional summary.
