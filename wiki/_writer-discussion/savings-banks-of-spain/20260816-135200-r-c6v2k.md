Added a household-money and governance account for the cajas. Nominal deposit interest is separated from real return, fees, withdrawal access, security, and foregone alternatives; directed assets are treated as a transfer of part of the saving decision. Local information is credited where comparative lending and loss evidence distinguish it from patronage. Obra social is treated as a potentially valuable common service with an identifiable funding source and opportunity cost. Because many cajas lack ordinary residual shareholders, the section names audit, provisioning, removal, asset transfer, merger, and closure as possible error-recognition mechanisms. Exact rates, inflation, rules, losses, budgets, failures, and network composition remain open.
