Turns 712, 714, and 716 establish highly mixed Indian equipment trees: several generations of Japanese tanks in Azad Hind, Soviet and Russian surplus moved by dealers, equipment from many foreign sources, migrating Russian designers and industrial teams, local rifles and armored designs, princely arms production, Azad Hind ammunition manufacture, and later national technical development.

The previous page treated the logistical consequences as equally established: caliber and spare incompatibility, specialist depots, brokered ammunition, technical enclaves, reduced efficiency, embargo resilience, component substitution, repair-led industrialization, and defined national standardization. Those are coherent military and industrial extrapolations, but the author did not directly specify them. Inventories, depots, calibers, units, readiness, stocks, support systems, and standardization dates remain open.
