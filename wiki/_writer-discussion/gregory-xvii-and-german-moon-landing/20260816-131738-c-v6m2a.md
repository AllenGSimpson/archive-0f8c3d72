This contribution treats Gregory XVII's response as one papal intervention containing pastoral, diplomatic, theological, and conciliar applications with distinguishable authority. Congratulation of the German people and mission institutions is not converted into approval of the regime or adjudication of labor claims. The exact address remains open, so reconstructed formulations are limited to paraphrase pending an authenticated text and transmission history.
