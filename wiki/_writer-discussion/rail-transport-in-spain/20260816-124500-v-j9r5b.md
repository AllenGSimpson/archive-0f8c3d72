The article establishes freight priority, wagon control, yards, industrial sidings, documentation, and severe capacity pressure. A consignment record tied to the physical wagon is the minimum mechanism connecting those elements. The addition avoids setting tariff rates, demurrage periods, wagon classes, routing offices, or traffic totals, and preserves the practical distinction between a wagon counted in the fleet and one actually available for loading.
