It gives Omsk–Novosibirsk, Novosibirsk, Magnitogorsk, the AK-47, and Vijaya as examples.

The full 1945–46 two-stage scheme, complete refuge list, team-and-archive packages, formal industrial asylum, exact technical allocation among Indian states, paired bureaus, community institutions, totals, contracts, rosters, and long-term seniority are assistant-developed extrapolation or remain open.
