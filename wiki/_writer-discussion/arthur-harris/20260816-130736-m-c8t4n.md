Expanded the article's brief absence-of-America point into a force-generation counterfactual. The section separates the immediate German defensive diversion created by an American deployment from the later economic effects of a mature combined offensive. It also accounts for British base and transport costs, competition with other theaters, the weak interchangeability between home-defense and eastern-front forces, replacement of crews and aircraft, and American political tolerance for early losses. Exact American group strengths, British base allocations, sortie totals, losses, target schedules, and German defensive orders of battle remain open.
