The campaign article retained Me 264 as its long-range British-theatre bomber after later direct canon split the roles: Me 264 is the long-range Ural bomber associated with Magnitogorsk, while Me 364 is the 1946 high-altitude development used against British airfields and on the final White Parachutes sortie. I replaced the British-campaign references with Me 364 and aligned the representative-aircraft and final-sortie facts. The separate omission of the tiny 1947 Canberra B.1 service-trial force remains a coverage handoff to the General Encyclopedic Editor.
