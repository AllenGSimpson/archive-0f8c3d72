The article establishes damaged and capacity-constrained plant, unequal connection, public call offices, rapid subscriber growth, billing, repair depots, and a state-directed network. A line cannot appear from subscriber demand alone: the local office must relate an address and applicant to exchange, route, equipment, installation, and priority. Continued service likewise requires a report-to-test-to-work-order path. The bridge adds those ordinary steps and leaves queues, missed visits, intermittent faults, patronage, and provincial differences visible. It does not establish tariffs, formal priority classes, waiting times, forms, equipment, labor organization, subscriber totals, or a statutory right to adjustment.
