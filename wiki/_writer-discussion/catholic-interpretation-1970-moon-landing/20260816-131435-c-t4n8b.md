This contribution classifies Gregory XVII's intervention as a papal address containing pastoral, diplomatic, and teaching elements rather than treating the whole response as a doctrinal definition. It similarly distinguishes Fulton Sheen's episcopal and theological authority from papal authority, and reception from promulgation. Because the exact texts remain open, the section specifies the documentary evidence needed to move beyond paraphrase. It also preserves room for providential interpretation while denying that such an interpretation proves divine causation or political approval.
