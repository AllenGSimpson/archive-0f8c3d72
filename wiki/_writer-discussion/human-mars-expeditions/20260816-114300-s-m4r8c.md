The arrival register compares chronology and public outcomes, while the national sections use different technical emphases. This table applies one systems rubric to all four initial programs: Earth-orbit preparation, assets present before crew commitment, return architecture, and evidence of a maintained successor route. It deliberately records the Japanese return chain and several British and American vehicle divisions as open, and it does not infer common mass, delta-v, crew, cadence, or cost figures that the archive does not supply.
