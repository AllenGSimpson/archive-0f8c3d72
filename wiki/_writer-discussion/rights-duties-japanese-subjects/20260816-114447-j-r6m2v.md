The nationality boundary is direct canon: Chōsen, Taiwan, Karafuto, incorporated Micronesia, and later incorporated Pacific territories belong to integral Japan, while associated-state citizens remain foreign nationals. The new section interprets the mature rights settlement after preservationism and does not establish a new bill of rights, regional nationality, or autonomy doctrine. The coercive formation of common citizenship remains part of the account because mobility and access to institutions do not resolve the legitimacy of assimilation, land seizure, unequal schooling, police restriction, or metropolitan preference.
