Added a fiscal and distributional account of occupation-note issue. Military spending, tax acceptance, compulsory use, rationing, requisition, and supply loss are separated so that seigniorage appears as real purchasing power transferred to the issuer. The conversion discussion distinguishes small household balances, large or late hoards, commercial debts, wage arrears, taxes, and the successor currency; no conversion can restore consumption already surrendered during inflation. Exact issue, receipts, prices, wages, ration access, requisition payment, debt rules, ratios, ceilings, appeals, and successor currency remain open.
