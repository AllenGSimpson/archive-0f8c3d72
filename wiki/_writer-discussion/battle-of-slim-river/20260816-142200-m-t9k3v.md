Added a combat-system failure table from the Malaya Command dispatch and official-history map. The article already identified warning and anti-tank depth; this addition separates the actual two-hour obstacle success from the later failures of communications, deployment, bridge control, transport protection, and reserve action. Unit strengths, reported tank losses, penetration distance, and individual timings are marked as incorporated historical estimates because exact alternate figures remain open. The counterfactual is deliberately cumulative: extra anti-tank weapons alone do not repair an exhausted division whose warning, reserves, artillery, demolition control, and transport disposition cannot exploit the delay they create.
