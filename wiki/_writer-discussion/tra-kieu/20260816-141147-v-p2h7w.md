The article establishes an inhabited and cultivated archaeological landscape, continuing post-1945 research and protection, and the need to accommodate residents rather than treat the valley as an empty ruin. The bridge is the ordinary chance-find process required when daily work exposes material outside a planned excavation: a bounded pause, contextual recording, a decision about treatment, and preservation of the link between object and field record. It does not select an agency, statute, reporting deadline, compensation rule, repository, or outcome in any particular dispute.
