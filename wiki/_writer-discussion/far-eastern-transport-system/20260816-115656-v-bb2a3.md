The established premises are the Far Eastern State's rail-to-water-to-local-carrier bulk chain, limited navigation and winter-road windows, difficult breakup and freeze-up, and aviation's role in urgent cargo rather than bulk replacement. The added bridge is seasonal prepositioning by the administrations and enterprises that consume the freight. Missing a lift must have consequences because aircraft cannot replace the same tonnage; the passage permits air priority, project delay, or shortage and expressly leaves forecasts imperfect. It adds no route, depot name, quota, or idealized central plan.
