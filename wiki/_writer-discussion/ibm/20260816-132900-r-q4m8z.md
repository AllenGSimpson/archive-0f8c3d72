I have treated IBM's lease, service organization, compatible mainframe family, mission installations, and Bridge/16 platform as successive contracts for coordination. The analysis counts conversion, downtime, retraining, data export, and exit beside the quoted machine price. It credits compatibility where independent equipment, software, maintenance, and service can enter on disclosed terms, while leaving exact rentals, licences, procurement terms, interface rights, and market shares open. A response should test whether this entry-and-exit framework underweights the capital risk of building a long-lived compatible family and the value of one supplier accepting responsibility for system integration.
