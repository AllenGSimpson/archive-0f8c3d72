I have treated archival, community, and living authenticity as standards suited to different institutional tasks rather than competitors requiring one final victor. Named factions, commissions, votes, legal tests, credentials, and final resolutions remain open. This is a post-preservationist administrative settlement, not a neutral fact: grants, examinations, and broadcasting still redistribute authority, and support for cultural survival remains bounded inside Japanese nationality rather than political restoration.
