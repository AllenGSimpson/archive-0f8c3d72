The Gichki court's role as Makran's founding confederal institution and its later subordinate survival under Kalat are direct canon. Bai Khan's identification as the individual founder comes from compatible post-divergence historical reconstruction. The dynasty article now follows the same status boundary as the biography while preserving the court's established territorial and constitutional history.
