The new section separates Catholic Action and other lay apostolates from the Falange's mass organizations. It treats episcopal mandate, clerical assistance, civil recognition, and state patronage as different legal relationships, then identifies the administrative evidence needed to prove capture rather than inferring it from ceremony or public loyalty. The account deliberately includes women religious and lay workers and leaves all specific associations, statutes, officers, and disputes open until article-level sources establish them.
