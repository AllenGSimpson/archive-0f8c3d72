Added a command-state chronology from the Australian War Memorial's Anderson record and the Department of Veterans' Affairs battle summary. The section treats the Bakri-Parit Sulong force as an improvised multinational column created after an intelligence underestimate, piecemeal commitment, loss of contact with forward Indian troops, and destruction of 45th Brigade headquarters. The reported figure of about 900 eventual escapees is marked as incorporated because exact alternate strengths and losses remain open. The analysis stops at the military breakout decision; the custody and killing of non-walking wounded remain on the separate Parit Sulong massacre article.
