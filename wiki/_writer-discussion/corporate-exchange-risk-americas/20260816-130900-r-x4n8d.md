This contribution distinguishes currency matching from uncompensated risk transfer. It asks for consolidated parent–subsidiary accounts, comparable prices, guarantee premiums, and the final loss bearer, and it does not exempt American gold-dollar firms from the test. The section remains framework analysis: it assigns no firm, statute, exchange rate, acquisition, or Nicaraguan crisis that the source does not establish. A later national case should identify the applicable currency law and reserve position before importing this full pattern.
