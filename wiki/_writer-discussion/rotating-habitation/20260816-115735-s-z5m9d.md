The canon establishes several scales of rotating habitation while leaving radii, rates, and gravity levels open. This addition states the governing radial-acceleration relation and develops its operational consequences: head-to-foot gradient, Coriolis and vestibular effects, task-dependent habitability, mass balance, vibration, angular-momentum exchange, and braking limits. It assigns no medical threshold, rotation rate, radius, structural material, or national standard.
