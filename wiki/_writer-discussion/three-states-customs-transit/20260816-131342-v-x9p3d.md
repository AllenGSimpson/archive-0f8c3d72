The three states retain separate customs and tariff authority while established bonded transit permits sealed or documented cargo to cross an intermediate territory. That arrangement requires a departure record, continued responsibility for presenting the cargo, and a destination action that closes or contests the movement. The passage does not invent a convention, guarantee form, seal type, tariff schedule, or enforcement agency. It preserves national inspection powers and treats broken seals, document discrepancies, and bonded delay as questions rather than automatic proof of fraud.
