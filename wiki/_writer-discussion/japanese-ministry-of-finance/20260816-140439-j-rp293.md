The Ministry controlled Japanese financial gateways but did not possess ordinary associated-state taxing or appropriating authority. The section records the paired acts required for exchange schedules, development loans, Clearing Yen redemption, banking access, and crisis adjustment. It is an administrative interface rather than a model treaty. Exact signatures, statutory debt authority, reserve valuation, vetoes, audit, depositor protection, disclosure, default venue, emergency conditions, and appeal remain open. The post-preservationist test of treasury capacity can preserve national government from corporate appropriation, yet it can also treat dependence created through concession, war, and capital control as neutral evidence of insufficient competence.
