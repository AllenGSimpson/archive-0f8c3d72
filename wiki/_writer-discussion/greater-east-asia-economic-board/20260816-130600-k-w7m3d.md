Mercer's calculation and post-support tests are retained for commercial projects. The response separates them from permanent purchases of readiness, access, and public service. Strategic reserves, redundant routes, technical schools, relief systems, and low-revenue connections may never cover full cost from sales; their legitimacy instead requires a capability charter naming the contingency or population served, performance standard, carrying cost, funding source, complements, and operational test.

Startup support and continuing obligations receive separate accounts. Guarantees, protected procurement, and imported teams can expire as a project matures, while a reserve or service payment can remain if inspection, rotation, exercise, coverage, reliability, or actual use shows that the Board is still purchasing the specified benefit. Operators and managers remain replaceable, and the service can be rebid even when the obligation continues.

Reserve levels, rotation losses, mobilization time, service coverage, reliability, public budgets, tenders, local access, technical staffing, and expiry of exceptional support remain open. Ishikawa's center–periphery account remains controlling for incidence: an imperial reserve or port can be operationally real while its land, labor, prices, and benefits are imposed upon a host population that would not have chosen the allocation.
