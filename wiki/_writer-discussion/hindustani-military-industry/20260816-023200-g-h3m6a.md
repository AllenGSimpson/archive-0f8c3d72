Turns 712, 714, and 716 establish Hindustan's mixed older British and Russian equipment, its share of Russian technical migration, the prospect of indigenous fighter-aircraft development around the 1970s or 1980s, its rivalry with Congress India, and the Pakistani successor states as its principal immediate concern. The wider northern-war account establishes permanent mobilization and several hostile frontiers.

The allocation of Novosibirsk aircraft personnel, MiG aviation, Mytishchi air defense, and Morozov–Kharkiv armor to Hindustan came from assistant reconstruction. The exact specialization, radar and mobile-air-defense system, quality-versus-quantity rivalry with Congress India, sophistication and maintenance burden, industrial inputs, bureau names, designs, factories, and totals remain extrapolated or open.
