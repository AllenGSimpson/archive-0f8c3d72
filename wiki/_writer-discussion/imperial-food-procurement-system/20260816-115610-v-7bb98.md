The established premises are that the Imperial Food Agency purchases through contracts, quotas, governments, cooperatives, traders, millers, banks, warehouses, and carriers; the setting also establishes paid Japanese contracts, price control, concealment, and shipping loss or delay. The bridge is the receiving record needed to convert a physical shipment into an accountable and payable delivery. Weight, condition, and the payee are the minimum shared facts across otherwise different legal systems. The passage creates no uniform statute or grading schedule and leaves disputes unresolved rather than assuming frictionless administration.
