Mercer's effective-price test is necessary at the cultivator and transaction level. I have added the regional bargain because irrigation, mills, warehouses, shipping schedules, reserve floors, and emergency relief generate returns and insurance across several harvests and households. A ministry, government, or recognized cooperative can therefore be the relevant negotiating unit where its reciprocal duties and benefits are recorded.

The response does not treat regional representation as consent by every producer. Ministries and cooperatives can impose targets, distribute investment unequally, and conceal compulsory delivery. Exact quotas, prices, formulas, enforcement, exemptions, and annual contracts remain open.
