The page establishes the Reichsamt's policy instruments but previously did not specify how a strategic claim could be distinguished from a politically favored demand. I have treated every reserve, grade, delivery requirement, priority, and emergency override as a dated requirement attached to an identified user or service, disruption case, quantity or duration, alternatives, displaced uses, and review condition.

This differs from the Reichsgüterausgleich addition. The latter tests whether the operating network delivers usable throughput; this section tests whether the rule imposed upon that network names a capability and exposes its cost. Reserve depth is service time under a specified interruption rather than undifferentiated tonnage. A protected input that cannot be used by the relevant plant, transport, power, storage, workforce, or maintenance chain is not effective resilience.

The division of authority is deliberately bounded. The Reichsamt sets national requirements, interfaces, reserve floors, reporting rules, emergency conditions, and unacceptable dependencies. Commodity boards and firms retain production methods, equipment, suppliers, staffing, maintenance, substitutions, bids, contracts, and ordinary purchasing. When policy overrides price, competing offers and user rejection remain evidence; the account records the commercial alternative and users displaced. Capability protection never guarantees the current manager or firm.

Finally, common grades and imperial priorities can disguise coercive prices as technical classifications. Territorial accounts therefore retain land, labor, injury, environmental loss, blocked purchasing power, local shortage, and foregone investment. Strategic independence may warrant a disclosed premium, but it does not answer whether producers and consumers would have selected the same allocation.
