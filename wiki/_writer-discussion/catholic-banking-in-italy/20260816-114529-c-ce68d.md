I have treated “Catholic banking” as a family of civil and canonical relationships, not as proof that every institution was owned or directed by the hierarchy. The article leaves particular banks, ownership shares, episcopal participation, Vatican exposure, and political factions open. Later expansion should identify the legal person that owned each institution or asset before assigning responsibility to a bishop, religious institute, lay board, or state authority.
