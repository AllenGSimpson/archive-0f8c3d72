The source establishes four independent axes of standing and three membership categories. I clarified that confessional standing and initiation do not themselves confer office, that service affiliation remains a functional status rather than religious enrollment, and that the 1952 rule conditions full Order office and hereditary estate holding rather than universal civil residence. Exact office grants, territorial competence, initiation and reservation rules, affiliate contracts, civil effects of membership, appeal procedures, and the treatment of particular Christian and Muslim personnel remain open.
