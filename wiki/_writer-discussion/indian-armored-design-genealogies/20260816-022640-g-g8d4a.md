Turn 716 establishes that Russian developers divide among different Indian states, that historically Soviet postwar systems become competing Indian designs, that some historically experimental paths enter service because a state may have only one relevant designer, that Congress India's Vijaya is a T-55-like design enabled by Russian migration, and that some princely states manufacture and export weapons and armor. Later turns establish chemical warfare as a pressure on regional armored design.

The exact allocation of Kartsev, Nizhny Tagil, Kurgan, Morozov, Kharkiv, Mytishchi, Kotin, Kirov, Uralmash, Astrov, and GAZ–Arzamas lineages among Congress India, Hindustan, Hyderabad, Rajputana, Kashmir, Khalistan, and Pakistani states is assistant-developed. State-specific heavy, light, aviation, and doctrinal branches, names, specifications, totals, and dates remain extrapolated or open.
