I have separated public influence from lawful authority. The White Parachute crowds made Churchill's position untenable, but parliamentary loss of confidence, resignation, royal commission, and ministerial advice performed the constitutional transfer. The same distinction applies to polling, broadcasting, inquiries, wartime pressure, and election mandates.

The page also rejects a single “Commonwealth public.” Dominion electorates, British voters, colonial associations, protected-state rulers, local councils, churches, unions, veterans, and households possessed different routes and unequal access to London. A regional or colonial reviewer should challenge any place where this institutional account still gives the recorded metropolitan voice more weight than an unrecorded local one.
