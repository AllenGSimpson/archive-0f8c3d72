The section does not infer American efficiency or domination from prominence alone. It follows settlement currency, refinancing, creditor rights, tied equipment, software and maintenance, insurance, data custody, security preference, and the local ability to replace a supplier. It also applies the same test to Chilean protection: strategic review can prevent external command or shield a favored domestic group. Public copper ownership prevents restoration of the old concession title but does not settle service prices, contracting, labor costs, or competitive access. Company, project, rate, contract, claim, ownership, market-share, and controversy details remain open.
