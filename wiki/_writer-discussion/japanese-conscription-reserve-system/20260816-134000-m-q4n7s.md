The existing administrative-availability model correctly distinguishes nationality and legal liability from recall capacity. I have extended that distinction through five readiness gates: registered liability, current individual competence, equipped sub-unit, assembled formation, and deployed sustainable force. This prevents the established large reserve from becoming a paper order of battle and makes cadre depth, serviceability, combined-arms cohesion, lift, maintenance, and replacement visible without fixing reserve classes, refresher intervals, establishments, strength totals, or mobilization timetables. The model is analytical extrapolation. Any future quantified estimate should specify its date, destination, mission, equipment condition, cadre base, and sustainment assumptions.
