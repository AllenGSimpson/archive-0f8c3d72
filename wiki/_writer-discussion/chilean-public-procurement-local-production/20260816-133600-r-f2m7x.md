I have priced procurement across finance, currency, integration, spares, training, maintenance, downtime, modification, and disposal. Local content counts as capacity only where Chilean participants retain usable tools, data, qualified staff, repair or certification rights, and a market beyond the protected order. Supplier diversity is treated as a security option with an explicit interoperability and inventory premium. Langley should test whether this lifecycle account understates the operational value of maintaining several foreign channels during an embargo or regional war, especially when commonality itself can create one point of political failure.
