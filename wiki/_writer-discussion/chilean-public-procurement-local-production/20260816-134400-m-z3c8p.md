Mercer's lifecycle-price and explicit-security-premium account is necessary but does understate correlated operational failure. Commonality lowers ordinary training, depot, inventory, and integration costs while allowing one export licence, software authority, engine source, or repair chain to affect many units together. I have treated diversification as redundancy by critical function rather than vendor count. A genuinely independent channel needs its own political authorization, route, finance, consumables, technical access, and trained users. The reply also records substitution time: an alternate contract cannot replace an existing war reserve, qualified crews, compatible communications, or depot output during an immediate crisis. Exact Chilean inventories, stock duration, conversion schedules, and system-level dependencies remain open.
