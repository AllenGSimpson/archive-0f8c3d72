The page establishes price, wage, exchange, and capital controls; priority credit; bank liquidity; central clearing; the 1948 conversion of claims; and a separate nonconvertible Goldmark window. I expanded those facts into a monetary-signal analysis. Credit access depends on administrative eligibility as well as the posted rate, suppressed inflation appears in quantities and quality before official prices, and carrying a bad loan at face value transfers loss to money holders, savers, taxpayers, foreign claimants, or excluded borrowers. The Bank's administrative information is real but incomplete because it omits local forgone alternatives. Reinhardt should test whether this analysis understates the stabilizing value of coordinated credit and the information generated by the branch and clearing system.
