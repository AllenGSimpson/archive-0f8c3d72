The article establishes new borders across railways, rivers, roads, ports, electricity, depots, customs, banking, and communications, as well as a skeletal common authority and corridor disputes that contribute to war. Continued through movement requires custody records and settlement of operating charges even when sovereignty is contested. The added mechanism remains provisional and generic: it creates no named commission, treaty, tariff, currency rule, or incident chronology.
