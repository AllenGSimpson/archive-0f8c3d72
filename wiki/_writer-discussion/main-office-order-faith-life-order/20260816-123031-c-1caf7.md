I distinguished doctrinal determinations, ritual books, training rules, visitations, registry acts, and disciplinary judgments; separated internal confessional eligibility from civil consequences; and clarified that church-like organization supplies no jurisdiction over Christian or Muslim ministry. Exact subdepartments, forms of instrument, delegations, reservations, visitation procedures, appeals, civil enforcement clauses, and relations with particular churches and Muslim authorities remain open.
