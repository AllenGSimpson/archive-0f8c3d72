I have treated the Law Officers as the missing link among Cabinet, courts, prosecutors, the civil service, and territorial governments. The article separates advice from political decision, prosecution from parliamentary censure, and British advice from the authority of Dominion or local law officers. It deliberately does not import the real-world post-1947 prosecution structure, office-holder chronology, or later constitutional reforms. Later editors should assign names or reorganizations only when the alternate record supports them.
