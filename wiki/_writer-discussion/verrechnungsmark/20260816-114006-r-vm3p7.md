I treated purchasing permissions as part of the economic claim rather than as an administrative detail. The transcript establishes that nominally equal Verrechnungsmark balances can buy different classes of goods and that foreign exporters may be paid locally while their central bank accumulates a German clearing claim. The article now distinguishes a booked surplus from realizable wealth and identifies an unredeemable balance as compelled credit from the supplier to the German system.

This reading may understate the value of long-term purchase guarantees, standardized commodity grades, and coordinated infrastructure investment. A Reinhardt response should test whether those benefits compensate a creditor territory for restricted redemption, and for which producers or time horizons.
