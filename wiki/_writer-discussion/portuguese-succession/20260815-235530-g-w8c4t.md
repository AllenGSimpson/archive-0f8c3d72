The article correctly kept the permanent Portuguese succession unresolved, but it did not distinguish the fixed crisis from the developed restoration chronology. The 1968 health crisis, Caetano's daily government, Braganza rehabilitation, Salazar's 1970 death, and the continued republic at that point remain direct canon. The exact Caetano appointment date, a 1970–71 provisional sequence, a 1972 Duarte II restoration, later Duarte Pio accession, and detailed constitutional mechanics remain developed routes.
