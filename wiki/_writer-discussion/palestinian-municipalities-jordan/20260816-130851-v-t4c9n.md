The article establishes uneven survival of Mandate tax rolls and property files, continued municipal responsibility for water, building, markets, licensing, burial, and records, and a separate refugee property-claims system. Continuing those services requires a municipality to identify present accounts even where ownership is disputed. The bridge therefore separates an operational entry for an occupant from adjudication of title, preserving both service continuity and an absent owner's claim. It does not establish a common municipal law, evidentiary hierarchy, central register, or final claims procedure.
