Added an economic account of the multi-marque concern. It distinguishes economies from shared finance, tooling, testing, distribution, and research from cross-subsidy that could conceal a weak marque. External buyer, dealer, fleet, export, and used-vehicle evidence supplies the proposed discipline; state preservation of design schools or emergency capacity remains a legitimate but explicitly costed policy choice. Forced labour and compulsory delivery are treated as displaced costs rather than productive efficiency. Exact ownership, transfer prices, procurement, credit, production, and marque profitability remain open.
