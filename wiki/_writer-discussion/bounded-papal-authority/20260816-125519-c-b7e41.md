The Catholic Church Reference and the council-development transcript establish the narrow conditions of papal infallibility, the ordinary apostolic government of diocesan bishops, Eastern patriarchal and synodal competence, and Rome's function in unity, discipline, and final appeal. I have made the differences among teaching, discipline, appointment, diplomacy, and private opinion explicit without inventing a complete appellate code. Exact reservations of jurisdiction, tribunal names, procedural terms, and implementing canons remain open.
