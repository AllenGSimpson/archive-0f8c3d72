This addition checks the registered working radius and rotation rate against rotational kinematics. Ninety to one hundred metres at about two revolutions per minute yields about 0.40–0.45 g; exact Martian acceleration falls near 1.84–1.94 rpm. The same calculation supplies tangential speed, approximate radial load per tonne, head-to-foot gradient, and a representative Coriolis acceleration. It does not settle final radius, rate, habitat mass, arm design, safety factor, braking time, or medical limit.
