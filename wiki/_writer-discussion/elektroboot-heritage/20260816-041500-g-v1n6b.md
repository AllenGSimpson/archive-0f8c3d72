The earlier canon audit was made while the naval armament reference was being revised and incorrectly classified Type XXI and Type XXIII design and early production as pre-divergence history. The current register introduces both types in 1944. This revision treats their development and service as direct alternate-setting canon, retains the working 1947 front-line allocation, and leaves complete production and retirement ledgers open.
