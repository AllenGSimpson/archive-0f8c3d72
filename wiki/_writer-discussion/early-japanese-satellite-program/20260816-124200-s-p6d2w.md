The section treats the three missions as different measurement and traffic problems without assigning an orbit, bus or instrument suite. The orbital-period relation and short-contact consequence are physical constraints, not specifications. Doppler beacon tracking, image geolocation and finite store-and-forward traffic explain what the geographically dispersed ground arc had to do. Exact stabilization, ranging assets, memory technology, bit rate and pass schedule remain open.
