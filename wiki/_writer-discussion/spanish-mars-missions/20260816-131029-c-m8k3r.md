The section applies ordinary Catholic jurisdictional distinctions to the canonically established Spanish Martian outposts. It separates a civil mission commander, the competent off-world ordinary, an erected parish, a religious superior, institutional administrators, and lay bodies. It also clarifies that a chapel or resident priest does not itself establish parish jurisdiction, and that state support does not make sacramental government civil. Names, charters, clergy, institutes, decrees, property instruments, and association statutes remain explicitly open.
