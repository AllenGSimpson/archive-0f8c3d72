The author explicitly names a British Expeditionary Corps and assigns it the principal duty of holding British port cities and key locations. The transcript does not call it the British Indian Expeditionary Corps or BIEC. Its parent command, Colombo–Trincomalee headquarters, direct and theater strength ranges, support-heavy organization, exact deployments, and later disposition all derive from assistant development and are now labeled working or open.
