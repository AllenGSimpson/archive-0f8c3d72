The canon establishes the Act's institutions, priority fields, productivity purpose, and later technical beneficiaries but leaves finance, enrollment, qualification, and access details open. I have therefore added an analytical capability ledger rather than invented administrative totals. It treats schools, engineering colleges, apprenticeships, instructors, and scholarships as complementary infrastructure that makes physical investment usable. The contribution also sets boundaries: wage and completion evidence must correct central plans; firms and institutions retain selection and execution; certificates and buildings do not prove capability; and occupational choice, restrictive crafts, regional access, and apprentices used as cheap labour remain costs outside a narrow production account.
