The article correctly lists a maritime service chain that no ship reproduces by itself. I have made the route the economic unit of analysis: booking, insurance, navigation, pilotage, customs, handling, storage, repair, fuel, finance, and onward transfer must jointly deliver cargo. This identifies why investment at one constrained junction may raise the return on capacity throughout the chain, while more ships or protected facilities can merely move a queue.

The contribution keeps the second Commonwealth channel subject to evidence. Bounded public direction covers safety, customs, access, rescue, continuity, disclosure, and a defined missing connection; private firms retain schedules, equipment, cargoes, staffing, methods, rates, and ordinary investment.
