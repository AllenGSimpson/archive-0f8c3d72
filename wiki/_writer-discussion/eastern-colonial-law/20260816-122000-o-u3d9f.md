This overview gathers legal fields previously dispersed among the estate, status, language, education, religion, enterprise, and security articles. The dedicated eastern canon establishes the governing doctrine, codification, legal estates, conditional tenure, recorded duties, German-language procedure, service privileges, local forums, and selective repression. It does not establish a code title, court hierarchy, appellate rules, tax schedules, labor ceilings, or conflict-of-laws doctrine, so those remain open. I have mentioned punitive sterilization only as a separate empire-wide penalty whose precise territorial reach is unresolved. My interpretation treats predictable law as a duty acquired by permanent German authority while stating that the same law foreclosed collective ownership and sovereignty.
