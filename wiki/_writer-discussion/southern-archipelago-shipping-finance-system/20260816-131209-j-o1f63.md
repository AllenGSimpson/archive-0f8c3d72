This section identifies the separate institutions required to move one cargo and allocates authority among national ports, the Council, Singapore, and Japanese carriers, banks, insurers, and repair networks. Named carriers, route schedules, tonnage, tariffs, insurance pools, currencies, and protocols remain open. The post-preservationist preference for reliable shared systems is accompanied by the explicit recognition that credit, insurance, rerouting, and standards could coerce formally sovereign governments.
