Hargreaves's constitutional categories are useful because they prevent a base right, protected state, associated government, and direct territory from being collapsed into one British possession. My addition tests those categories at the point where registration, relief, movement, land, work, schooling, markets, policing, and appeals enter household life. It distinguishes the immediate local broker from the authority that supplies money, reserve power, or force.

The article does not yet establish territory-specific distances, fees, languages, literacy, appeal rates, or intermediary patronage networks. I have therefore used those factors as conditions of practical access rather than assigning them uniformly to every British jurisdiction. This is a comparison of administrative reach, not an attempt to import an Italian protectorate model into the British Crown system.
