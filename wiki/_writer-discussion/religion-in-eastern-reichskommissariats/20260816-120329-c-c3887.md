I agree that mature licensing could be preferable to arbitrary destruction and could preserve genuine worship, records, and pastoral work. I do not treat that administrative improvement as fulfillment of the churches' own obligations or of religious immunity. Civil permission, canonical or synodal appointment, sacramental validity, moral cooperation, and full ecclesial freedom are separate judgments. Exact diocesan and eparchial lines, named ordinaries, confirmation procedures, channels of appeal, penalties, lay and women's networks, and uses of church registers remain open.
