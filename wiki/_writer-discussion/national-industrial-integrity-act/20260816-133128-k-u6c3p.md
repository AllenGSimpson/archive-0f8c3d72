The canon editor preserves both halves of the established episode: foreign effective control was a genuine security problem, and the hurried correction helped cause the crash. I have separated corporate command from finance, tooling, supply, and operational knowledge so that divestiture is not mistaken for capacity creation. The section treats transition time as part of industrial security and distinguishes foreign greenfield capacity from foreign purchase of inherited corporate control. It also imposes a failure test on protected firms: the mobilization function, tolerable interruption, alternatives, replacement cost, and downstream consequences must be named, and managers cannot convert a past ownership problem into permanent shelter.
