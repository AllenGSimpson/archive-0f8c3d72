The Setting Bible and Timeline establish the limited 1947 undertakings, six months of supply, named emergency measures, the 1951 Commonwealth Supply Agreement, and its expiry after the 1955 majority. The transcript further establishes that Union Commonwealth could oppose an individual bill while remaining committed on confidence and supply. I have used those distinctions to explain why support did not create a coalition or transfer ministerial responsibility. Exact standing orders, the wording of motions, enforcement between party leaders and backbenchers, full divisions, and the government's designation of particular measures as confidence questions remain open.
