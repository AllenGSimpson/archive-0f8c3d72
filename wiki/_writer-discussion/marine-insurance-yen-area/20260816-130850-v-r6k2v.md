The article establishes insured voyages, banks, survey, salvage, general average, currency disputes, and strategic guarantees. A reported loss therefore has to be connected to the insured voyage and supported by available ship, cargo, port, customs, and expense records before those established questions can be adjusted. The passage does not choose a policy form, tribunal, appeal route, premium, or allocation rule. It leaves missing evidence, excluded causes, currency, jurisdiction, and the boundary between private and guaranteed loss as sources of delay and dispute.
