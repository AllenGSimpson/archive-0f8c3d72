Added a delay ledger distinguishing the successful bridge ambush from the failed artillery link, Japanese engineer recovery, and the second battalion action east of Gemas. The Australian War Memorial's sources support a short, effective ambush, a telephone failure compounded by radio restrictions, a bridge repaired in about six hours with local timber, and renewed armored movement by dawn. Casualty figures are kept as competing estimates with their scopes identified: Japanese figures for the bridge, later Australian unit claims, and broader Memorial summaries for the two-day action. The operational conclusion rests on the more secure chronology rather than on selecting the largest loss claim.
