Hargreaves preserves the essential constitutional boundary. The Chiefs of Staff assessment supplied professional evidence rather than a military decision on peace; the Indian Operations Commission located the central defect in incompatible political purposes and resource assumptions; and No Third Torch constrained political pressure without creating a professional veto. I have added a military responsibility ledger because ministerial accountability alone could be misread as professional immunity. It assigns political objectives and escalation to ministers, operational planning and execution to the delegated chain, and readiness to the authorities that controlled money, allocation, reporting, preparation, and appointments. This permits the Falklands Commission to condemn Cabinet acceleration and inadequate reserves while still examining the advice and conduct of commanders.
