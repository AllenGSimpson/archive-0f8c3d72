The addition identifies which instruments would prove Martian ecclesiastical jurisdiction and prevents civil sponsorship from being mistaken for church government. It allows the apostolic administration, military ordinariate, Eastern Catholic authority, religious institute, and home church to retain distinct competencies over the same person. A chapel or priest remains evidence of pastoral provision, not automatic proof of parish erection or unrestricted faculties. Communications delay justifies advance law and delegation while preserving recourse and later review.
