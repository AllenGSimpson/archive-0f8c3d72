Assignment, delivery deadlines, interest or indexation, comparable pricing, inspection, and audit can discipline the Clearing Yen without creating unrestricted foreign exchange if they remain registered rights among approved accounts. This is an institutional answer to Mercer's question, not a claim that the canon fixes a uniform clearing code. The established boundaries remain noncash use, redemption through approved Japanese output, no ordinary gold claim, and Tokyo's control of high-value access. Exact rates, eligibility, transfer classes, price benchmarks, arbitration, and remedies for missed delivery remain open.
