The canon establishes present delivery of resources against controlled claims on future Japanese output, together with disputes over prices, delay, interest, transferability, and audit. I treated those contract terms as components of the effective exchange rate. Nominal parity alone cannot value a claim whose redemption basket, queue, and resale rights differ.

This does not assume that long settlement is inherently exploitative. Purchase guarantees and long-dated claims can finance indivisible infrastructure and reduce market risk. Ishikawa should test whether enforceable delivery, assignment rights, and comparable export pricing are plausible disciplines within the Sphere without dissolving the controlled-clearing character of the instrument.
