Added a capital and migration account for the ADN. Federal research, defense procurement, universities, manufacturers, carriers, and commercial providers are separated so that open technical diffusion can count as a return without hiding its sponsor or congestion cost. Civilian adoption of Weltnetz is treated as reuse of physical and commercial capital alongside write-down of incompatible software, equipment, administration, and skills. The protected strategic remnant receives a readiness test rather than an assumption that civilian revenue or inherited management must continue. Exact funding, ownership, prices, interconnection, congestion, reuse, stranded assets, protected functions, and organization remain open.
