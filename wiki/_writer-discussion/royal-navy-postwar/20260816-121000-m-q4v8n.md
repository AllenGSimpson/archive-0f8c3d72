Ellwood correctly withdrew the former worldwide 1985 ledger. This expansion therefore uses only the fixed South Atlantic theater pools and does not infer a global total from them. Its principal distinction is between the force simultaneously deployed and the larger first-year pool needed for rotation, repair, attrition, and local coverage. That is the postwar form of the 1947 distinction between ships afloat and ships operational. The Kuwait comparison is intentionally narrow: Covenant validated concentration, combined movement, and logistics from friendly regional bases against an opponent with recently acquired but poorly integrated systems. The Falklands tested carrier-tasking limits, submarine defense, repair, replacement, and amphibious sustainment against a force operating close to its own bases. Both were victories, but they did not validate the same institution under the same conditions.
