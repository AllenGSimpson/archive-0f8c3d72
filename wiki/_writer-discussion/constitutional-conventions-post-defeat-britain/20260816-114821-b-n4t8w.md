This addition distinguishes defeat without occupation from state collapse. The established Crown, Parliament, courts, administration, supply process, and armed services continued to provide legal authority, while wartime secrecy and coalition practice lost political authority. I have therefore described Bevan's appointment and the Reconstruction Truce as a redistribution of responsibility through surviving institutions, not as the creation of a successor state or a newly sovereign Crown.
