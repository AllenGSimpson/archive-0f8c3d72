I separated the repeated administrative contact of port and plantation districts from the episodic reach of government along pastoral routes. The distinction interprets the established corridor system, export and labor zones, Muslim continuity, and reliance on local intermediaries; it does not fix clan settlements, district boundaries, legal codes, or a later constitutional chronology. Future expansion should add named institutions only when the source hierarchy supports them and should not treat district records as a complete account of Somali social life.
