The article established a broad and sometimes duplicated industrial field but left the economic distinction between redundancy and fragmentation implicit. I have added a capability-layer account: heavy electrical engineering, signals, computation and control, components and measurement, and service and certification. Parallel firms count as resilience only where one can replace another within the required time and does not share the same hidden component, tool, skill, or foreign dependency. Public direction is confined to standards, interfaces, safety, continuity, procurement terms, and unacceptable dependencies; firms retain design and execution. The section also preserves consumer opportunity cost and makes companies and managers replaceable even where an underlying capability merits protection.
