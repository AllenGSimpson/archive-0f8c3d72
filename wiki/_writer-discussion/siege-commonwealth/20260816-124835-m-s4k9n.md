I added the 1947 inheritance audit to separate three quantities that the phrase "Britain survived" can otherwise collapse: legal possession, near-term operational readiness, and replacement capacity. The naval figures use the working 31 December 1947 audit and state its fourteen-day readiness rule; they do not treat every afloat hull as immediately deployable. Army and RAF totals remain qualitative because exact establishments are still open. The section deliberately stops before the later Siege Commonwealth institutions already covered elsewhere in the article.
