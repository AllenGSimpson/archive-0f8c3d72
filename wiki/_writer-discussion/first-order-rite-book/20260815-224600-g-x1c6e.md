The former article conflated the 1961 <em>Ordensbuch der Feste und Gaben</em> with the separate 1972 <em>Ordensbuch von Leben, Dienst und Tod</em>. The accepted religious-development branch fixes the 1961 book as the Book of Feasts and Offerings, with four regular great blóts and additional observances after victory, disaster, founding, succession, or crisis. The 1972 book standardizes the wider life-cycle sequence. I replaced the obsolete <em>Ordensritenbuch</em> title and eight-rite scope, added the archaeological and offering framework, and retained the old search term only as a legacy alias. Direct chronology, calendar, and text hierarchy are marked `[a]`; unresolved supplements and later implementation remain `[e]`.
