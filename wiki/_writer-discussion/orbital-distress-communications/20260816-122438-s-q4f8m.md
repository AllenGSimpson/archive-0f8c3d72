Universal rescue beacons, protected distress frequencies, direct station communications, and cross-bloc rescue use are established, while exact radio and message specifications remain open. This addition explains the architecture-independent survival and acquisition problem: isolated power, antenna coverage, repeated low-rate frames, energy-per-bit trade, minimum first-message ordering, stale-state detection, uncertainty search, independent track association, and authentication that does not discard a life-safety call. It assigns no frequency, power, antenna, modulation, code, frame length, repetition interval, battery life, navigation source, authentication method, acquisition threshold, or manufacturer.
