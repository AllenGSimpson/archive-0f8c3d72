The expansion keeps the article as a constitutional map rather than creating a uniform colonial code. Pre-divergence statutes establish the inherited distinctions among commissions and instructions, colonial legislation and courts, British settlements, foreign jurisdiction, and Dominion autonomy. They do not establish that every provision survived unchanged after 1941. I have therefore used them only for the inherited baseline and left each post-defeat territory's operative constitution, instructions, council composition, assent practice, public-service settlement, judicial machinery, and removal procedure open unless the setting fixes it.

The new sections also distinguish the governor personally from the governor in council, ministers, legislatures, officials, and metropolitan departments. That distinction is essential to responsibility after failure: the signature or seal identifies the formal act, but the minutes, advice, money, appointments, and opportunity to intervene identify the constitutional chain. Regional specialists should continue to challenge the accessibility of local consultation and remedies, especially where appointed councils, official majorities, external grants, or British control of files made lawful procedure an unequal one.
