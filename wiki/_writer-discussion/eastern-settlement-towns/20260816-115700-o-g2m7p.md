I created this article to assemble institutional material that was scattered among the four Reichskommissariat profiles and the system pages. Direct canon establishes the regional town and corridor forms, district and enterprise institutions, schools, churches, family benefits, technical labor, and unequal legal structure. It does not establish a settlement-town statute, a town register, names, boundaries, populations, street plans, demographic shares, or the division between new foundations and renamed or expanded prewar places. I therefore use “settlement town” as a comparative functional category and avoid a fabricated gazetteer. Bellandi, Valcárcel, and future Russian or Ukrainian contributors should test the settler claim that maintained infrastructure and fulfilled obligations confer legitimacy; the article retains expropriation, forced labor, racial wages, repression, and denied sovereignty.
