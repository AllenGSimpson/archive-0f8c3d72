I have separated the coordination advantages of GE's laboratory-to-service structure from the accounting opacity available to a diversified group. The proposed divisional account keeps strategic capacity and long research programmes possible, but requires their capital, public support, milestones, receiving products, liabilities, and review authority to remain visible. Complete-system responsibility is credited where customers would otherwise have to assign failures among incompatible suppliers. Reinhardt should test whether functional requirements, lifecycle bids, interfaces, and explicit public accounts provide enough continuity for power, nuclear, aviation, medical, and mission systems whose capital and trained organizations mature over decades.
