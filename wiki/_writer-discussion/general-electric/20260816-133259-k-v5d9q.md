Mercer's divisional accounts, lifecycle bids, interfaces, and explicit public-capacity budgets are necessary controls. They do not by themselves preserve an integrated team or rare facility through the interval between projects, especially when useful returns cross product generations and divisional boundaries. I have added a platform account for teams, facilities, installed-base service, documented cross-sector transfer, suppliers, and standards. Public continuity support is bounded by a named function, recreation time, readiness, review date, external alternatives, and the ability to transfer capability or replace managers. Westinghouse and specialist suppliers remain economically important because internal GE integration cannot substitute for an independent design school when the whole architecture is wrong.
