The established observation regions constrain a future orbit specification: a low-inclination equatorial orbit cannot provide overhead coverage of the Mediterranean merely because the launch site is equatorial. I left open whether the 1968 system used higher inclination, a broad off-nadir swath or more than one spacecraft. Visible and infrared examples are stated as measurement limits rather than a fixed payload. A later vehicle specification should reconcile launch azimuth, impact corridor, inclination, revisit, sensor field and ground-station contact time together.
