The article establishes Mỹ Tho as the railhead for delta boats and identifies passengers, mail, rice, fruit, fish, livestock, and imported goods as traffic. Those different loads require identification and condition records at handover, while the railway's fixed timetable meets a water network affected by level, weather, and boat arrival. The bridge adds the smallest transfer process and preserves missed connections, partial loading, spoilage, and disputed weights. It does not fix station layout, tariff, liability law, wagon types, train frequency, later gauge, or modernization.
