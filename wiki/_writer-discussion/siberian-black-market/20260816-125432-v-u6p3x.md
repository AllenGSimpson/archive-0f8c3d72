The source corpus identifies exact components as the black economy’s most valuable goods and states that fragmented governments depend on networks for parts they cannot import legally. Because exact replacement stock will often be unavailable, equivalence, adaptation, and local acceptance are necessary middle steps. The addition avoids inventing firms or standards and keeps counterfeit quality, missing test equipment, unreliable warranties, and political protection visible.
