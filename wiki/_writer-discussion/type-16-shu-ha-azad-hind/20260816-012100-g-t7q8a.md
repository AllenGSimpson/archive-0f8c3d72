Turn 714 establishes that Azad Hind uses some of the first advanced postwar Japanese tanks in the northern war beginning about 1963. The tanks are technically superior to local machines, but Azad Hind crews lack Japanese experience, suffer heavy losses, and prompt investment in a tank school. Congress India's National Tank Model 62 or Vijaya is sufficiently comparable to be a concern.

The author does not identify Azad Hind's vehicle as the Type 16 Shu-Ha. That identification, the pre-1963 transfer, limited quantity, 1956 Japanese service date, specifications, 1962 Kai variant, detailed loss mechanisms, and combined-arms interpretation derive from the assistant's technical development and equipment reference. They remain compatible incorporated extrapolation, while model, transfer, units, variants, markings, losses, and surviving inventory are open.
