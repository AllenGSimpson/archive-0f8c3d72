This new systems article treats spacecraft structure as a configuration-dependent load and geometry system, and mechanisms as verified structural transitions rather than incidental moving parts. Blocks `wb-s-20260816-a8m3d` through `wb-s-20260816-h6v1m` cover load paths, strength and buckling, materials, pressure hardware and joints, mechanisms and tribology, structural dynamics and thermal distortion, fracture and life control, and building-block plus flight verification. NASA standards and technical reports supply the engineering basis. National materials, factors, allowables, joint types, lubricants, detailed mechanisms, and failure records remain open.
