Turn 714 establishes widespread civilian armament, conscription, occasional legal expectations of gun ownership, a government preference for an armed population over one unable to resist an enemy breakthrough, and actual wartime cases in which guerrilla forces help prevent a complete advance. It also establishes a culture of expected call-up, battlefield death, treason accusation, and political repression.

The Armed Reserve Society term, common annual reserve service, household or local-depot rifles, sealed ammunition, gas masks, field kits, mobilization papers, receivers, drills, territorial assignments, state profiles, and administration of ordinary life are assistant-developed. They are preserved as working institutional possibilities rather than shared established practice. Statutes, ages, terms, weapons, custody, training, exemptions, participation, and state variation remain open.
