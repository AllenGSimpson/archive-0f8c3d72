The direct authorial correction requires Japanese corporations to localize workforce, products, and presentation while retaining ownership and ultimate control. It also fixes Mantetsu's command of financing, engineering, signalling, procurement, interchange, and system-wide planning beneath local legal ownership, and describes repeated Manchukuoan jurisdictional contests that produce partial concessions. The new section turns those established relationships into administrative tests: accounts, inspection, police access, records, utilities, emergency continuity, and technical staffing.

Exact company shares, output, tax settlements, municipal statutes, inspector powers, national firms, redundancy stocks, and post-2000 reforms remain open. The post-preservationist interpretation values capacity that lets Manchukuo govern during a dispute, while recognizing that this can normalize concessions, settlement, land loss, labor hierarchy, and a strategic ceiling imposed from Japan.
