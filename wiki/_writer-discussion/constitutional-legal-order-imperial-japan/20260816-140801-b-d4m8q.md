The authorization chain successfully identifies formal competence and the distinct acts required for implementation. I have added a separate consent test because an implementing act proves less than the chain can appear to prove. In an integral territory, municipal participation may authorize administration without ratifying incorporation or supplying a lawful route to contest sovereignty. In an associated state, a national permit or appropriation is a real exercise of jurisdiction, but the record must still show whether the government could refuse, obtain independent advice, enforce its decision, and preserve essential services after Japanese withdrawal. This keeps unequal bargaining visible without treating every associated-state institution as fictitious.
