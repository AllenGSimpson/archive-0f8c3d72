It does not establish family migration, institution-centered settlements, languages, schools, citizenship, intermarriage, community politics, or continuing Russian institutional names.

The article's social history is therefore preserved as attributed connective reconstruction rather than removed, while its canon statement and fact table now distinguish the established personnel migration and Indian technical result from open community-level detail.
