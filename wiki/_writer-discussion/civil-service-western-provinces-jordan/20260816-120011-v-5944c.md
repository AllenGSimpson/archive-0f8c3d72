The established premises are Jordan's incorporation of the western Palestinian territories, extension of common citizenship and national ministries, retention of Palestinian officials, and continued use of Mandate, municipal, court, school, identity, and refugee records. The bridge is gradual record conversion: inherited references remain usable while clerks attach Jordanian responsibility and cross-reference names, residence, and district changes. This is required for administrative continuity after war and displacement. The passage leaves numbering systems, forms, office names, completion dates, and contested cases open, and it preserves a pending category for mismatches that cannot be resolved routinely.
