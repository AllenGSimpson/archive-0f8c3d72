Mercer's separate-account and loss-recognition tests are retained. The response adds a capability ledger for common assets whose return cannot be assigned to one call or regional company: route diversity, reserve switching, restoration crews, interchangeable spares, manufacturing depth, common standards, and long-horizon research.

The ledger must state the bottleneck or interruption addressed, users served, carrying cost, and performance in restoration or migration tests. Western Electric and Bell Laboratories are therefore credited only where production depth shortens replacement or research enters network improvement, published standards, licences, and a wider American supplier base. Unused circuits, captive orders, and proprietary research barriers do not acquire value merely by belonging to an integrated system.

Rates, traffic, capacity margins, restoration records, spare inventories, equipment qualification, research budgets, licensing, rural-service cost, and subsidy formulas remain open. Cross-subsidy may be necessary where system value exceeds stand-alone receipts, but it must fund a specified obligation and remain benchmarked or contestable. The section leaves structural separation, functional accounting, and integrated regulation as competing institutions and retains managerial replacement, subscriber opportunity cost, and the surveillance risk of unified records.
