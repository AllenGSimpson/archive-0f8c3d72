The page omitted Shinano and treated Yamato and Musashi as the active first-line heavy force. The later register establishes three completed Yamato-class battleships in reserve, support, training, or experimental roles, with Shinano reconstructed circa 1970–74 as a nuclear battleship and electrical testbed. Four modern fast battleships guard first-line carrier formations.
