The established sequence is conquest, three military command zones, seizure or custody of the commanding economy, Japanese corporate operation, continuity of production, and successor sovereignty after those rights were embedded. The new section makes explicit the functional connection between property transfer and operating capacity: records, personnel, repairs, working funds, suppliers, transport, standards, and buyers had to be assembled around the physical estate. This is institutional interpretation rather than a company-by-company transfer register. Exact custodial instruments, company allocations, valuations, compensation, personnel policy, command boundaries, production recovery, coercive labor practice, and local participation remain open. The post-preservationist continuity test is deliberately bounded: a proposed successor should be able to maintain the complete service chain, but incumbent Japanese control cannot define every excluded local alternative as proof that transfer was impossible.
