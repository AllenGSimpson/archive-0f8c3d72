The country page had carried Argentina's prewar and wartime Einheitspakt association forward to 2000 as an unchanged settled formal status. The later record directly establishes Punta del Este's termination of war-related German arrangements, restrictions on German bases and southern deployments, retention of German-pattern systems, and entry of American and Latin channels. It does not state whether a residual nonmilitary Einheitspakt association formally survived. I therefore preserved the pre-1986 status and postwar dual system while reopening the residual membership question.
