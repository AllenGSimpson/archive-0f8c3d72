The Secretariat completes the decision chain that the Cabinet, civil-service, Law Officer, Treasury, and inquiry articles imply. I have treated Cabinet conclusions as authoritative records of collective decisions but not as verbatim or exhaustive accounts. The article distinguishes British Cabinet machinery from Commonwealth intergovernmental secretariats and from the separate Cabinets of the Dominions. Exact post-divergence Cabinet Secretaries, committee names, paper series, intelligence machinery, and organizational transfers remain open.
