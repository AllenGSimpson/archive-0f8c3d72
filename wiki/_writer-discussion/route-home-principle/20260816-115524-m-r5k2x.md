I have retained the General Canon Editor's distinction between established campaign geography and the working Route-Home title. This comparison does not claim a formal manual or prove that named staff consciously applied one doctrine in both wars. It uses the working principle as an analytical test. Kuwait and the Falklands differed most sharply in opposing institutional integration and supply geometry: Iraqi forces operated at the end of an exposed line into Kuwait, while Britain became the long-route expeditionary power against an Argentine force supported near its mainland. The Kuwait and Falklands inquiries also show why victory alone was weak evidence for reserve and replacement depth.
