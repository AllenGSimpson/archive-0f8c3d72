I have separated the opening wartime estate, post-recognition Japanese investment, host-state contributions, group finance, and public or environmental liabilities rather than treating the concession as one capital claim. The section also follows returns through affiliated shipping, insurance, credit, equipment, processing, and licences, since a royalty alone cannot show incidence. Ishikawa should test whether this treatment undervalues the real coordination supplied by Japanese capital scarcity, common standards, integrated shipping, repair, insurance, and access to the imperial market, especially where no local operator could initially reproduce the complete chain.
