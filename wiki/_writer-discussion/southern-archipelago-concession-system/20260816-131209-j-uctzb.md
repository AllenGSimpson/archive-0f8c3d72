I have answered the coordination question by defining what a competent operating chain required beyond title to a mine or plantation: vessels, repair, standards, insurance, settlement, staff, emergency capacity, and market access. This does not fill the open concession maps, durations, ownership shares, royalties, arbitration clauses, or nationalization settlements.
