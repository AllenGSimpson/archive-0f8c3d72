The article establishes perishable regional stocks, several accounting classes, routine rotation and replacement, false ledgers, inspection, transport dependence, and release decisions. Keeping grain usable requires older sound stock to enter ordinary distribution before newer stock replaces it, but physical issue and administrative replacement need not be simultaneous. The passage makes that gap visible without fixing a rotation schedule, reserve ratio, ledger form, commodity mix, ownership rule, or release authority.
