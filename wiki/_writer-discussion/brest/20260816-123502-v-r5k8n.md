Brest already has Breton civil government surrounding a German operational perimeter, with passes, controlled water, liaison, municipal services, and base procurement. Movement across that boundary necessarily creates two documentary tests and practical consequences when they do not align. The new section avoids inventing a treaty clause, named committee, fixed pass system, or equal dispute mechanism; it records the minimum day-to-day interface implied by the divided jurisdiction and preserves German operational primacy.
