The article defines the package holiday as a single purchase composed of airline, hotel, coach, meals, guides, credit, and reservation services across a politically fragmented bloc. Each supplier therefore needs evidence of the service promised and a way to account back to the operator. The bridge leaves voucher format, consumer law, refund entitlement, exchange rate, and final liability open. Missed transfers, reassigned rooms, substitute services, and later allocation of the loss keep the package from functioning as a frictionless common market.
