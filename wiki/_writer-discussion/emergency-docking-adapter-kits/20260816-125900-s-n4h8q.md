Canon establishes emergency adapters by the middle 1980s but leaves their interfaces and certification open. This addition separates rendezvous, soft capture, hard structural mate, pressure seal, hatch release, and service transfer; quantifies hatch pressure load; and treats the adapter stack, vehicle control limits, converters, regulators, test gear, spares, and crew training as one qualified system. It assigns no ring diameter, latch count, capture envelope, closure rate, stiffness, pressure, hatch area, seal material, voltage, gas mixture, connector, carrier allocation, certification authority, or approved national pairing.
