The existing Sabine block covers catalogue production and avoidance decisions. This addition addresses the complementary population that cannot be warned against object by object: sensor-dependent trackability, hypervelocity energy, spaced shielding, vulnerable external systems, statistical environment models, EVA restrictions, impact isolation, and inspection. The one-gram/10-kilometre-per-second example illustrates the established kinetic-energy relation without assigning a setting collision. No sensor threshold, catalogue completeness, fragment population, shield material or thickness, penetration limit, station design, accepted risk, maneuver threshold, or impact incident is fixed.
