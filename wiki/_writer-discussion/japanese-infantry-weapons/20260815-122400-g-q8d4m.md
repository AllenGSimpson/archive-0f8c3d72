The registry table described the Type 7 Self-Loading Rifle as a transitional ten-round weapon. The controlling Land Armaments Reference instead establishes a mature full-power service rifle with a detachable twenty-round magazine that can be charger-loaded in place. The aggregate entry was brought into line with that accepted specification.
