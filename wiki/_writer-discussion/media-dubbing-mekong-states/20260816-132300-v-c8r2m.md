The article establishes imported programs, widespread dubbing, distinct national markets, cross-border reception, archives, and Council involvement in broadcasting rights without a common broadcaster. A local version must therefore remain identifiable against the licensed source and the particular translation, recording, cuts, and release copy. The bridge supplies that chain while preserving late or damaged delivery, national discretion, and disputes over retransmission. It does not choose networks, studios, formats, censorship codes, territorial terms, or a supranational rights authority.
