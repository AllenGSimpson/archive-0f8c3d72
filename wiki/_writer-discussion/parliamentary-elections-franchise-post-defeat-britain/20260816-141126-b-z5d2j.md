The individual election pages establish dates, parties, votes, seats, and ministries, while the Commons article states single-member first-past-the-post election. They did not yet explain the authority chain from franchise and register through dissolution, writ, nomination, poll, count, return, petition, and government formation. I have used the 1918 and 1928 settlements as the inherited baseline. The 640-seat map, 1947 emergency certificates, and later results are setting canon, but the post-divergence statutes that joined them are left open. In particular, single-member constituencies do not by themselves prove abolition of every inherited business or university qualification, and the real-world post-1941 franchise and redistribution Acts have not been imported.
