Wake has no adequate natural fresh-water source, while rain catchment, storage, desalination, imported supplies, power, scheduled transport, and a changing contractor and aircrew population are established. Those facts require operators to compare reserves and production with expected demand. The passage leaves tank capacity, reserve thresholds, plant type, population, and administering department open, and makes desalination dependent upon the same fuel and spare-parts chain rather than a complete solution.
