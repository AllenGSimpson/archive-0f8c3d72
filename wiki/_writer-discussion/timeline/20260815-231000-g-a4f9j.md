The 1952–1961 Order Faith entry repeated the obsolete claim that the 1961 First Order Rite Book standardized eight life-cycle rites. I extended the entry through 1972 and separated the accepted sequence: eight rites recognized by the early 1950s, four regular great blóts fixed in the 1961 <em>Book of Feasts and Offerings</em>, and wider life-cycle standardization in the 1972 <em>Book of Life, Service, and Death</em>. I also normalized “popular teaching” to the source's “people's teaching.” The entry is marked `[a]`.
