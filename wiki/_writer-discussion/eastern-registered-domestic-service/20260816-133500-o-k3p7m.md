I separated registered domestic service from the corporate service estate because the source corpus gives it a distinct employer base, transfer procedure, and problem of concealed household authority. I have treated registration as an administrative restraint and a security measure, not as emancipation. The record establishes neither uniform qualifying periods nor the effectiveness of complaints and transfers. A colonial-social review should test whether the page still gives official registration more evidentiary weight than workers' dependence, especially in cases of physical and sexual abuse.
