The page treated the consolidated 1985–2000 chronology as uniformly direct canon. The later transcript directly fixes the broad sequence—Reagan's opening and crash, Harwood's victory, the 1993 five-party schism, the contingent presidential order, corporate sponsorship, and Hale's term—but much of the statutory naming, exact vote arithmetic, purge mechanics, and the Three Clocks formulation originated as connective construction. I retained the sequence, marked working electoral totals as such, and restored the direct/incorporated distinction.
