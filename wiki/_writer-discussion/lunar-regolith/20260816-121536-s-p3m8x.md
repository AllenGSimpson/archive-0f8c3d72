Prepared pads, roads, foundations, excavation, shielding, and machine wear are established, but the page did not connect them to geotechnical practice. This addition supplies the chain from site survey and in-situ testing through controlled fill placement, contact-load and stability checks, commissioning measurements, plume-resistant surface preparation, stand-off, and maintenance. It deliberately distinguishes low static weight from bearing, settlement, sliding, overturning, transient, and plume-ejecta cases. It assigns no soil parameters, footing size, allowable pressure, compaction standard, pad recipe, exhaust loading, or inspection interval.
