Mercer's balance-sheet boundaries identify genuine monetary disciplines, but they do not identify one institution that makes every investment decision. I have located borrower knowledge at the designated bank, long-horizon appraisal at the Imperial Development Bank, complementarity and priority judgments in sector ministries and the Economic Board, public guarantees in fiscal and cabinet government, and monetary eligibility and liquidity at the Bank of Japan.

Patience remains defensible only when the weak claim and the institution carrying its loss are visible. The canon leaves the later Policy Board, statutory amendments, branch network, discount chronology, and exact inspection procedure open, so the response describes established functions rather than inventing a final committee or rule.
