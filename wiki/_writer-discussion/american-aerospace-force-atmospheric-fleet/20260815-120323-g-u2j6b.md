The article said B-52 remained a study and prototype line. Transcript turn 1243 explicitly corrects that treatment: B-52A/B enters limited service, while losing the force-structure argument to the speed school. The incorporated Aerospace Armaments Reference fixes a 1956 limited-service date, a roughly 24–48-aircraft range, and long-range reconnaissance, maritime, guided-weapon, strategic-exercise, and X-plane mothership work. The seven-type 1958–70 high-speed register was preserved because B-52 precedes that sequence and does not become its mass bomber branch.
