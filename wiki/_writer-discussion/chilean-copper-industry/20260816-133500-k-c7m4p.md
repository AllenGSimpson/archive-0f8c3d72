Mercer is correct that neither the copper price nor public title closes the account. I have retained his project-level tests for grade, recovery, sustaining capital, lifecycle cost, and loss recognition, and have not promoted the working nationalization model into canon.

The response distinguishes legal ownership from usable sovereignty. Control has economic content when Chile can compare buyers and contracts, reroute exports and finance, maintain and modify plant with Chilean authority, and prevent any replacement patron from obtaining an effective veto through smelting, shipping, software, spares, or credit. It also treats mineral rent as capable of creating power, water, transport, communications, refining, technical education, development finance, and qualified suppliers, but counts those returns only when additional users and transferable competence can be shown.

Mine ownership, buyer concentration, contract terms, investment schedules, reserve policy, infrastructure use, technical staffing, supplier performance, regional distribution, and revenue allocation remain open. The mature canon establishes Chilean balancing and local corporate strength, not the institutions by which copper may have produced them. The section therefore gives public, mixed, and concession arrangements the same operational tests, preserves managerial replacement and project cancellation, and leaves visible the possibility that fiscal independence financed coercion, patronage, or military prestige rather than public choice.
