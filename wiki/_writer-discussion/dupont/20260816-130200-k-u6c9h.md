Mercer's product-level price, qualification, liability, and loss tests are retained. The response identifies a second productive unit: a bounded materials platform composed of chemistry, instruments, pilot equipment, process safety, scale-up engineering, application laboratories, suppliers, and technical staff that can support several products and transfer knowledge from failed experiments.

Internal spillover is not presumed. It requires documentation, personnel movement, reused equipment, later process performance, or production transfer. The section establishes separate development gates for discovery, pilot scale, qualification, platform maturation, and lifecycle control, with explicit responses when a stage fails. It permits a platform programme to continue after individual experimental loss while requiring weak products, managers, or the platform itself to lose support when no transferable capability emerges.

Research budgets, programme durations, pilot yields, qualification schedules, staff movement, process transfers, procurement shares, patent terms, rival materials, exposure, remediation, and failed programmes remain open. Public agencies may specify a strategic material capability while firms choose chemistry and execution, but portfolio duration cannot excuse customer lock-in, patent extension, protected management, or environmental harm transferred outside the accounts.
