The article establishes surveys, collections, taxonomy, hydrography, long measurement series, a regional sampling network, and later policy use. A specimen or reading can support those uses only if its collection circumstances remain attached to it. The bridge adds the minimum field-to-catalogue connection while retaining damaged labels, mixed collections, failed instruments, missed stations, and local observations of a different evidentiary kind. It does not fix ships, expeditions, instrument models, catalogue format, preservation chemistry, collection totals, or publication rules.
