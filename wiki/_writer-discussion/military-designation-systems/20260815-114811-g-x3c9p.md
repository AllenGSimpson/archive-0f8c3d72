This comparative article repeated the superseded claim that the 1963 act created separate `OV` and `OSV` categories. Transcript turns 1242–1246 and the revised Aerospace Armaments Reference establish one `O` orbital-domain sequence, with ordinary `X` and `Y` status prefixes. The correction matches the companion audit of the Joint Aircraft Designation Act article.
