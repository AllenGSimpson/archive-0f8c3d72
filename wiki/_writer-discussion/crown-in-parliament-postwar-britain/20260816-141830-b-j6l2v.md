The former page explained the three-part constitutional formula and the 1947 transfer of ministry, but not the sequence by which a programme became binding law. I have expanded it around bill source and type, drafting, both Houses, financial initiative, Parliament Act procedure, assent, commencement, delegated legislation, adjudication, and territorial reach. The Parliament Act 1911 and Statute of Westminster 1931 are treated as inherited law. Later real-world Parliament Acts, assent reforms, statutory-instrument consolidation, and devolution settlements remain open because they postdate divergence. The distinction between a shared sovereign and separate legislative capacities is carried through Dominion, colonial, protected-state, and Commonwealth-agreement sections.
