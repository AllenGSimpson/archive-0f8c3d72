List of Countries

The 1985 world is organized through five recognized systems—the Einheitspakt, GEACPS or Dai Tōa Kyōeiken, the less-formal Latin Bloc, the British Commonwealth, and the American Hemisphere—alongside armed-neutral and independent frontier states. The tables distinguish direct rule, protectorates, nominal sovereignty, and genuine association.

Einheitspakt

The German-centered system is generally recognized as the Einheitspakt. The name covers radically unequal relationships: the Reich and direct colonies, formal Reichsprotektorate, nominally sovereign economies integrated like protectorates, and genuinely sovereign associated states.

GEACPS / Dai Tōa Kyōeiken

The Japanese system is generally called the Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere or GEACPS in English-language diplomatic and strategic writing. Dai Tōa Kyōeiken is normal in Japanese, ideological, ceremonial, and intra-sphere contexts. Both names describe the same hierarchical system.

Latin Bloc

The Latin Bloc is the ordinary de-facto designation. Italy, Spain, Portugal, Occitania, and subordinate Mediterranean states develop clearing, defense, Catholic-diplomatic, and procurement institutions only gradually; the system is much less formalized at first than the Einheitspakt or GEACPS.

American Hemisphere

The American Hemisphere is a sufficiently established name for Washington's claimed regional order, but it is not a uniform alliance.
