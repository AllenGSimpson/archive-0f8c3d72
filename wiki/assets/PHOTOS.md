# Fictional setting photographs

These images are user-supplied visual canon for the *Fractured Peace* alternate-history setting. They are used as in-universe archival photographs rather than documentary images of real events.

| File | In-universe identification |
| --- | --- |
| `Hitlers1959.jpg` | Last public photograph of Adolf Hitler, pictured with Eva Braun and their son Wolfgang in 1959 |
| `Wolfgang1965.jpg` | First official photograph of Wolfgang Friedrich Hitler as Führer in late 1965 |
| `Katherina1965.png` | Katharina Elisabeth Hartmann at eighteen, before meeting Wolfgang |
| `HitlerWedding1970.png` | Wolfgang and Katharina following their 1970 wedding |
| `Hitlers family1985.png` | Wolfgang, Katharina, and their seven children in the official 1985 family portrait |
| `VonBraunWolfgangModel1957.png` | Wernher von Braun showing ten-year-old Wolfgang an A9/A10-derived crewed-flight model after the success of *Raumbote I* in 1957 |
| `HitlerVonBraunLowerVolgaFacility1975.png` | Wolfgang and von Braun at the Lower Volga facility east of Adolfstadt shortly before the launch that finalized commissioning of the *Orbitaler Raumhafen* |

Additional setting photographs may be appended to this archive as they become available.
