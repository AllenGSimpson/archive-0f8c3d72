Chronology · 1939–2005

Setting Timeline

This chronology records the major events that shape the setting from the altered Second World War through the World Market Boom, Open Door Depression, five-party American republic, and mature network age of 2005. It emphasizes turning points rather than reproducing the full master timeline.

The long war, 1939–1947

1939

War begins in Europe Canon

Germany invades Poland. Britain and France declare war; the United States remains outside the conflict.

10 Jul.–31 Oct. 1940

First Battle of Britain Historical campaign; alternate legacy

Fighter Command denies the Luftwaffe air superiority. The victory creates Britain's lasting belief that survival will eventually bring strategic reversal.

22 Jun. 1941

The German–Soviet War begins Historical opening; alternate outcome

Operation Barbarossa opens the decisive continental theater. The later absence of American belligerency and a successful western front allows Germany to sustain the eastern war through 1945.

1941–42

Japan strikes south—but not east Canon

The Imperial Limited-War Directive authorizes attacks on British and Dutch possessions while excluding Pearl Harbor, the Philippines, Guam, and Wake. The Malayan Campaign, fall of Singapore, and East Indies conquest secure the resource empire.

21 Jun. 1942

Wewelsburg Address on the Eternal Chain Canon

Himmler privately links Wralda, cosmic order, ancestry, descendants, duty, and the future inner SS confession without yet producing a settled theology.

1941–47

The American Spectator War Canon

Americans experience the conflict through radio, newsreels, relief, volunteers, maps, and political argument without belligerency, mass mobilization, or a common veteran generation.

1943–44

China becomes Japan's main war again Canon

The Chongqing Operation and enlarged Ichi-Go campaign isolate organized Chinese resistance after the Limited Southern War secures a deliberately short perimeter.

c. 1943–44

Operation Torch ends at Algiers Canon event; date open

Britain's Algerian bridgehead is isolated by Vichy, Italian, and German reinforcement and Axis interdiction. Harold Alexander surrenders the expedition in the Algiers Capitulation.

1945

Germany breaks the Soviet western system Canon

The decisive eastern offensives destroy rail, command, agricultural, and political depth. Moscow, Leningrad, and Stalingrad fall, are isolated, or cease to function as viable strategic centers.

Late 1945–early 1946

Stalin is removed and executed Canon

An emergency party-state conspiracy replaces Stalin's personal rule with a collective survival leadership of Malenkov, Molotov, Beria, Zhukov, and Khrushchev.

1946

The Ural settlement ends the eastern war Canon

Soviet sovereignty ends west of approximately the Urals. Germany accepts the limit of its sustainable logistics while the defeated government receives time and permission to carry movable state capacity east.

1946

People and industry move beyond the Urals Canon

Factories are dismantled; machines, rolling stock, archives, stores, formations, specialists, and civilians move by rail, road, cart, and foot into the successor east.

1946 onward

The Soviet east fragments Canon framework

Industrial administrations, military enclaves, party-state remnants, refugee governments, and regional successors inherit serious Soviet institutions without one uncontested capital.

Spring–21 Jun. 1945

Order Faith synthesis begins Canon

Hitler orders a coherent SS confession and fixed boundary among doctrine, speculation, and superstition. The Wewelsburg Working Commission convenes on 21 June.

12 Apr. 1945

James F. Byrnes succeeds Roosevelt Canon

The succession president inherits American nonbelligerency, gradually narrows New Deal emergency administration, and prepares the civilian republic for the fractured peace.

1946–47

The Second Battle of Britain Canon

Jets, submarines, cipher reform, and long-range strikes exhaust British depth. A final authorized London sortie carries a locally substituted food-and-medicine load beneath white cargo parachutes; only afterward does Berlin create the Friedensbrot myth.

The settlement, 1947–1950

1942–1947

Japanese East Indies administration Canon

Separate army and naval commands govern Sumatra, Java and Madura, and Borneo and the eastern islands as resource zones. Japanese firms assume custody of plantations, oil, mines, banks, ports, shipping, railways, and utilities before any successor government is sovereign.

1947–1955

The fifteen Southern Archipelago states receive recognition Canon

Sunda, Mataram, East Java and Madura, four Sumatran states, two Bornean states, two Sulawesi states, Bali, Lombok–Sumbawa, the Eastern Lesser Sunda Federation, and the Moluccan Federation inherit sovereignty after Japanese corporate concessions are embedded.

1945–47

Japan's late Pacific expansion Canon framework

As British, French, and Australian oceanic systems lose coherent defense, Japan acquires the Bismarcks, Solomons, Fiji, New Caledonia, French Polynesia, and other islands beyond its original short perimeter.

1945–47

Sihanouk anchors protected Cambodia Canon framework

Japan removes the remaining French authority but backs King Norodom Sihanouk and Cambodia's Buddhist monarchy over a wholly revolutionary administration. The political crown survives without the historical 1955 abdication.

Early 1947

American aid to Britain ends Canon

Washington concludes that further support can prolong resistance but cannot restore victory. The termination removes food, fuel, spares, machine tools, shipping support, and the confidence that Britain can wait for rescue.

1947

The Lisbon Conference Canon

Portugal mediates the peace. Britain remains sovereign but loses its Mediterranean chain, Asian possessions, and strategic control of Suez. Germany, Italy, and Japan receive recognition of empires already made.

1947 onward

The Cape–Ceylon–Australia chain replaces the lost eastern artery Canon

Britain retains the Seychelles, the Maldives, Ceylon, Indian Ocean Christmas Island and the Cocos Islands. Ports, airfields, weather, rescue, radio, and naval support connect the Cape to Australia without Suez, Singapore, or Malaya.

4 Feb. 1948

Ceylon becomes a Commonwealth realm and Indian Ocean fortress Canon framework; ministry details open

Dominion government and Crown continuity coexist with Colombo's theater headquarters, Trincomalee's fleet base, and a growing airfield network. Ceylon becomes the constitutional and logistical core of Bastion India.

1947 onward

South West Africa remains unsettled Canon framework; exact instrument open

South Africa continues to administer the former German colony. Pretoria seeks effective incorporation, Berlin preserves a revisionist colonial claim, Britain protects the Cape balance, and no postwar institution conclusively resolves the mandate.

1945–1950

Italian postwar reconstruction begins Canon

Giuseppe Volpi supplies political sponsorship and Donato Menichella the technical architecture for the Imperial Stabilization of the Lira. Blocked balances, long reconstruction bonds, productive credit, imperial commodity purchases, and the IRI holding system convert victory liabilities into a durable economic order.

1947

Purchase of Gibraltar Canon

Italy cancels a major share of Britain's settlement debt if London legally sells the Rock to Spain, creating Franco's greatest prestige victory and the British “sold Rock” grievance.

1947

National Maritime Program Canon

Spain begins with merchant shipping, steel, power, ports, yards, finance, and training before attempting construction of a first-rank fleet.

1939–47

The Iberian Pact expands Canon framework

The 1939 Spanish–Portuguese agreement becomes a durable system of consultation, border security, Atlantic defense, intelligence, trade, and Catholic diplomacy without political union.

1947–1951

First Far Eastern Republic Canon framework; dates working

Japan converts occupation committees east of Lake Baikal into a federal Russian buffer. Chita is the capital and Khabarovsk the operating center, but commanders, territories, former Soviet officials, White émigrés, and Japanese missions retain divided practical authority.

1947

Danube and Balkan Settlement Canon framework

Yugoslavia is abolished and divided. Hungary takes northern districts, Bulgaria takes Macedonia, Italy dominates the Adriatic and southern protectorates, and reduced Serbia becomes the principal overlap between Italian sovereignty and German infrastructure.

1947–49

Kongo-Gesamtwerk is selected Canon

Germany creates Mittelafrika, seizes Belgian Congo-development archives, forms the Kongo-Ausbaukommission, and selects the maximal full-basin plan over narrower Inga and Lower Congo alternatives.

Aug.–Oct. 1947

Operation Homeward Canon

German and Italian ships return British prisoners under Royal Navy escort. The staged Homecoming Fleet and an edited sergeant's remark intensify the election-era dispute over wartime truth.

6 Nov. 1947

White Parachute Election Canon

A five-week campaign produces no majority. George VI commissions Aneurin Bevan's minority Reconstruction Ministry, sustained for urgent business by the informal Reconstruction Truce.

Late 1947

Japan changes from victory to settlement government Canon

Tōjō resigns honorably to chair the Imperial Defense Council. Yonai forms a short Settlement Cabinet and redirects public duty from conquest to construction.

Late 1947

The Siegeskind is born Canon

Wolfgang Friedrich Hitler becomes the public symbol of victory and the eventual focus of a succession the regime denies is hereditary.

1947–48

Conquest intoxication in the east Canon

SS commands treat victory as a demographic blank slate. Removals, requisitions, competing jurisdictions, and theatrical estate rule destroy local knowledge, reduce production, and expand resistance.

1948

Windsor Arrangement Canon

Edward, Duke of Windsor, may return part-time for controlled family, veterans', and prisoner-rehabilitation duties in exchange for permanent exclusion from the succession, constitutional office, partisan endorsement, and any rival court.

1948–49

Costa Rica establishes the civilian republic Historical framework retained

Civil conflict gives way to a new Constitution, independent electoral institutions, and abolition of the standing army. The settlement becomes Costa Rica's principal defense against the security-state politics of the surrounding corridor.

21 Dec. 1947

First Wewelsburg Articles Canon

The founding Order Faith confession joins divine Order, struggle, form, blood, Volk, leadership, and SS guardianship into one authoritative chain.

1948

Reconstruction and Imperial Financial Rectification Canon

Germany begins the Reichswährungsbereinigung. Shigemitsu becomes Japanese premier; the New Yen Settlement protects small balances, converts wartime money and debt, and creates the layered yen system.

2 Nov. 1948

Dewey wins the four-way American election Canon; totals open

Thomas E. Dewey and Earl Warren defeat Byrnes-aligned Recovery Democrats, Progressive–New Deal–Labor, and National Renewal. The Republican victory ends Recovery Democratic government without restoring the old two-party equilibrium.

1949–53

First Greater East Asia Reconstruction Plan Canon framework

Merchant shipping, petroleum, ports, coal and steel, power, railways, food and fertilizer, Japanese housing, machine tools, and technical education turn victory institutions into a functioning corporate developmental empire.

1946–49

Rival computing schools acquire institutions Canon framework; historical foundations

Alan Turing and Maurice Wilkes establish the NPL and Cambridge poles of British computing. German computing grows from protected programs under Konrad Zuse and Helmut Schreyer, security files, cryptanalysis, and rocket control. Alonzo Church, John von Neumann, and Grace Hopper connect American theory, architecture, and commercial translation. Akira Nakashima's relay-circuit theory becomes an indigenous Japanese foundation.

1948–52

General Registration and the Counting Wars Canon

Census coercion, translation failure, clearances, massacres, early Kongowerk surveys, and refugee flight reveal the failure of direct German registration across Mittelafrika.

1949–52

Eastern quota crisis Canon

Berlin's grain, timber, coal, oil, transport, and security demands force the SS to restore useful native specialists, discipline incompetent commanders, and replace arbitrary predation with legible colonial obligations.

1949–53

Kongowerke AG opens the basin Canon

Survey roads, quarries, camps, preliminary Inga works, registration districts, and clearance corridors make the infrastructure project and colonial census institutionally inseparable.

1948–49

The Breaking of the Raj Canon

All-Indian command dissolves amid communal wars, princely mobilization, and foreign intervention. Subhas Chandra Bose carries Azad Hind into Calcutta while Jawaharlal Nehru becomes the executive center of the Congress claimant. Operation Tideway overloads the British rear area in Burma.

1948–52

Azad Hind consolidates the east Canon

The preexisting Provisional Government makes Calcutta its administrative capital, establishes an autonomous East Bengal administration, controls Assam through its strategic corridor, and integrates Orissa as its principal southern depth and resource base.

1948–53

Burmese Companion War Canon

Britain's protected Rangoon government, frontier and nationality armies, radical Bamar forces, and Japanese-backed nationalists fight over the collapsing rear area. Japan ultimately brokers transfer rather than inheriting a destroyed country.

1952–53

Rangoon Settlement Canon

A package of agreements ends British protection and creates an asymmetric Union of Burma: one international state, a Bamar core, seven constituent nationality governments, nested autonomous districts, and an armed federal division of forces.

1947–49

The princely sovereign rush Canon framework

With paramountcy gone, courts, chiefships, restored dynasties, and local commanders assert sovereignty. Rapid annexations eliminate the weakest, while the surviving states begin building regional durbars and a revived Chamber of Princes.

1947–49

Western Succession Crisis Canon

Afghanistan occupies Chagai and Quetta-Pishin, Sindh secures Karachi, Kalat declares independence, an Iranian-backed Baloch confederation forms, and Pakistan's emergency government takes shape around Sibi.

1949–55

First Baloch Wars Canon framework

Kalat, Balochistan, Afghanistan, and Pakistan contest Las Bela, Makran, Kachhi, Chagai, and Quetta while Sindh arms the shifting balance.

1949–50

The cold peace takes shape Canon

British planning returns to the Cape route. Germany builds a differentiated imperial administration and directed imperial market, while Japan administers its sphere through its own hierarchy of legal categories.

15 Aug. 1950

Assam–Tibet earthquake Historical disaster; political effects reconstructed

A magnitude 8.6 earthquake devastates eastern Tibet and the Brahmaputra basin, isolating garrisons and interrupting Azad Hind's early consolidation of the Assam corridor.

1949–51

Dewey restores the American gold dollar Working dates and parity

The Dewey administration reopens convertibility in stages at a working $35 per fine troy ounce, creating the first major hard reserve claim of the fractured postwar order.

1947–49

Release, resettlement, and the Brick Corps Canon

Demobilized personnel enter training, placement, housing, and public-work programs. The National Reconstruction Corps reaches roughly 250,000–300,000 direct workers at its 1949 peak.

1948

Merchant Fleet Renewal Act Canon

Under the slogan “Ships are bread,” public finance and private operation renew Britain's commercial lifeline while the Commonwealth Standard Ship Programme distributes construction among participating yards.

1949

Arsenal continuity becomes law Canon

Parliament preserves minimum capacity in shipyards, aircraft, signals, escorts, submarines, auxiliaries, and ammunition against demands for complete demobilization.

1950

Health and family security settlement Canon

Universal essential care and family guarantees pass through a plural compromise joining public finance to independent doctors and voluntary, municipal, religious, and charitable institutions.

1947–51

The Brick Parliament rebuilds Britain Canon

Shifting majorities demobilize the armed forces, employ the Brick Corps, renew merchant shipping, preserve strategic yards, and establish health security.

1950

Commonwealth Council of Defence and Reconstruction Canon

Parliament creates a Crown-chaired advisory council that records Dominion, service, shipping, financial, and technical warnings without gaining command or appropriation powers.

Postwar era

National Commonwealth Corridor Canon system; project chronology open

Ottawa coordinates east–west rail, roads, pipelines, refining, ports, communications, and strategic stores so Canadian grain, energy, minerals, and industry retain Canadian and Commonwealth routes beside the larger American market.

1947–55

Industrial conversion, repair, and surplus motorization Canon

Surviving combines and workshops redirect wartime capacity while released trucks, improvised conversions, remanufacturing, dealers, and local mechanics supply reconstruction. A replacement crisis begins as the oldest fleets wear out.

1950–mid-1950s

Postwar motor industries take their durable form Canon framework; company instruments open

Volkswagen settles KdF claims through purchase certificates, Auto Union remains intact in Saxony, and Borgward serves the independent middle and commercial market. In America, Nash–Hudson becomes American Motors, while Studebaker–Packard and Kaiser–Willys adopt specialist paths outside the Big Three.

1965–1975

Protected competition produces distinct national vehicle schools Canon framework

The German industry turns toward front-wheel drive, diesel, safety, turbocharging, and bounded rotary experiments; American independents normalize practical compacts, utility vehicles, and specialist engineering. Borgward undergoes managed reorganization while Volkswagen begins its break from one rear-engined car.

1947–late 1960s

Japanese settlement transforms Taiwan Canon framework

Approximately four to five million Japanese and Ryukyuan citizens settle through veteran grants, corporate migration, and ordinary internal movement. The Government-General administers the first phase while the integration settlement gives way to citizenship, Diet representation, prefectures, and ministry government. Taihoku becomes the commanding metropolis and the Shinchiku corridor a major technical region.

1947–55

South Seas territorial reorganization Canon framework

Japan creates associated Papua New Guinea from the whole main island while placing most smaller Pacific acquisitions under four directly governed South Seas administrations.

1947–55

National China enters the age of pacification Canon

Nanjing claims one Republican diplomatic identity while Japanese support and provincial armies defeat the largest Communist bases. Peace, railway markets, and predictable local administration become the first sources of postwar legitimacy.

Consolidation, 1951–1961

1951–early 1950s

Bevan falls; the emergency works system contracts Canon

The Reconstruction Truce ends with defeat of Bevan's budget and the 1951 general election. As councils and firms resume ordinary construction, the shrinking Brick Corps leaves a smaller permanent Royal Civil Works Service.

1948–49

National motor racing returns Canon framework

Hill climbs, airfield circuits, endurance events, and revived Grands Prix restore the industrial culture that later feeds racing technology into civilian production.

1950

The FIA sporting secretariat moves to Geneva Canon framework

Germany and Italy provide the central postwar bargain, while neutral technical officials create an inspection and arbitration system acceptable to British and continental entrants.

1951

International Grand Prix Cup Canon event; calendar open

A provisional competition tests the formula, inspections, transport, and arbitration used by the first world-championship season.

1952

The Formula One World Championship begins Canon

Mercedes-Benz and Auto Union and the Alfa Romeo–Ferrari–Maserati field form the founding industrial core. Drivers’ and manufacturers’ championships are awarded from the beginning.

1951–1954

Far Eastern Federal Crisis Canon framework; dates working

Cabinet collapse, provincial refusal, Manchukuoan territorial demands, Chita–Khabarovsk rivalry, and repeated Japanese arbitration discredit the republican constitution and produce the monarchist proposal.

1952–56

Delhi Campaign and Azad Hind's Lost Victory Canon

Azad Hind advances west through Bihar and the Ganges routes, reaching its high-water mark in 1955. The Burmese Federal Crisis breaks the Burma–Assam ammunition corridor; stocks run down, a final 1956 offensive fails, and Congress drives the army back toward its eastern depots.

After 1956

Azad Hind begins munitions independence Canon framework

Calcutta prioritizes cartridges, mortar bombs, artillery and tank shells, propellant, primers, barrels, tracks, and engine parts so that another broken foreign corridor cannot silence the army. Japan assists while retaining control of advanced vehicles, optics, engines, and designs.

Oct. 1951

The India Election Canon

Conservatives become the largest party without a majority. R. A. Butler replaces Aneurin Bevan through the Commonwealth Supply Agreement with Union Commonwealth.

4 Nov. 1952

Dewey is narrowly reelected Canon; totals open

Charles Lindbergh carries Minnesota for National Renewal, giving the party its first electoral votes and its durable belief that it is early rather than marginal.

6 Feb. 1952

George VI dies Historical date; alternate legacy

Elizabeth II inherits a Crown strengthened by her father's wartime endurance, constitutional refusal during the White Parachute crisis, and postwar Commonwealth convening role.

2 Jun. 1953

Coronation of Elizabeth II Canon

The coronation demonstrates that defeat did not settle Britain's status. Delegations from every great power attend below head-of-state level where security or protocol requires it; Edward returns to the family observances but not the Abbey or Crown.

1 Aug. 1953

Federation of Rhodesia and Nyasaland Historical date; alternate survival

Southern Rhodesian administration, Northern Rhodesian copper, and Nyasaland's labor and geography enter a common federal system. German rule in the Congo turns the official development union into the Commonwealth's inland defensive barrier and prevents its historical 1963 dissolution.

1950s–1970s

The New Elizabethans take form Canon framework

Defeat, reconstruction, the Coronation, technical education, the German satellite shock, Commonwealth recovery, and Kuwait form a cross-party Coronation Generation centered on skill, property, Crown, and strategic independence.

20 Oct. 1955

Butler wins a parliamentary majority Canon

Conservatives take 326 of 640 seats, ending formal dependence on Union Commonwealth and beginning Butler's second ministry without dismantling the Contract Commonwealth.

6 Nov. 1956–Jan. 1957

The House selects Earl Warren Canon; ballot open

No candidate wins an Electoral College majority after Lindbergh and National Renewal carry enough upper-Midwestern states to fracture the result. State delegations choose Warren, giving him constitutional title but a damaged mandate.

c. 1950–51

Amman Settlement Working canon

The main Palestine War ends with a reduced coastal Israel, an enlarged Jordan holding Jerusalem and most Arab Palestine, holy-place guarantees, and continuing British security and transit relationships.

1951–54

Second Israeli–Jordanian War Canon framework; operations open

The assassination of Abdullah I destabilizes the Amman order. Israel gains western Jerusalem, a more defensible road corridor, and small frontier corrections, but Jordan and the broad territorial settlement survive.

Early–mid 1950s

Proposed Straits Security Settlement Working canon

Turkey preserves formal independence by balancing Germany and Italy while considering passage, observer, technical, and economic privileges and excluding Anglo-American military missions.

1953–59

Kongostadt and the early Mosaic Order Canon

The Inga-Werke advance, the practical capital moves to high ground, and the Language and Mediation Service, Tribal Cadastre, and recognized communities establish the early Mosaic Order.

1955–56

African Refugee and Frontier Act Canon

Refugee testimony from Mittelafrika creates an international scandal. The Butler government joins Commonwealth relief to controlled reception districts, permits, work schemes, territorial resettlement, and legally selective frontier closure.

1955–56

Commonwealth African Reserve System Canon framework

Canadian, Australian, New Zealand, British, South African, and territorial supplies move through Atlantic, Cape, and Indian Ocean ports. Relief depots, transport yards, clinics, and communications also strengthen the Commonwealth mobilization system.

1956

No Empty Flagstaff Doctrine Canon

The Butler government declares that Britain will not withdraw before a recognized local government, reliable security institutions, defensible borders, excluded hostile bases, continuing Commonwealth access, and protection against immediate rival occupation exist.

1957

Accra Compact Canon

Britain creates the Associated Crown State: real domestic self-government and local citizenship under continuing Crown sovereignty or binding Commonwealth control of defense and alignment. The Gold Coast becomes the first model, Nigeria federalizes, and Southern Cameroons becomes a frontier territory.

From 1957

Southern Cameroons becomes a Crown Frontier Territory Canon framework

A separate assembly, citizenship, constabulary, and local government coexist with British external defense, air, signals, and intelligence facilities opposite German Cameroon and Kribi.

1958–60

East African common services expand Canon framework

The East African High Commission develops into a common authority for transport, aviation, disease control, strategic reserves, refugee registration, frontier intelligence, and security procurement while Kenya, Uganda, and Tanganyika retain separate governments and constitutional paths.

Late 1950s

Salisbury Understanding Canon framework

London tolerates broad Rhodesian frontier discretion in exchange for quiet borders, moving copper and rail traffic, Crown loyalty, and restraint against unauthorized cross-border war.

1959–69

Malebo closure and MOSAIK Canon

The Malebo-Sperrwerk closes in stages, the reservoir spreads into the western Cuvette, and paper and punch-card administration becomes the linked MOSAIK system. Wolfgang fixes the final operating band near 330–335 metres.

1952–61

The Order Faith becomes daily practice Canon

Full hereditary office requires confession from 1952; the 1953 Wewelsburg Rescript on Sacrifice regulates blót; the 1956 recension organizes popular, Order, and inner teaching; and the First Order Rite Book standardizes eight life-cycle rites in 1961.

1951

The Great Inventory Canon

German survey teams measure mines, harvests, railways, ports, labor, power, currencies, and industrial capacity. The Reich Office for Raw-Material Economy and Reich Goods Equalization assume recognizable form.

1952

Imperial clearing is regularized Canon

The Verrechnungsmark becomes the German sphere's principal noncash clearing unit while Einheitsmark and currency-board arrangements discipline tighter protectorates.

1952–58

The Eastern estate order is codified Canon

Conditional hereditary tenure, graded status, controlled councils, service privilege, regulated commands, and enterprise-linked urban standing turn emergency compromise into a durable colonial law.

Early 1950s

National Examination Settlement Canon

The Examination Yuan establishes classical-administrative, practical-service, and technical-professional tracks, creating credentials recognized across the Chinese provinces.

Late 1950s

The Chinese Classical Settlement Canon

Nanjing expands the Five-Yuan order, classical publishing, public rites, examination ceremony, temples, ancestral institutions, and a Republican claim to imperial civilizational inheritance.

1957–64

The American Rocket Awakening Canon

Germany's first artificial satellite confronts Warren's administration with a technical legitimacy crisis and triggers an expansion of science education, technical media, engineering prestige, and Kennedy's public mission politics.

8 Nov. 1960

Kennedy defeats Warren and Lindbergh Canon; totals open

John F. Kennedy wins a popular plurality and clear electoral majority in a televised three-way race. Aerospace purpose, hemispheric renewal, and constitutional activism end twelve years of Republican presidential government.

1957–58

Silent Overflight and High Mast Canon

Raumbote I makes orbit appear to Britain as undefended access. The Commonwealth answers in 1958 with the Commonwealth Space and Signals Board, a distributed tracking network, and authorization of independent launch work.

From 1958

The Indian Ocean route becomes a telemetry chain Canon framework

Ceylon and Indian Ocean stations supply equatorial telemetry and hand observations between southern Africa and Australia. Maritime weather and radio sites acquire orbital tracking, timing, communications, and later crew-safety functions.

1957

Sarit displaces Phibun Canon

Plaek Phibunsongkhram's Victory Regime gives way to the Sarit–Bhumibol settlement: military authoritarianism, royal development, and differentiated rule without surrendering Thailand's wartime gains.

1957–65

Japan builds a practical satellite service Canon

Hideo Itokawa helps turn the Raumbote shock into a civil program. A 1961 failure precedes Ōsumi I in 1962, Kuroshio I in 1964, and Yamabiko I in 1965; the temporary council becomes the Imperial Space Development Agency.

1944–52

Plankalkül becomes Rechenplan 52 Canon

Zuse’s typed plan notation is normalized for cards and tape, then becomes Germany’s first practical high-level programming specification.

1949–55

Stored-program and electronic computing enter regular service Canon framework; historical foundations

Cambridge EDSAC serves university users from 1949, Turing's ACE program expands at the NPL, the Princeton IAS machine operates by 1952, the Schreyer–Telefunken line enters limited German production, and Hopper's 1953 A-0-class system makes automatic translation a practical American commercial program.

1952–60

The first high-level programming-language families Canon

Rechenplan 56, Common Autocode, American FORTRAN, COBOL, and Lisp, Japanese KEISAN and TEJUN, and Italian LIA turn machine orders into durable scientific, symbolic, and administrative source cultures.

1958–62

American Aerospace Command becomes an independent service Canon

The satellite shock produces a unified strategic and orbital command in 1958. Kennedy elevates it to the United States Aerospace Force in 1962.

Feb. 1959

The winter-tour charter is cancelled Canon

A worsening weather warning keeps Buddy Holly, Ritchie Valens, and J. P. Richardson on the ground. Their continuing careers alter American rock, and no “Day the Music Died” enters popular memory.

1951–53

Philippine Independence Assistance Program Canon

Under the Siam Formula, Japanese scholarships, publishing, political money, radio, legal relief, trade, and later covert arms prepare a nationalist challenge to American tutelage.

Feb.–Mar. 1953

The Davao Papers Canon

A lorry accident at Panabo exposes Japanese technicians, armored components, inventories, and a draft association treaty. Raids, arrests, and emergency powers follow.

4 July 1953

The Luneta shootings Canon

Security forces fire during Manila's Independence Day crisis. Army and constabulary defections transform the scandal into full nationalist revolt; casualty totals and the first responsible unit remain open.

1946

Pius XII consecrates Russia Canon

The act is little known inside the occupied east, yet an unexplained Orthodox revival follows across Russian lands and Caucasia and reshapes Rome's Eastern policy.

1947–50

Rome surveys four clandestine currents Canon

Communist-derived, German, Italian, and Spanish networks diverge after the war while neutral Swiss channels preserve cross-bloc communications.

1954–63

Kazakh–Russian War Canon framework

The largest post-Soviet secession war produces mass expulsions, a rough north–south partition, and a northern Russian Steppe State.

1946–54

Railway Wars and Western Siberian War Canon sequence; campaign dates working

Successor governments fight for rolling stock, grain, factories, and command. Omsk and Novosibirsk fail to conquer one another and form a divided dual state.

1947

Kalashnikov’s rifle enters Novosibirsk trials Canon

A reduced successor-state competition adopts the developing AK-47 family for the Omsk–Novosibirsk system.

Late 1940s–1950s

Soviet design lineages move into India Canon

Russian successor governments cannot finance the whole evacuated establishment. Congress receives the Kartsev–Nizhny Tagil and Chelyabinsk–Kurgan branches. Hindustan receives the Morozov–Kharkiv and Mytishchi branches, Hyderabad the Kotin–Kirov and Uralmash lines, and the western states the Astrov and GAZ–Arzamas lineages.

1950s–1960s

The aircraft and naval bureaus divide southward Canon framework

MiG settles principally in Hindustan, Ilyushin in Congress India, and Yakovlev in Hyderabad. Iran hosts the main Tupolev and Sukhoi establishments and the cores of a Russian naval-design system, while Karachi develops submarine, escort, landing, and support-vessel branches.

1953

Kishi forms the Imperial Development and Security Cabinet Canon

The Davao Papers and Luneta shootings bring down Shigemitsu. Kishi sustains the Philippine revolt one degree below direct Japanese–American war.

1954–63

Kazakh–Russian War Canon framework

The largest post-Soviet secession war produces mass expulsions, a rough north–south partition, and a northern Russian Steppe State.

1955–1956

Chita Restoration Canon framework

The Far Eastern Zemsky Sobor elects Michael Andreevich Romanov as sovereign. The Fundamental Law creates a geographically limited federal monarchy whose domestic Russian institutions coexist with Japanese strategic reserved powers.

1955–1965

The Mikhail aviation settlement Canon framework

A national civil air service, flying schools, weather and radio organization, medical and postal aviation, and licensed local aircraft assembly turn aviation into the monarchy's prestige public service.

1955–1985

The northern transport state matures Canon framework

Rail carries strategic bulk, water serves seasonal northern freight, aircraft connect people, roads serve developed corridors, and snow-and-swamp vehicles bridge the last mile. Japanese satellites and computing increasingly coordinate the system after 1975.

1957–68

Yenisei conflicts and the Baikal frontier Canon systems; campaign dates working

Krasnoyarsk resists western consolidation while Soviet-restorationist Irkutsk raids and destabilizes the Japanese-protected Far Eastern monarchy.

Late 1950s–early 1960s

Amur Crisis Working date

Manchukuo and the Russian Far Eastern State approach war before Japan freezes the border, guarantees Russian territory, and substitutes Manchukuoan economic access for annexation.

Jan.–Feb. 1957

Baguio Truce Canon framework

American, associated-government, and nationalist representatives establish a ceasefire and negotiating channel without yet settling sovereignty, withdrawal, or foreign alignment.

4 July 1957

Manila Accords Canon

The accords create a Government of National Reconciliation, schedule American withdrawal and installation transfers, and fix 12 June 1958 as the date of complete Philippine sovereignty.

12 June 1958

Philippine sovereignty restored Canon

American tutelage ends and fixed installations pass into Philippine title while limited evacuation and technical personnel remain temporarily.

26 June 1958

Cebu Declaration Canon

The National Assembly enters the Greater East Asia Council as a Sovereign Associated Member and receives a collective Japanese-led guarantee of territorial integrity and constitutional independence.

1959

Treaty of Cebu Canon framework

The treaty defines constitutional and religious guarantees, Philippine base sovereignty, Japanese access, military reconstruction, peso–yen clearing, development finance, and trade.

June 1960

The Independence Fleet commissions Canon

Japanese politicians describe associated governments, local technical cadres, dependable infrastructure, and corporate leadership as mature coordination. Critics identify structural capture, while scandals are disciplined as breaches of method rather than failures of the system.

By 2000

Japanese imperial hierarchy becomes ordinary infrastructure Established trajectory; later profile extrapolated

Company paternalism loosens, dual professional households and specialized care machines expand, and Japanese leadership is encountered chiefly through standards, transport, networks, and dependable services. Japanese cyberpunk turns the same functioning systems into a critique of unlocatable authority.

Early 2000s

The war-memory contest enters mass network culture Canon framework

Localized games, archive television, faction communities, and unauthorized ShadowNet campaigns carry the five incompatible settlement narratives into a shared but politically divided media market.

2005

The mature fractured peace Canon framework

Corporate-political ecosystems, private contractual services, Brightnet and ShadowNet identities, commercial orbital traffic, and enduring imperial blocs define a world more connected economically than strategically.
