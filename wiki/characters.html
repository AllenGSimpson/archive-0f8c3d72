Selected historical figures

Historical people remain central where the divergence supplies a credible altered career: Adolf Hitler, Hermann Göring, Joseph Goebbels, Heinrich Himmler, Reinhard Heydrich, Erwin Rommel, Karl Dönitz, Albert Speer, Wernher von Braun, Luigi Broglio, Michael Andreevich Romanov (Tsar Mikhail), Mikhail Kalashnikov, Joseph Stalin, Georgy Malenkov, Vyacheslav Molotov, Lavrentiy Beria, Georgy Zhukov, Nikita Khrushchev, Alan Turing, John F. Kennedy, Nelson Rockefeller, George Lincoln Rockwell, Pat Buchanan, Ronald Reagan, Charles Lindbergh, Margaret Thatcher, Harold Wilson, Umberto II, Yasuhiro Nakasone, and the Catholic leaders indexed above.

The Character Dossier deliberately excludes historical people from its original-cast register. Individual biography pages maintain that distinction by grounding pre-divergence life in the historical record and citing the setting documents that control the altered career.
