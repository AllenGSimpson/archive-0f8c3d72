# SS Order Faith Reference

## Status and authority

- **Scope:** the theology, rites, offices, membership, and historical development of the SS religion from 1941 through 1985.
- **Canonical source:** the timestamp-ordered SS Religious Development branch archived in [transcript.md](./transcript.md), principally Turns 520–569.
- **Related reference:** [National Socialist Philosophy, Metaphysics, and SS Ideology](./nationalSocialistPhilosophyMetaphysicsReference%20-%20AltWWII.md) governs the documented historical and philosophical substrate. This document governs the alternate post-divergence religion that the victorious SS constructs from that substrate.
- **Authority rule:** direct user corrections control. Proposals retained and used in later exchanges are working canon; unresolved metaphysical interpretations remain schools rather than compulsory dogma.
- **Content note:** this reference describes a racist, authoritarian religion connected to colonial rule, coerced hierarchy, eugenics, antisemitism, sacrifice, and political violence. Description is not endorsement.

## Governing summary

The SS does not begin the divergence as a church. It begins as a hereditary racial order, security apparatus, settlement authority, military institution, and ideological elite containing several connected but inconsistent religious currents.

Victory forces coherence. Hitler does not permit every estate commander to invent a private cult, and Himmler cannot govern a permanent eastern aristocracy through suggestive speeches alone. Between 1941 and 1966 the SS converts political metaphysics, ancestry law, Germanic revival, Indo-European comparison, oath culture, funerary practice, and Himmler's mysticism into an authoritative **Ordensglaube**, or **Order Faith**.

The mature faith is not Germany's established national religion. It is the confession of the inner SS governing estate. Christians may serve in affiliated or military capacities, and most native subjects remain Orthodox or Catholic, but full hereditary Order office requires initiation into the Order Faith. Its publications circulate internally, its theological language is easily presented as philosophy or political education, and outsiders—including the Vatican—cannot prove the full institutional system by 1980.

Its shortest statement of ultimate value is:

> The highest good is the preservation and fulfillment of living Order through faithful service to the inherited chain of ancestors, the living, and descendants.

## Sources and method

The faith has a braided inheritance rather than one linear genealogy. Wagner, Hegel, Marx, Blavatsky, Guido von List, Jung, Evola, Hitler, Himmler, Romantic nationalism, Ariosophy, Germanic antiquarianism, racial pseudoscience, comparative Indo-European studies, archaeology, and SS administrative practice influence different layers. No single thinker supplies the whole system.

Political religion, personal theology, public rhetoric, occult practice, philosophy of history, ritual, and institutional loyalty remain distinguishable. They also influence one another. The religion becomes coherent by arranging those layers, not by pretending they were identical from the beginning.

The mature institution distinguishes:

1. **Common teaching** for every initiated member.
2. **Order teaching** for officers, household heads, and ritual specialists.
3. **Inner interpretation** for licensed theologians, comparative scholars, and occult investigators.

Archaeology may confirm, qualify, or destabilize inherited claims. A historical discovery is not automatically revelation. The Ahnenerbe gathers evidence; doctrine offices decide what authority that evidence possesses.

## Divine order and the gods

### Wralda or the Allfather

The highest divine reality is the living ground of Order, conventionally called **Wralda**, the Ancient One, or the **Allfather**. It is not the Christian creator standing wholly outside creation. It is the conscious ordering depth through which form, life, struggle, inheritance, and history exist.

Whether Wralda possesses personality in precisely the manner of an individual god remains a permitted theological dispute. Common teaching requires that Order is more than an accidental material arrangement and that the divine is genuinely active in living reality.

### The gods

The gods are real, not merely poetic symbols. They are differentiated personal manifestations or encounters with the greater divine Order as received through particular peoples and inherited forms.

**Wotan** is the highest Germanic disclosure of sovereignty, knowledge, sacrifice, kingship, war, inspiration, and dangerous wisdom. Other Germanic gods retain distinct functions and personalities. The gods are neither independent creators of separate universes nor disposable masks with no reality of their own.

Other peoples may encounter genuine divine powers through their own inherited traditions. This does not imply religious or political equality. The faith teaches **differentiated revelation**: truth is received through the form and vocation of peoples, while the SS claims that the Germanic inheritance is the one entrusted to its own Order.

### Matter, form, and technology

The religion is not radically anti-material. Matter without proper form is lower and disorderly; matter shaped through body, blood, cultivation, craft, technology, settlement, and history may be sacred.

Technological power therefore does not disprove myth. Engineering is one means by which will gives form to matter. Spaceflight, nuclear power, prosthetics, automation, and later cybernetics become tests of whether new instruments serve inherited form or dissolve it.

## History, cycles, and Ragnarök

History moves through recurring cycles of formation, flourishing, corruption, dissolution, struggle, and renewal. **Ragnarök** has three related meanings:

- a cosmic truth that no created order is immune from crisis and dissolution;
- a historical pattern through which peoples repeatedly face decisive ordeals;
- an ultimate mythic horizon whose literal timing is not defined.

The faith does not teach that every technological advance postpones Ragnarök or that modernity makes it impossible. A civilization can be technologically brilliant and spiritually disordered. Nor is Ragnarök reduced to an individual self-help metaphor. Personal ordeals participate in a larger historical and cosmic pattern.

The German victory is interpreted as an awakening and renewal, not as a permanent end of history. This lets later theologians explain defeat, suffering, corruption, and future crisis without admitting that the entire faith was disproved by one setback.

## Blood, person, Volk, and free will

### Blood and inherited form

Race unites body, soul, spirit, lineage, disposition, and historical capacity. Biological descent is the necessary carrier of inherited form, but measurements and files do not exhaust the reality they attempt to classify.

The system remains racially hierarchical and administratively unequal. At the same time, fraudulent ancestry accusations, private manipulation of racial records, and arbitrary classification are treated as religious offenses because they corrupt a judgment claimed to be sacred.

### The Volk

The Volk is a living communion of the dead, the living, and the unborn. An individual receives language, body, memory, obligation, and possibility through this chain and owes descendants an inheritance not consumed by the present generation.

The SS claims to be the hereditary guardian order within the German Volk, not a separate sovereign people and not a priesthood entitled to replace the Reich.

### The human person

The person is constituted by:

- biological origin;
- lived embodiment;
- personal form and character;
- free will;
- ancestral-spiritual continuity;
- and acts that become part of the completed life.

Loss of a limb does not make a veteran less human or less spiritually complete. A prosthesis may restore the body's capacity to express personal form. Later cybernetic modification is judged by whether it serves the person's integrated vocation or substitutes an externally controlled mechanism for responsible will.

### Free will and responsibility

Free will is indispensable because will, choice, oath, and responsibility are central to Order morality. Fate supplies conditions, inheritance, opportunity, and constraint; it does not erase agency.

The faith is strongly responsibility-centered. Appeals to divine command, destiny, intoxication, or institutional pressure do not automatically excuse an act. Degrees of knowledge and physical compulsion matter, but the administrative presumption is that a person remains answerable for the form he gives to his conduct.

## Suffering, guilt, honor, and purification

Suffering may arise from finitude, another person's disorder, the consequences of one's own acts, chosen sacrifice, or causes that remain unreadable. Misfortune is not automatic proof of guilt. Honorable defeat is possible when a person acts faithfully under conditions he cannot master.

Honor is not a fixed quantity taken from another person. It can be gained, lost, restored, and deepened through one's own acts. This prevents the religion's honor system from becoming necessarily zero-sum.

The mature process of moral recovery is **purification**, not Christian absolution:

1. truthful declaration of the fault;
2. judgment by the competent household, office, or tribunal;
3. restitution where material injury can be repaired;
4. a proportionate sacrificial act, discipline, service, fast, vigil, or dangerous duty;
5. formal re-entry and restoration of honor.

Some offenses permanently bar particular offices even after personal restoration. Private vengeance does not substitute for judgment.

## Death and the afterlife

The dead retain completed personal identity. The heroic and faithful participate in a great ancestral order commonly imagined as halls, feasts, households, cultivated land, and continuing service. Those who have radically dissolved their form may exist in isolation or arrested cold, symbolized by the glacier in the Vision of the Great Hall.

The religion permits several schools concerning reincarnation. The controlling doctrine is **ancestral recurrence**, not simple personal replacement. A dead ancestor may answer a call through the return of courage, vocation, affinity, burden, capacity, or *hamingja* in a descendant. The ancestor remains himself; the child is a genuinely new person with a new will.

The best-known formula is:

> The dead may answer a call without ceasing to be among the dead, and the living may receive what returns without ceasing to be new.

## Family and household

The household is the basic religious unit. The mature Order Faith is explicitly androcentric.

- Marriage is an asymmetrical and normally permanent bond that places the wife within the husband's lineage household while imposing protective, material, and paternal duties upon him.
- Divorce as simple dissolution is rejected. Invalidity and judicial separation remain possible; widowhood has its own rites and legal protections.
- Regulated concubinage develops as the compromise between official monogamy and historically attested elite polygyny. It does not create equal marriages or release a man from obligations to wife, concubine, or children.
- Biological paternity determines lineage. A child born outside marriage belongs to the biological father's line rather than becoming fictively legitimate through the mother's husband.
- Adoption creates guardianship, duty, and household membership but not a false claim of biological descent.
- Infertility, miscarriage, stillbirth, and congenital disability are sufferings to be borne and ritually acknowledged, not automatic moral faults.
- Permanent celibacy lacks the Christian prestige of a higher vocation and generally requires a service-based justification.
- Adultery, homosexual acts, rape, and prohibited incest are condemned, although the religion's explanations and penalties arise from lineage, authority, fertility, and order rather than Christian sexual theology.

Women do not share the general male command priesthood. Female ritual authority exists in specialized spheres of birth, lineage memory, household cult, mourning, healing, hospitality, and rites directed to women and children.

## Ritual life

### Life-cycle rites

By the early 1950s the faith recognizes eight principal rites:

1. lineage registration and naming;
2. childhood incorporation into the household ancestors;
3. youth threshold and first oath;
4. full Order initiation;
5. marriage;
6. consecration of house, estate, or settlement;
7. funeral;
8. incorporation into ancestral memory.

Household shrines normally include named ancestors, carved figures or symbols, flame or lamp, soil or objects from the estate, and a weapon representing protective authority.

### Blót and sacrifice

Three scales of **blót** develop:

- household offerings and ancestral remembrance;
- estate or kin feasts led by the household lord;
- Order blóts at major sites and solstices.

Food, drink, grain, weapons, jewelry, tools, inherited objects, and animals may be offered. Animal sacrifice followed by communal consumption is lawful and normal at larger rites.

Human sacrifice is accepted **in principle** but monopolized and almost never practiced as a separate public rite. Voluntary self-offering in battle or grave service is the highest ordinary human sacrifice. Private killing as sacrifice is murder. A pure *Mannblót* requires the joint approval of Führer and Reichsführer-SS; some executions of condemned traitors or rebels receive an inner sacrificial interpretation while remaining judicial executions in public records.

### Occult disciplines

Lawful practices include meditation, disciplined dream records, ancestral contemplation, symbolic divination, and licensed study of historically attested Germanic or wider Indo-European techniques. Tibetan and Asian contemplative methods influence elite discipline without turning the faith into Buddhism.

Private operative magic, unauthorized sacrifice, mediumistic movements that create rival authority, and claims of automatic divine command are prohibited or closely controlled.

## Runes

Johannes Rabe does not abolish the SS-associated Armanen rune row. He distinguishes three legitimate uses:

1. **Elder Futhark:** the principal historical and comparative scholarly row.
2. **Younger Futhark:** the historically appropriate Nordic row for Viking-age material.
3. **Armanen or Order Row:** a modern initiatory and institutional symbol system.

Guido von List's Armanen row is therefore judged historically false as an ancient alphabet, theologically possible as a later symbolic construction, and institutionally established within the SS. Historical scripts govern inscriptions presented as ancient; Order symbols govern uniforms, offices, teaching diagrams, and modern rites.

## Membership and authority

Membership has four independent axes:

- descent and genealogical eligibility;
- confession of Order teaching;
- initiation and ritual standing;
- office and jurisdiction.

The wartime categories remain:

- **full Order membership** for genealogically, confessionally, and reproductively admitted members;
- **Germanic association** for selected related foreign peoples and formations;
- **service affiliation** for soldiers, police, technicians, and auxiliaries who serve without entering the hereditary religious order.

The Führer is the political bearer of German historical authority. The Reichsführer-SS is guardian of the SS confession, rites, and hereditary estate. Neither office is simply priest of the other.

Wolfgang's Lutheran household is accepted through a distinction between political vocation and complete initiatory knowledge. Rabe argues that Providence may employ a ruler who does not possess the Order's inner teaching. This preserves loyalty without pretending Wolfgang is secretly pagan.

## Founders and custodians

### Adolf Hitler

Hitler is the **Awakener**, not a god or incarnation. He forces Himmler to settle a coherent confession, limits rival cultic authority, and establishes the political frame within which the Order Faith exists.

### Heinrich Himmler

Himmler is founder, first doctrinal arbiter, and authorizer of the Wewelsburg confession. He defines the core claims about Order, gods, blood, Volk, struggle, Providence, leadership, and the SS before his death on **23 May 1976**.

### Friedrich Hagedorn

Hagedorn succeeds as Reichsführer-SS on **28 May 1976**. He does not invent a new creed. He audits, standardizes, trains, inspects, and gives the religion a durable bureaucracy. Under him the Main Office for Order Faith and Life Order receives its final form.

### Professor Dr. Johannes Eberhard Rabe

Rabe, born in Lüneburg in 1921, is the mature faith's principal systematic theologian. Initially trained in Protestant theology, he moves into classics, Old Norse, Sanskrit, Indo-European studies, comparative religion, and philosophy.

He enters SS education during the war, serves as a translator, funerary administrator, and Wewelsburg editor, and develops a concordance method that ranks sources by authority, preserves established teaching through careful distinctions, tests conclusions against the whole system, ignores unsupported Christian pressure, and states the ritual or legal consequence of every determination.

By 1980 he is an SS-Oberführer, director of the Office for Doctrine and Interpretation, and **Lehrwalter des Ordensglaubens**—Custodian of Order Doctrine. His major work is:

> *Die Ordnung des Lebendigen: Lehrsumme des Ordensglaubens*  
> *The Order of the Living: A Doctrinal Synthesis of the Order Faith*

The seven-book *Lehrsumme* treats divine order; gods and differentiated revelation; blood and Volk; person, death, and recurrence; duty and purification; rite and occult discipline; and the relationship among Führer, Order, household, foreign peoples, and rival religions. It continues into the early or middle 1980s without reopening Himmler's founding confession.

## The two corroborated visions

### The Hall of Names

Rabe's formative vision occurs during the war. He enters a vast library whose central Book of Names receives new names **as people are born**; it does not contain every future person in advance. Completed lives occupy the great hall, while books of the living are still written in an adjoining scriptorium.

- ordinary deeds appear in black;
- heroic deeds in gold;
- sacrifice in blood;
- acts of dissolution or self-erasure are associated with a clear, washing medium that leaves no durable writing.

Rabe finds his father's completed book and its final page written in blood. He then learns that his father died during an unexpected British raid while shielding family members. The timing and manner of death give Rabe reason to classify the dream as a corroborated vision without making him a prophet.

Gold filigree on some black-bound books signifies inherited vocation or preparation, not predestination. Red-bound books belong to lives completed in sacrificial self-offering. A name, binding, and inherited circumstance may be given; the living pages remain open to free will.

### The Great Hall and Green Height

The second visionary is **SS-Oberscharführer Ernst Keller**, a reliable records and instructional clerk, not Rabe. After hearing Himmler speak about Barbarossa and heroic recurrence, Keller dreams of a great hall of the dead, a green ancestral settlement in which persons may appear at several ages, a glacier of arrested isolation, and a wounded Bavarian soldier who answers a call and disappears.

Rabe seals Keller's account before investigation. Records identify the soldier as **Josef Hintermayr** of the Royal Bavarian 17th Reserve Infantry Regiment, killed in 1918, matching the described facial scar and missing fingers. On the same night, a direct descendant gives birth to a son named Josef within minutes of Keller's awakening. Forgotten exposure to a memorial image and pregnancy record remains a possible natural explanation.

The case produces a fourfold classification:

1. *Traum*—ordinary dream;
2. *Schau*—significant symbolic vision;
3. *bestätigte Schau*—corroborated vision containing independently confirmed information;
4. *Offenbarung*—binding revelation, recognized only with extreme reluctance.

Rabe's controlling rules are:

> Confirmation establishes that an experience deserves interpretation. It does not establish that the visionary's interpretation is correct.

> A man who once saw truly does not thereby become incapable of dreaming falsely.

## State of the faith in 1985

By 1985 the Order Faith is institutionally mature, internally diverse, and still publicly deniable.

- Himmler's confession is fixed.
- Hagedorn controls offices, training, inspection, and succession.
- Rabe's theology supplies internal consistency.
- Household and estate rites reproduce the faith across generations.
- Romantic mystics, racial biologists, administrative pragmatists, and Rabe's systematic school remain factions inside one confession.
- Wolfgang tolerates and constrains the SS religion without adopting it.
- Alexander is attracted to memorial, genealogical, oath-bound Order culture but is not initiated or surrendered to SS schooling.
- Christian native populations remain outside the ruling confession.
- The Vatican and foreign intelligence services possess fragments, reports, and ritual descriptions, but no public documentary proof of the complete church-like structure.

The central contradiction remains unresolved:

> A religion that claims living Order as its highest good is administered by an elite whose colonial hierarchy repeatedly produces secrecy, domination, and disorder.
