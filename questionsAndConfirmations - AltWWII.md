# AltWWII Questions and Confirmations

## How to use this file

Each item has a stable identifier. To resolve one, replace **Authorial answer: _Pending_** with the decision. The Setting Bible and Timeline can then be updated without reopening the entire transcript.

Priority:

- **P0:** contradiction capable of producing incompatible canon.
- **P1:** important missing decision with downstream effects.
- **P2:** flavor or precision that can remain open safely.

### P0-08 — German demographic policy after 1979

**Conflict:** Foreign reporting describes severe fertility collapse as the result of systematic German coercion. German internal policy instead describes voluntary contraceptive provision and small-family incentives, with a separate punitive-sterilization rule for a narrow criminal category.

**Current working treatment:** Superseded by the authorial answer below.

**Recommendation:** Decide whether the reforms materially raise subject fertility, merely reduce the most visible abuse, or shift coercion into benefits/employment rules.

**Status:** Confirmed.

**Authorial answer:** Contraceptive pills are illegal for Germans to use but are supplied freely and formally voluntarily to native subject populations. Official explanation presents them as permitting intimacy without the risk of pregnancy, deliberately framing pregnancy as undesirable. Mittelafrika also offers incentives for smaller families. Foreign critics describe the combination as coercion, but authorially the contraception itself is not forced. Sterilization is available as punishment for violent, repeat native criminals, not as a general demographic treatment; Wolfgang sharply restricts that punishment and makes the contraceptive arrangement explicitly voluntary. The dispute between formal voluntariness, manipulative framing, unequal law, and the surrounding power structure is intentional.

### P0-09 — American operational spaceplane architecture

**Status:** Authorially resolved; specialist-reference implementation pending.

**Authorial answer:** Operational American orbital vehicles take off horizontally from very long runways under their own integrated multi-cycle propulsion, transition from air-breathing flight to onboard closed-cycle rockets, enter orbit as one aircraft, and return horizontally to a runway. The working lineage is Pathfinder, Columbia/Columbia Tanker, Liberty/Liberty Automatic, Hercules, Ranger, Enterprise, and Zenith. The dedicated Aerospace Armaments Reference must be structurally reset before its masses, payloads, propulsion details, and production totals can be treated as controlling.
